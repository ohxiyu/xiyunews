    Strive's Preferred Stock SATS Lists on Nasdaq, Raising $630 Million in IPO With a 15% Annual Fixed Dividend

    Strive, a Nasdaq-listed Bitcoin treasury company chaired by Matt Cole, closed an upsized IPO of its SATS preferred stock on Oct. 20, selling 7 million shares at $90 each for $630 million in gross proceeds and listing the stock on Nasdaq. The preferred carries a 15% annual fixed dividend on its $100 stated amount, is cumulative, and is scheduled to be paid each business day starting Nov. 9, subject to board declaration. The deal gives public-market investors a daily-paid, 15%-yielding instrument issued by a Bitcoin treasury company, and Strive has said proceeds may fund additional Bitcoin purchases alongside working capital and income-generating assets. However, the company has not specified how much of the proceeds will actually go to Bitcoin. Strive estimated net proceeds of about $608.15 million after underwriting discounts, commissions and expenses; at an illustrative Bitcoin price of $82,500, that amount could buy roughly 7,372 BTC, which would lift reported holdings from 36,818 BTC to about 44,190 BTC and potentially exceed Metaplanet's reported 44,000 BTC. At the $90 IPO price, the $15 annual stated dividend equals about 16.67% of the purchase price, and chief risk officer Jeff Walton said the company carries no debt or maturities and is focused on a single obligation: paying dividends.

    Evernorth Partners With Ripple, Kraken, Coinbase and BitGo to Deploy $648 Million XRP Reserve

    Nasdaq-listed Evernorth has selected Ripple, Kraken, Coinbase and BitGo as its first institutional partners to deploy roughly 473 million XRP, worth about $648 million at current prices, through institutional strategies starting in the fourth quarter. The company said it will later expand into lending on the XRP Ledger and on-chain DeFi strategies, with the deployment led by former Coinbase Asset Management co-CIO Hugo Jeandesboz. A publicly listed treasury company is naming regulated exchanges and custodians to actively put a nine-figure XRP reserve to work rather than simply holding it, which ties a large XRP position into established institutional custody and trading venues. The plan is a stated intention to deploy from the fourth quarter onward, not a completed deployment. The 473 million XRP figure is valued at approximately $648 million at current prices, and the XRP Ledger lending and on-chain DeFi expansion is described as a future step.

  • 2026-10-14

    XRPN took shareholder vote with no knowledge of XRP inflation bug

    A newly disclosed bug in the XRP Ledger's base-layer payment engine could have allowed an attacker to mint trillions of new XRP for almost nothing; researcher Cayden Liao and Veria AI reported it on September 22, and Ripple shipped a closed-source fix in xrpld 3.4.1 on September 25 described only as "integer-arithmetic hardening." Shareholders of the Ripple/Arrington-backed XRP treasury vehicle Evernorth (XRPN) voted on their plan without being told about the flaw, and an official report admitting what the patch actually fixed appeared only on October 9, the day the merger closed. The flaw meant XRP's fixed supply of 100 billion — the core claim underpinning Evernorth's treasury plan — was technically unenforceable on the network for roughly a decade. The episode also raises governance questions, since XRPN shareholders made redemption and merger decisions inside a window in which Ripple knew about the bug but they did not. According to the report, the bug likely dated back to 2015 and a single validated transaction using the exploit "could have created spendable XRP far beyond the total supply." The patch shipped without an amendment vote, and no actual exploitation of the vulnerability has been reported.

    Ripple-Backed Evernorth Hits Nasdaq Holding 473 Million XRP Worth Far Less Than It Paid

    Evernorth, the Ripple-backed XRP treasury company, began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker XRPN on Monday after closing its merger with SPAC Armada Acquisition Corp. II on October 9, holding roughly 473 million XRP and about $300 million in gross cash. At the $1.43 XRP reference price used in the closing filing, that treasury is worth about $677 million — well below the more than $1 billion the company said it had raised to buy XRP, when the deal was signed at a $2.37 token price. The listing gives ordinary brokerage-account investors direct equity exposure to an XRP treasury without opening a crypto wallet, and it arrives sharply marked down: about 80.3% of Armada's public shareholders redeemed for roughly $10.58 per share, pulling about $195 million out of the deal and leaving a thin float that sent XRPN as high as $53 before the close. The redemptions covered 18.5 million shares and left only about 4.5 million original public shares outstanding; XRPN touched an intraday high of $53 on October 2 and closed that Friday at $39.42 before sliding to $17.09 on October 9, and on its first day as Evernorth the stock briefly rose toward $19 before fading to $16.64 by midday Monday. SBI Holdings controls 39.9% of Evernorth's voting shares, Arrington Capital's fund holds 19.9% and Ripple co-founder Chris Larsen is tied to 9.9%, with Ripple and its affiliates capped at 9.9% voting power and six-month lock-ups barring Ripple, a Larsen-linked trust and Arrington's fund from selling until April 2027, according to the filing.

    XRP treasury firm Evernorth completes SPAC merger, to list on Nasdaq on October 12, holding 473 million XRP

    Evernorth Holdings announced on October 10 that it has completed its business combination with special purpose acquisition company Armada Acquisition Corp. II, and its common stock is expected to begin trading on Nasdaq under the ticker XRPN on October 12. At closing, Evernorth holds roughly 473 million XRP and has raised about $300 million in gross cash proceeds, before transaction fees. The deal creates a Nasdaq-listed, actively managed XRP treasury vehicle backed by Ripple, SBI Group, Pantera Capital, Kraken and GSR, giving public-market investors a listed equity route to XRP exposure that differs from an ETF structure. Evernorth is backed by Arrington XRP Capital and other investors, with SBI Group, Ripple, Pantera Capital, Kraken and GSR also among its investors. The company positions itself as an actively managed XRP treasury and plans to deploy capital into the XRP ecosystem — institutional-grade onchain finance, tokenized assets, onchain credit and settlement infrastructure — aiming to raise XRP held per share over the long term through institutional and DeFi yield strategies, ecosystem building and capital markets activity.
