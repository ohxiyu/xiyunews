    Strategy raises $5.4B but barely buys bitcoin

    Strategy raised $5.4 billion through at-the-market common stock sales between July and September but spent only $588 million of that on bitcoin — less than 11 cents of every dollar raised. The company also sold 5,552 of its own BTC for $348.6 million, partly to fund dividends and partly to repurchase its STRC preferred stock. The disclosure breaks a pattern: in 2024 essentially every ATM dollar became bitcoin, and even in the anomalous third quarter of 2025 more than 94 cents of each dollar still bought BTC. The shift matters because Strategy is the largest corporate bitcoin treasury, so the share of its fundraising that reaches spot BTC purchases is a directly observable demand channel. Strategy bought 7,218 BTC during the quarter and, after accounting for its sales, ended with just 1,666 more coins than it started with. In the reporting week of October 26, $164 million of fresh MSTR dilution saw only 40 cents of each dollar go toward BTC.

    Strategy reports $19.5 billion in Q3 net income as preferred shareholders approve daily dividends

    Strategy reported $19.5 billion in third-quarter net income and won shareholder approval to move its preferred stocks to daily dividend payments. Per Cointelegraph and Bankless, the change covers four preferred securities — STRC, STRF, STRK and STRD — with daily payouts on STRC starting in November and the other three following in January 2027. Strategy is the largest corporate holder of Bitcoin, so its capital-structure decisions are closely watched by investors using its securities as a proxy for BTC exposure. Shifting preferred dividends from periodic to daily payments changes the cash-flow profile of those instruments and the company's ongoing payment obligations to preferred holders. The approval was confirmed on Wednesday, Oct. 28, covering a change the company first floated in September. Daily dividends begin on STRC in November, with STRF, STRK and STRD converted in January 2027.
