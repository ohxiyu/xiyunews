    Introducing Binance AI Pro

    Binance announced the global rollout of Binance AI Pro, a finance-focused AI agent that converts trading ideas described in conversation into tested, automated strategies whose parameters are set by the user. The rollout covers the Binance website and app for all users globally starting 2026-10-19. Binance says the product lets users build automated strategies without coding, lowering the technical barrier to strategy automation for retail users on one of the largest exchanges. Strategies are first tested through simulated trading and deployed to live trading only after user approval; each live strategy runs in a separate sub-account, and funds can only be moved manually by the user. A free Standard tier offers a 250,000 monthly quota with access to the DeepSeek model, while a Premium tier at 19.99 USDC per month offers a 10 million monthly quota and access to models including ChatGPT Sol, ChatGPT Astra and Claude Opus, depending on region; the earlier Beta version continues to run and users can migrate chat history and strategies to the new version.

    Binance launches AI trading tool after 150,000-user beta

    Binance rolled out Binance AI, a free generative-UI trading interface, to all English-language users, following a beta that involved roughly 150,000 testers. The tool was previously limited to the beta group. Binance is the largest exchange by trading volume, so putting an AI-driven interface in front of its full English-language retail base extends AI tooling from a beta experiment into a mainstream trading workflow. Binance AI is a free suite that consolidates market signals from charts, news, social platforms, onchain data and research tools into a single interface, and adapts to each user's knowledge level through generative UI. The rollout was limited to English-language users at launch.
