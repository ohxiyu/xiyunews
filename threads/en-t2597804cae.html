    OKX UAE Launches Tokenised US Stocks with 24/7 Trading on VARA Licence

    OKX's UAE entity has launched tokenised US stock trading that is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, operating under its licence from Dubai's Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (VARA). The offering extends regulated, crypto-style market access to US equity exposure. A VARA-licensed venue offering round-the-clock tokenised equity exposure widens access to tokenised securities in one of the crypto industry's more developed regulatory jurisdictions, putting equity exposure on the same always-on footing as crypto trading. Tokenised stocks are blockchain-based tokens designed to reflect the value of a specific equity. Separate reporting on OKX's broader tokenised-equity plans describes trading against stablecoins including USDC, USDG and USDT through permissioned Uniswap v4 liquidity pools on XLayer, OKX's layer-2 blockchain.

    Morpho Supports Borrowing Stablecoins Against Robinhood Stock Tokens as Collateral

    Morpho announced on October 22 that Robinhood stock tokens are now live on its platform, allowing users to post tokenized equities as collateral and borrow stablecoins against them. Morpho supplies the underlying credit network while Robinhood Chain provides the tokenization infrastructure, and any application can integrate the resulting lending markets. The integration extends on-chain credit markets to tokenized equity collateral, a step beyond the crypto-native assets that typically back DeFi loans. Morpho positions itself as the credit layer rather than the tokenization layer, leaving issuance and settlement of the stock tokens to Robinhood Chain. The announcement did not specify which equities are supported, collateral ratios, or which applications have integrated the markets.

    Tokenized stock trading takes off overseas as Goldman, other U.S. companies object

    Tokenized stock trading is gaining volume in overseas jurisdictions, according to CNBC, while Goldman Sachs and other U.S. companies are objecting to the practice. The standoff sets up a fight over market structure and regulation for tokenized equities. The objection from a major U.S. issuer raises the question of whether offshore venues can keep expanding tokenized share trading without the consent of the companies whose stock is being tokenized. Under U.S. law, a tokenized instrument that meets the definition of an equity security remains subject to the federal securities laws, SEC regulations and FINRA requirements, according to a recommendation from the SEC's Market Structure Subcommittee. The CNBC report describes an evolving trend rather than a single ruling or enforcement action.

    Robinhood Reports $15B in Stock Token Trading as Chain Passes 100 Days

    Robinhood says its stock tokens have crossed $15 billion in cumulative trading volume and reached 200,000 holders, as its chain passes the 100-day mark. Morpho's dashboard shows about $1.1 billion deposited on the network. The figures are among the largest quantified milestones disclosed so far for tokenized equities, and the Morpho deposit number indicates the Robinhood chain is already hosting third-party DeFi lending activity. Robinhood's stock tokens are tokenized debt securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited that provide economic exposure to the underlying securities without granting legal or beneficial rights in the issuers, and more than 190 are available, including those linked to NVIDIA, Google, Apple and Invesco QQQ. The roughly $1.1 billion figure comes from Morpho's own dashboard rather than from Robinhood.

    Tokenized Stocks Show Real Demand But Remain Volatile and Illiquid, IMF Finds

    The IMF's latest Global Financial Stability Report found that more than 50% of tokenized stock trading occurs outside regular U.S. market hours and that about 80% of trades are for less than a full share, while tokenized equities were roughly 1.5 times more volatile and less liquid than the underlying stocks. The report, covering the five most liquid tokenized U.S. equities across 11 trading venues over 365 days, says systemic risks remain limited for now and urges regulators to consider circuit breakers for 24/7 trading. The IMF said it urged regulators to consider circuit breakers for 24/7 trading and to monitor links between tokenized and traditional markets more closely, while noting that systemic risks remain limited for now. It also said its findings "should be interpreted with caution" given the market's early stage. The tokenized equity market is worth about $2.3 billion and highly concentrated, with Ondo Finance and Backed Finance's xStocks accounting for more than 70% of it; both issue synthetic tokens offering price exposure without direct share ownership. When traditional markets reopened, conventional shares absorbed 87% to 99% of the overnight price moves their tokenized counterparts had made, and tokenized prices on decentralized exchanges strayed further from traditional markets.
