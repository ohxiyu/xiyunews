    Brazil's Biggest Bitcoin Exchange Says It Will Refund Users After a $40 Million Hack

    Mercado Bitcoin, Brazil's largest crypto exchange, said it detected a security incident in one of its systems early on November 2 and froze deposits and withdrawals as a precaution, pledging to absorb the losses itself rather than pass them to customers. The exchange put the value of assets involved at about $38 million and said it had already blocked $11 million with help from Tether, Circle, Paxos, BRL1 and Chainalysis, while outside trackers estimate higher losses. A guarantee to cover losses is only as good as the money behind it, and exchanges are not required to show customers their books, so the pledge cannot be verified from outside. Deposits and withdrawals remain suspended with no restart date, leaving users unable to move funds. Loss estimates vary: Hypernative flagged more than $40 million in suspicious transfers across at least 10 blockchains including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and Tron; Arkham traced at least $46 million and Chainalysis about $49 million, with nearly $10 million frozen. Mercado Bitcoin said no customer lost money and no data leaked, did not say whether its $38 million figure includes the frozen amount, and has not said how attackers got in; Tether froze more than $4.5 million in USDT tied to the incident, according to CEO Paolo Ardoino.

    Cybersecurity Stocks In Focus After Binance Hack Raises Digital Asset Security Questions

    Simply Wall St published a market commentary item flagging cybersecurity stocks as a focus of attention after a hack at Binance renewed concerns about digital-asset security and exchange safeguards. The piece is secondary stock-market commentary rather than primary incident reporting, and it does not establish the size of the loss or the number of users affected. The item links an exchange security failure to investor attention on listed cybersecurity vendors, framing crypto-incident risk as a potential demand driver for that sector. Other reporting surfaced in search results describes a 2026 Binance breach as the largest-ever cryptocurrency heist, surpassing the $1.5 billion Bybit hack in 2025, with investors withdrawing $3.6 billion in funds; those figures come from separate outlets and are not part of the Simply Wall St commentary.

    Ripple freezes about $200 million in RLUSD as hackers shift to DEXes

    According to Decrypt, Binance co-CEO Yi He said Ripple has frozen approximately $200 million of RLUSD. Suspected attackers are using Uniswap and Aave to swap freezable stablecoins into ETH, and Kamino has paused borrowing in its SOL/BTC markets. The freeze shows an issuer using its blacklist capability at scale against suspected exploit proceeds, while the attackers' move into ETH via DEXes and lending markets illustrates how freezable stablecoins are being converted into assets issuers cannot freeze. Kamino's halt shows the response extending into Solana lending markets. The figure comes from Binance co-CEO Yi He as cited by Decrypt, and full loss attribution remains emerging. Kamino's pause applies to borrowing in its SOL/BTC markets.

    Binance investigating hack as suspicious outflows reach up to $5.8 billion

    Binance said it is investigating a security incident after wallets linked to the exchange were drained of at least $5.5 billion on Thursday, with on-chain tallies cited by The Block putting suspicious outflows at up to $5.8 billion across Ethereum, Bitcoin and Tron. Binance has not confirmed that user funds were affected, though security researchers widely describe the incident as a hack that would be the largest in the industry's history. The affected venue is the largest centralized exchange by trading volume, so unresolved questions about the scale of losses and whether customer balances are impaired bear directly on how traders price counterparty and custody risk across the market. Bloomberg reported that the suspected hack triggered emergency asset freezes and sent digital asset prices tumbling. Tether blacklisted 975.1 million USDT and Circle blacklisted 823 million USDC in the main receiving wallet, and Binance co-CEO Yi He said Ripple also froze about $200 million in RLUSD. The attacker routed funds through Uniswap contracts and borrowed ETH on Aave against USDT collateral — roughly 253,000 ETH (about $570 million) sat in two wallets with no outgoing transfers as of 22:07 UTC — while Aave froze its V3 markets on Ethereum mainnet and Solana-based Kamino paused borrow markets as a precaution.
