    Nine U.S. Issuers Block Their Shares From Trading on OKXICE Token Platform

    Nine U.S. issuers have moved to block their shares from trading on OKXICE's token platform, restricting tokenized equity trading on the venue. The action targets multiple tokenized equity instruments tied to U.S.-listed companies that the platform intended to offer. The move signals legal and operational risk for tokenized securities venues, as issuer consent can determine whether tokenized shares of a given company remain tradable on a platform. OKXICE is the OKX–ICE tokenized stock venture that filed with the SEC to offer tokenized shares in more than 60 U.S.-listed companies, with one report citing 63 NYSE-listed names, under the agency's Innovation Exemption.
