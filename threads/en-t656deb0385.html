    THORChain Halts Cross-Chain Swaps Over Security Flaw, Disrupting Laundering of Binance Hack Funds

    THORChain suspended cross-chain swaps after a security flaw was identified in the protocol. According to the report, the halt also cut off a route that had reportedly been used to move funds stolen in the Binance hack. The halt takes a widely used decentralized cross-chain swap venue offline for users, and per the report interrupts a laundering path for stolen Binance funds. The measure is described as a precautionary halt tied to an identified flaw rather than a confirmed exploit, and no user losses have been quantified in the report.

    THORChain Swaps Remain Paused on 12 Chains After Reported Refund Error

    THORChain's cross-chain swap functionality remains suspended across 12 supported chains following a reported error in its refund mechanism. According to Chainalysis, an Ethereum chain reorganization on the morning of October 31 (EDT) caused THORChain to mistakenly issue a double refund to a user who had deposited 10 ETH, prompting an emergency pause. The pause blocks cross-chain swaps for all users across most of THORChain's bridges. Chainalysis said the halt also inadvertently delayed the laundering of a significant amount of funds stolen from Binance. Chainalysis attributed the trigger specifically to an Ethereum chain reorganization, a event in which previously accepted blocks are replaced by a competing chain. No loss figures for the incident have been confirmed in the supplied material.
