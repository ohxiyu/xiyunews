    Bitcoin Lightning developers sound alarm over security flaws as second patch lands in five days

    Bitcoin Lightning developers are warning publicly about security flaws after a second patch was released within five days, CryptoSlate reported. No confirmed large-scale exploit or systemic loss event has been reported in the available material. The warning puts Lightning node operators on notice to apply the latest fixes, since security defects in payment-channel software are an operational risk for systems that hold and route funds. The second patch arrived within a five-day window, underscoring that fixes are being rolled out in quick succession. The available evidence does not establish that the flaws were exploited at scale.
