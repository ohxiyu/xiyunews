Entity background

Hyperliquid is a Layer 1 blockchain optimized for onchain financial markets. HyperCore provides fully onchain perpetual and spot order books, while HyperEVM adds a general-purpose EVM environment, making protocol upgrades, validators, market structure, and listings relevant to its ecosystem.

    Intercontinental Exchange (ICE) and trade[XYZ] have announced their plan to use

    Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), the operator of the New York Stock Exchange, and trade[XYZ] announced on Thursday a plan to use Hyperliquid as the infrastructure layer for perpetual futures markets serving US traders across crypto, equities, commodities, foreign exchange and other real-world assets, subject to regulatory approvals. Under the structure, Hyperliquid supplies the underlying technology via HIP-3, trade[XYZ] designs and implements the markets, and ICE operates the regulated market and clearing structure. The arrangement would give US retail traders a regulated route to Hyperliquid-based perpetual futures, while handing ICE a new entry point into retail trading; ICE said the vehicle would run separately from its institutional futures business. OKX CEO Star said the project is being advanced jointly by ICE, trade[XYZ] and OKX, with retail distribution to flow through an ICE-OKX joint venture. ICE said it has signed a definitive agreement with Trade.xyz and will be the exclusive US operator, with a dedicated clearing structure intended to shield its existing customers — more than 99% of its market participation is institutional — from retail counterparty risk. Existing trade[XYZ] HIP-3 markets will remain unchanged, and HIP-3 deployers remain independent operators responsible for their own deployments.

    A new centralized laundering service FreedomXMR (FXMR) appeared on Hyperliquid C

    On-chain investigator ZachXBT reported that FreedomXMR (FXMR), a centralized money-laundering service that appeared on Hyperliquid Core in August 2026, has more than 87% of its inflow volume tied directly to illicit activity. A manual review of all historical FXMR inflows above $10,000 traced over $20 million to ransomware, exploit, malware and social-engineering incidents, and the supporting spreadsheet was shared with the Hyperliquid team. The disclosure names Hyperliquid Core as a channel repeatedly used by laundering services, and ZachXBT said the chain has previously hosted operations such as b1exch that exploit its permissionless design. The evidence has been handed to the Hyperliquid team, but no confirmation of action by the team was reported. The traced incidents include the DCENT Wallet, Fetch.ai and Axelar exploits and an unnamed ransomware payment in September 2026, plus the Gamdom exploit in October 2026. ZachXBT said the FXMR admin sources XMR liquidity from KuCoin with more than 3,000 deposits to the exchange, likely using a swap model similar to Wagyu's; the FXMR ticker was bought in a Hyperliquid auction on July 27, 2026 for 518.28 HYPE (about $28,000).

    Hyperliquid launches native lending on HyperCore as HYPE tops $90

    On Sept. 18, Hyperliquid rolled out native manual borrowing, allowing users to pledge HYPE or Bitcoin as collateral to borrow USDC or USDT directly through HyperCore. Roughly $269 million was borrowed on the first day, while HYPE rallied about 15% on the week to an intraday high near $91.06, surpassing its prior record of about $89.60. The launch extends Hyperliquid from a derivatives and spot exchange into credit, collateral and yield generation, letting users move between trading and borrowing without leaving the platform. Immediate day-one scale of $269 million suggests the lending primitive can become a meaningful source of stablecoin liquidity and HYPE demand within a single ecosystem rather than a standalone side product. HYPE carries a 65% loan-to-value ratio versus 50% for Bitcoin, with liquidation thresholds of 82.5% and 75% respectively. Borrowers can tap more than $400 million of already-supplied liquidity because the same pools support portfolio-margin activity; stablecoin suppliers earn variable interest based on utilization, and portfolio-margin users can earn yield on idle stablecoin balances.
