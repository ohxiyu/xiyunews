    Circle freezes $1.1B in USDC after Binance hack, blocking funds at specific addresses.

    Following a major hack on the Binance exchange in October 2026, Circle has frozen $1.1 billion worth of its USDC stablecoin in specific blockchain addresses linked to the incident. This action prevents the immediate movement of a significant portion of the stolen funds, potentially aiding in their recovery and demonstrating the centralized control stablecoin issuers can exert in response to security breaches. The Binance hack, which occurred on 29 October 2026, resulted in a loss of approximately $6.3 billion. Circle's freeze is part of a broader response that has reportedly seen around $3 billion in stolen assets frozen across various entities.

    Introducing Binance AI Pro

    Binance announced the global rollout of Binance AI Pro, a finance-focused AI agent that converts trading ideas described in conversation into tested, automated strategies whose parameters are set by the user. The rollout covers the Binance website and app for all users globally starting 2026-10-19. Binance says the product lets users build automated strategies without coding, lowering the technical barrier to strategy automation for retail users on one of the largest exchanges. Strategies are first tested through simulated trading and deployed to live trading only after user approval; each live strategy runs in a separate sub-account, and funds can only be moved manually by the user. A free Standard tier offers a 250,000 monthly quota with access to the DeepSeek model, while a Premium tier at 19.99 USDC per month offers a 10 million monthly quota and access to models including ChatGPT Sol, ChatGPT Astra and Claude Opus, depending on region; the earlier Beta version continues to run and users can migrate chat history and strategies to the new version.

    Ledger theft hacker moves $1.07M in ETH to Tornado Cash, with some funds reaching a Binance hot wallet

    On October 10, Onchain Lens reported that suspects linked to the theft tied to Ledger distributor CryptoBillis — an incident said to involve close to $90 million — sent 430.2 ETH, worth about $1.07 million, into Tornado Cash through four wallets. After Tether froze some related funds, the suspects reportedly began swapping USDT into USDD via SUN.io and the USDD PSM, with some funds flowing into a Binance hot wallet. The swaps suggest the suspects are trying to route around Tether's freeze of related funds, and the traced wallets reportedly still hold about $70.6 million, leaving the case actively in motion. The flagged suspect wallets reportedly still hold 11,406 ETH (~$28.35M), 213.371 BTC (~$17.65M), 13.646M USDD (~$13.65M) and 10.909M USDT (~$10.91M). The report notes that because on-chain funds are still moving, the tracing data and the suspects' attribution require further verification.

    Binance Ends BounceBit Mainnet Support After Chain Sunset

    Binance announced it will discontinue mainnet support for BounceBit (BB) after the project decided to permanently sunset BounceBit Chain following a hacking incident. Deposits and withdrawals of BB will be moved to the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network. This is significant because it marks the permanent shutdown of a Layer 1 blockchain after a security breach, forcing BB holders to migrate to BNB Smart Chain. It also highlights how exchange support changes can directly affect a token's viability and network choice for a mid-cap asset. The exploit reportedly resulted in a loss of about $3 million, leading BounceBit to permanently sunset its chain. On Binance, BB deposits and withdrawals will now use the BEP20 token standard on BNB Smart Chain, so users must select the new network to avoid asset loss.
