- **Production rails bonus:** If a story describes a live or named-institution deployment of tokenized settlement, collateral, or stablecoin FX, start it at 8.0 and add +0.5 for each additional regulated participant or production transaction. Apply to “英国最大型银行完成全球首笔使用代币化存款的银行间交易”, “以固定利率抵押比特币借款：Coinbase 扩展 Morpho 贷款业务”, “Circle 推出 7×24 小时稳定币外汇引擎，意在切分 10 万亿美元外汇市场”, and “Aave 在 Base 上新增 Coinbase 股票代币作为抵押品”.
- **Single-source headline cap:** If the only source is a single aggregator headline and it lacks leverage limits, venue eligibility, or effective dates, cap at 7.9 regardless of topic. Apply to “CFTC 批准比特币永续期货进入美国市场 - CoinMarketCap”.
- **Same-event duplicate penalty:** When two stories on the same day cover the same underlying event with no independently sourced new consequence, score the event once and cap the second at 7.5. Apply to “ZetaChain 投票决定逐步关停 Layer 1，并将 ZETA 迁移至 Solana” / “这条区块链刚投票决定关停自身，转向Solana驱动的AI”; “一个钱包将155万美元FetchAI盗窃与4.085亿NTX巨额铸造联系起来” / “Fetch.ai、NuNet 和 SingularityNET 遭三重攻击，200 万美元被盗”; and “恶意 iPhone 应用突破 iOS 沙盒，劫取 58 万美元 USDT” / “窃取加密资产的 FOMO 应用在苹果商店上架一周”.
- **Follow-up-potential trigger for security and governance:** For security incidents, require at least one of funds frozen, attribution, protocol pause, or regulatory action to score >=8.5; for governance votes, require live operational change or confirmed timeline, not first vote/proposal.
