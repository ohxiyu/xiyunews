Current outcome

Mizuho, MUFG, SMBC, MUFG Trust and TISI announced they completed an FSA-backed pilot for jointly issuing a yen stablecoin under Japan's Funds Settlement Act and using it for cross-border settlement, validating the full issuance, transfer and redemption flow ahead of planned FY2026 live transactions.
