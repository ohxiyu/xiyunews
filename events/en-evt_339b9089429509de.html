Current outcome

Vitalik Buterin warned that AI-accelerated mathematics could weaken lattice-based post-quantum cryptography such as ML-DSA and FHE — and eventually ECDSA — within about two years, prompting Ethereum's Lean roadmap to favor pure hash-based signatures, while advising users not to hastily move assets but to prefer unused addresses and offline multisig signing.
