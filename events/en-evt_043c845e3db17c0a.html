  1. #0110.12
    Initial2026-10-12 09:30 · publication time

    Standard Chartered adds Ripple USD custody, eyes LMAX collateral deal

    Standard Chartered has rolled out institutional custody for Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin on the XRP Ledger and plans to extend its collateral-mirroring model to LMAX Group so institutions can post RLUSD as trading collateral while assets remain in the bank's custody.

    Source evidence: Cointelegraph · The Block · CoinDesk · theblockbeats · The Defiant · foresightnews

  2. #0210.12
    Confirmation2026-10-12 09:36 · publication time

    【渣打银行上线 RLUSD 托管服务，拟与 Ripple、LMAX 合作将 RLUSD 用作抵押品】

    Confirmed the custody service is offered through Standard Chartered's DFSA-regulated DIFC branch, and that making RLUSD eligible collateral for LMAX products still requires final documentation, testing, internal approvals, and regulatory preparations.

    State after update: Standard Chartered has launched institutional custody for Ripple's RLUSD on the XRP Ledger via its DFSA-regulated DIFC branch and is working with Ripple and LMAX Group to make RLUSD eligible collateral for selected LMAX products using a collateral-mirroring model, subject to final documentation, testing, internal approvals, and regulatory preparations.

    Source evidence: foresightnews · CryptoSlate · Decrypt

  3. #0310.13
    Confirmation2026-10-13 16:02 · publication time

    Ripple与Stripe旗下Privy合作，将为XRP Ledger引入嵌入式钱包功能

    Ripple partnered with Stripe-owned Privy to add XRP Ledger support to Privy's developer platform, enabling embedded XRPL wallets with wallet creation, management and transaction signing for XRP, RLUSD and other XRPL assets.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats

  4. #0410.14
    Confirmation2026-10-14 13:10 · publication time

    【DriveWealth 与 Ripple 达成合作，将机构数字资产基础设施引入全球投资】

    DriveWealth partners with Ripple to integrate stablecoin, payments, custody, and prime brokerage infrastructure into its global investment platform, starting in Europe and expanding to the US.

    Source evidence: foresightnews

  5. #0510.15

    Ripple's prime brokerage arm Ripple Prime rolled out institutional RWA perpetual contracts spanning 100+ stock, index, ETF and commodity markets through Hyperliquid-based Trade.xyz, offering shared cross-position margin and weekend/after-hours exposure.
