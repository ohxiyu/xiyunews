Current outcome

Brazilian stocks shed more than $200 billion and the iShares MSCI Brazil ETF (EWZ) plunged as much as 26% after Lula defeated Flávio Bolsonaro in a runoff that Polymarket and Kalshi had priced as an ~80% Bolsonaro win, with the real sliding up to 12% and B3 triggering its first circuit breaker since the pandemic.
