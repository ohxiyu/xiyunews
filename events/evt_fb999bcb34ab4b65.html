目前结论

Keystone reported that attackers exploited a third-party site analytics tool to obtain the WebAuth keys used to verify device authenticity, prompting it to destroy 18,835 in-stock hardware wallets made with the old keys, halt unshipped orders in favor of pre-sales, and shorten personal-data retention after 25,964 Shopify purchase records and 12,873 abandoned checkouts were exposed.
