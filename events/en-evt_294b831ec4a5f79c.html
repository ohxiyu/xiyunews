Current outcome

Meta released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full on 2026-10-12 with a 1 million token context window and new Active Memory and Active Focus capabilities; Muse Wonder is live via Meta Model API and Muse Code, with standard Wonder charging $4 per million input tokens, while Muse Wonder Full is limited to Muse Code's $50 or $200 monthly subscription tiers. Meta calls it a step toward 'personal superintelligence' and its own benchmarks put the models ahead of GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5, but independent Artificial Analysis scores show Wonder Full at 56 and Wonder at 52, above Fable 5.1 and Astra at 53 yet below Opus 5.5 at 58, with Abacus.AI's Bindu Reddy challenging the performance claims.
