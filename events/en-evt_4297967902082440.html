Current outcome

The Ledger hardware-wallet loss incident is now confirmed to involve the supply chain: devices sold through an authorized reseller contained unauthorized implants that could capture and exfiltrate recovery phrases, intensifying scrutiny of hardware-wallet supply-chain and self-custody security guarantees.

    Can crypto users trust the supply chain behind hardware wallets?

    Ledger confirmed that one affected device sold through authorized reseller CryptoBilis contained an unauthorized implant; the malicious implants captured users’ recovery phrases and transmitted them over cellular networks. Trezor also announced it would reduce resellers as a preventive step.

    State after update: The Ledger hardware-wallet loss incident is now confirmed to involve the supply chain: devices sold through an authorized reseller contained unauthorized implants that could capture and exfiltrate recovery phrases, intensifying scrutiny of hardware-wallet supply-chain and self-custody security guarantees.

    Crypto Hardware Wallets Hit in Estimated $70 Million Attack

    Ledger reported that cryptocurrency hardware wallets were drained in an attack estimated at $70 million, raising concerns about the security guarantees of self-custody devices.
