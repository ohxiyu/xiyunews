Current outcome

South Korea's Financial Services Commission has proposed detailed rules for tokenized securities, now in public consultation through Nov. 11 and scheduled to take effect on Feb. 4, 2027. The latest development shows the scope will extend to bonds and funds, initially focusing on institutional investors with private money-market funds, private debt, and trust-based unlisted equity tokenization, plus a pilot for public fractional investment securities; retail OTC annual net buy limits are set at 100 million won per exchange, and non-financial institutions with 4 billion won in capital may manage investor accounts.
