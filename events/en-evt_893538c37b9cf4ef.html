Current outcome

An onchain analyst tied approximately $18.4 million in extracted value across 10 Pons V2 memecoin launches on Robinhood Chain to a single rug-pull operation, in which the launch creators whitelisted wallets from the tokens' anti-sniping tax so those wallets could buy up most of each supply before dumping.
