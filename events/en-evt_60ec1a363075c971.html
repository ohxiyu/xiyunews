Current outcome

The New York Attorney General is seeking about $1.37 billion in disgorgement and interest from NovaTech founders Cynthia and Eddy Petion, alleging the project used $1.16 billion in investor crypto deposits to pay earlier investors and promoters while only a small fraction reached its trading provider, with four promoters settling for nearly $6 million and receiving permanent industry bans.
