Current outcome

Payward has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Lithuania-based Saldo Bank, expected to close in 2027 subject to regulatory approval; the deal would provide an EU banking license and expand into traditional banking services including interest-bearing deposits, loans, cards and euro payments, and is reportedly the first acquisition of a European bank by a crypto company.
