Current outcome

Alphabet's Q3 2026 results showed Google Cloud revenue of $30.709B, up 103% YoY, and expanded operating margin, but total revenue and ad revenue missed expectations, search advertising growth slowed, free cash flow was negative for a second consecutive quarter with a wider gap, GAAP EPS was hit by an unrealized SpaceX investment loss, and adjusted EPS also missed; the stock fell about 10% after hours. The company maintained its 2026 capex guidance and expects a significant increase in 2027.
