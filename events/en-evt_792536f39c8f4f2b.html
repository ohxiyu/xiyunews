Current outcome

An investigator posting on the Telegram channel "investigations" said they identified an additional $27M+ in centralized exchange (CEX) withdrawals by the illicit actor behind an incident whose total now exceeds $133M. According to the post, a portion of those CEX withdrawals took place after the investigator's initial disclosure. The investigator states that the absence of a proper incident disclosure allowed $30M+ to be laundered away that EU law enforcement could otherwise have frozen. The investigator's working theory is that a large Western European financial institution was exploited and that the matter is currently being covered up — a hypothesis, not a confirmed finding. The post lists four Ethereum addresses identified as theft addresses: 0x5cf70edce65847ef989933c3072fb0e7f335abc1, 0xe378cdccb369a64a0190577787ef61ca2eefa6b2, 0x3e6c3d135ca8f8be793e5fe1715ce6c057d140f2 and 0x99d314e6970b8d3db0dbe6f6e041cc904143400f.
