Current outcome

Underdog released Saluki 27B, a ~2-bit quantized derivative of Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B that shrinks from ~54GB to 7.89GB, runs on 16GB laptops, and reportedly beats the original on a 120-question tool-calling benchmark while retaining ~96% of its average benchmark score.
