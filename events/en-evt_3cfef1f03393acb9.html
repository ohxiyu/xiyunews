Current outcome

Datavault AI confirms about 1,328 BTC remain unavailable for withdrawal from its Biconomy wallet; it plans to reclassify the proceeds as an equity subscription receivable, restate affected financials, and reassess going-concern status, while its stock closed down 49.6% at about $0.067 on October 27.
