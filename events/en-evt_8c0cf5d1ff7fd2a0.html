Current outcome

BitMine holds approximately 6.02 million ETH (~4.9% of circulating supply) and has set a 5% hard cap, after which it will stop accumulating; it expects to reach the cap in 6–7 weeks. The previously disclosed staking of 5.07 million ETH was not addressed in this update.

    BitMine’s staked ETH equals nearly 12% of Ethereum’s active stake, but who controls it?

    BitMine has staked 5.07 million ETH (~85% of its 5.93 million ETH holdings), equal to roughly 11.8% of Ethereum's active stake, and ended its management-services agreement with Ethereum Tower while appointing affiliate American Validator to advise MAVAN Holdings.

    State after update: BitMine holds 5.93 million ETH, with 5.07 million ETH staked, representing about 11.8% of Ethereum's active stake, while the distribution of validators and signing-key control remains undisclosed.

    Source evidence: CryptoSlate

  3. #0310.08

    BitMine即将停止囤积ETH？Tom Lee称总量5%为「硬上限」

    At Token2049 Singapore, Tom Lee said BitMine has set a hard cap of 5% of ETH circulating supply and will stop buying once reached; it currently holds ~6,016,414 ETH (~4.9% of supply), needs ~90k–100k more ETH to hit the cap, expected in 6–7 weeks. After stopping, no further financing for purchases is needed.

    State after update: BitMine holds approximately 6.02 million ETH (~4.9% of circulating supply) and has set a 5% hard cap, after which it will stop accumulating; it expects to reach the cap in 6–7 weeks. The previously disclosed staking of 5.07 million ETH was not addressed in this update.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats · CryptoSlate
