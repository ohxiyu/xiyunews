Current outcome

Terence Tao published an essay titled "Mathematics has changed. Let's act like it," arguing that AI has transformed mathematical practice enough that researchers must rethink how credit is assigned, how problems are selected, and how results are digested by the field. The post drew high-engagement discussion on Hacker News about mathematics shifting from craftsmanship toward industrial-scale production. Commenters framed the essay as part of Tao's longer-running argument that, in mathematics, choosing a worthwhile problem, obtaining a proof entirely with AI, understanding it thoroughly and developing further questions from it can itself constitute a substantial contribution — even without supplying a new ingredient to the original proof. Discussants described the shift in terms of mathematics moving from an age of craftsmanship to an age of industrial production, and questioned why most writing on AI in mathematics assumes capabilities will not extend beyond theorem proving to tasks such as choosing which problems are worth pursuing, steering large-scale efforts, and explaining findings.
