    Cardano gives token issuers power to freeze, seize and restrict assets

    The Cardano Foundation released CIP-0113, a token standard that lets issuers of regulated assets like stablecoins, funds and bonds enforce identity and sanctions checks on every transfer and freeze, seize or redirect holdings without a network hard fork.
