Current outcome

Australia's ASIC opened consultation to extend its CFD intervention order to 2037 and bring digital-asset-settled contracts under CFD rules, subjecting them to retail leverage caps, forced margin close-outs and negative balance protection, with comments due 9 November and ministerial approval still required.
