  2. #0210.18
    Escalation00:45 UTC · publication time

    ⚡️彭博社：CoreWeave困境或导致AI垃圾债融资市场阶段性关闭

    Bloomberg reported that CoreWeave's financing troubles are spilling into the broader AI high-yield/junk-debt market: average high-yield yields rose to 8.41%, an 18-month high, and risk premiums widened to the highest since March; AI cloud startup Volta Infrastructure's $5 billion leveraged loan terms were revised to a yield above 11%; new AI infrastructure borrowers may face stricter terms or prohibitive unsecured funding costs. CoreWeave's co-founder said the company is exploring investment-grade debt and insurance funding, while CoreWeave said its finances are sufficient to service obligations and Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang publicly defended its financial health.

    State after update: CoreWeave bonds remain at distressed levels with yields around 15%; its financing concerns have spread to the broader AI high-yield market, where average yields hit an 18-month high and some AI infrastructure borrowers face stricter terms, raising fears of a temporary closure in AI junk-debt financing. CoreWeave says it has sufficient debt-service capacity and is exploring investment-grade debt and insurance funding.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats

  3. #0310.16
    Escalation04:06 UTC · publication time

    《金融时报》：投资者抛售数据中心债券，AI借款方面临优胜劣汰时刻

    CoreWeave bonds fell below 80 cents on the dollar with yields near 15%, reaching levels typically viewed as distressed; the company's stock dropped 17% this week; Nvidia lost about $270 billion in market value over the past week. Investors are dumping datacenter debt amid doubts that AI revenues can service the sector's massive debt load, with Allianz's CIO calling it a 'Darwin moment' for datacenter bonds.

    State after update: CoreWeave bonds now trade below 80 cents on the dollar with yields near 15%, and its stock is down 17% this week; AI datacenter debt is facing broad selling pressure amid intensifying concerns over debt service capacity and AI revenue sufficiency.
