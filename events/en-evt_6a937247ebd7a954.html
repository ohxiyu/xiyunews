Current outcome

Itaú Unibanco has notified Brazil's central bank of its crypto trading and custody operations, becoming the first Brazilian bank to do so under the new digital-asset rules; it supports 15 cryptoassets, with existing operators facing an October 30 deadline for notifications or applications.
