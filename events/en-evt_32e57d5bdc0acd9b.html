Current outcome

SoFi has announced SoFi Earn (SOFIE), an interest-bearing tokenized deposit at 4.4% APY, FDIC-insured within applicable limits, with full rollout to crypto members expected in November; SOFIE is convertible 1:1 into SoFiUSD, which itself is not an FDIC-insured deposit.
