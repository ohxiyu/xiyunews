Current outcome

Binance will accept five bStocks tokenized securities — BOTB, BRKBB, KSTRB, TMFB and VSTB — as eligible collateral in Cross Margin, Portfolio Margin and Portfolio Margin Pro from 2026-10-21 12:00 UTC, and will make the corresponding bStocks pairs available for margin trading.
