Current outcome

The White House has formed a 'Superintelligence Working Group' (SIF), co-led by the Director of National Intelligence, FTC Chair, DoD Under Secretary for Research and Engineering/CTO, and OPM Director, reporting to the President and White House Chief of Staff, tasked with a 120-day assessment of AI risks and opportunities, coordinating federal efforts, and preserving US leadership in superintelligence.
