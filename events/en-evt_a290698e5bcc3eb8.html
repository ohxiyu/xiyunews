Current outcome

RippleX and the XRP Ledger Foundation disclosed a decade-old payment-engine overflow bug that could have let an attacker mint ~18 trillion XRP beyond the fixed supply, earning a $250,000 maximum bounty after being patched in xrpld 3.4.1 with over 80% of validators upgraded.
