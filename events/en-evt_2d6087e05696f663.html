Current outcome

Starknet's community has approved the non-binding research proposal supporting research into becoming an independent post-quantum L1; a working group will develop technical proposals and a draft whitepaper is planned for November, but the vote does not authorize an actual transition, a later binding governance vote is still required, and Starknet currently remains an Ethereum L2.

    Starknet voters back research into life beyond Ethereum

    Starknet's seven-day non-binding STRK holder vote has closed and the proposal passed with about 93.52% support and record governance participation; StarkWare and the Starknet Foundation will form a working group and plan a draft whitepaper in November, but any actual transition still requires a separate binding vote, and Starknet continues operating as an Ethereum L2 for now.

    State after update: Starknet's community has approved the non-binding research proposal supporting research into becoming an independent post-quantum L1; a working group will develop technical proposals and a draft whitepaper is planned for November, but the vote does not authorize an actual transition, a later binding governance vote is still required, and Starknet currently remains an Ethereum L2.
