Current outcome

Riot Platforms signed a 15-year, ~$28B AI data-center agreement with Anthropic to provide 910 MW of capacity, with average annual rent of ~$1.8B; build costs are ~$5.5B, and Anthropic can recover eligible construction costs via rent credits plus 12% annual interest; Riot plans to finance 80–90% via debt, contributing $500M–$1.1B itself, with financing starting in 2027; Riot sold 20,264 BTC (~$1.5B) in the first nine months of 2026, holds 2,418 BTC, and first capacity is expected in June 2027.
