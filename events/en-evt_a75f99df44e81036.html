Current outcome

On November 7, AI data center developer Crusoe confirmed the sale of its unfinished 10GW data center project in southeastern Wyoming to Google. Google plans to invest $17 billion to take over and continue the development, though the specific acquisition price was not disclosed. This transaction marks a strategic shift where a top-tier AI lab moves from a potential customer role to direct ownership to mitigate construction cost and timeline risks. It highlights the systemic challenges independent developers face in delivering massive-scale AI infrastructure. The project was originally announced in 2025 by Crusoe and Blackstone-backed Tallgrass, with a target capacity of 10 gigawatts. Google previously considered being a customer but decided to take over due to concerns about management under Crusoe.
