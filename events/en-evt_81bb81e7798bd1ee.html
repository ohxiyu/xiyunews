Current outcome

A suspected private key leak in the 79AU/79thVault operational hot wallet on BNB Chain allowed an attacker to drain roughly 2.01 million 79AU through seven transfers, swap it back through the pool via ~95 trades, and cash out 16,249 BNB (~$12.5M), cutting the pool's USDT reserves from $15.2M to $3.9M.
