Current outcome

Meta and Sierra released the first 0.1 draft of the Personal Agent Protocol (PAP) code-named Poppy ahead of schedule, with OpenAI, Visa, Mastercard, PayPal and 35 other firms newly joining; Walmart, Shopify, Stripe and others had previously participated. PAP aims to standardize AI agent identity verification, user authorization, and enterprise service connection.
