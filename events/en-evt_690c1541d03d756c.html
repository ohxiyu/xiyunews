DevelopingCryptoExchange operationFirst tracked 2026-10-02Last changed 2026-10-07

Current outcome

Blast is ceasing operations because maintenance costs exceed revenue; users need to withdraw assets to Ethereum mainnet via the standard interface by Oct. 26; withdrawal wait time is planned to be shortened to 24 hours, but withdrawals are temporarily unavailable for about a week while Blast exits its Lido holdings, and after the deadline assets can still be withdrawn via the Blast Bridge contract on Ethereum L1. Bithumb and Upbit added BLAST to trading-caution watchlists and suspended deposits; Coinbase posted a status update requiring users to move BLAST off Coinbase by Oct. 26, with media reports that it will suspend BLAST trading on Oct. 20.
