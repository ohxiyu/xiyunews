Current outcome

Blast is ceasing operations because maintenance costs exceed revenue, and the standard withdrawal interface deadline passed on Oct 26; about $40.41 million remains in the bridge, and users must withdraw manually by interacting directly with Ethereum bridge contracts. Bithumb and Upbit added BLAST to trading-caution watchlists and suspended deposits; Upbit has further announced it will delist BLAST and will delist BLAST spot trading on Oct. 26 at 12:00 local time, with withdrawals halted during the associated network conversion. Coinbase requires users to move BLAST off Coinbase by Oct. 26 and is reported to suspend BLAST trading on Oct. 20.
