Current outcome

Entropy opened a temperature-check vote to make Paxos-issued USDG a core strategic initiative of ArbitrumDAO, adding 100 million ARB to the DRIP incentive program and routing Arbitrum Expansion Program fee revenue through the treasury committee into USDG.
