Current outcome

Baidu released and open-sourced Unlimited-OCR 2, a ~3B-parameter (5B active) document OCR model whose SR-SWA sparse attention speeds single-step decoding ~5.6x on 100-page documents and shrinks peak KV-cache memory from 47GB to 1.77GB, alongside a new long-document benchmark where it scored 77.06 versus 28.61 for Gemini 3.8 Flash.
