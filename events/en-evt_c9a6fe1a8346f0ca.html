Current outcome

Anthropic is pitching U.S. lawmakers on an unpublished draft bill that would let the government restrict a frontier model's release or use only when it poses a foreseeable, imminent risk of over $1 billion in damages or at least 50 deaths or serious injuries, while requiring regular independent evaluations and folding some state AI safety rules into a federal framework.
