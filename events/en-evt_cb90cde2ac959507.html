Current outcome

The investigation identified an attack vector affecting versions 2.4.1 and earlier; the team advises upgrading to 2.4.7 and says there are no credible reports of hot-wallet theft for users on the latest release.

    【BTCPay Server 已识别针对旧版本的攻击向量并提醒用户更新】

    BTCPay Server identified an attack vector affecting instances running version 2.4.1 or earlier, advised upgrading to 2.4.7, and said there are no credible reports of hot-wallet theft for users on the latest release.

    BTCPay Server reported that attackers are unauthorizedly moving funds from hot wallets on live instances and urged all operators to shut down their servers until the investigation concludes.
