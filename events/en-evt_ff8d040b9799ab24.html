Current outcome

Kalshi has confirmed it is developing exchange-traded futures tracking US CPI and core CPI, targeting a December launch, cleared by Kalshi Klear with Sunday-Friday 24-hour trading; the product has not yet launched.

    【彭博社：Kalshi 计划 12 月推出与美国 CPI 挂钩的期货合约】

    A Kalshi spokesperson confirmed the CPI futures are in development; Bloomberg-reviewed pitch documents show Kalshi Klear as clearinghouse, contracts covering 12 reference months plus May/November contracts for the following four years, and Sunday-Friday 24-hour trading; the report also says it would be the only directly CPI-linked futures product currently available.
