Current outcome

Cross-chain protocol Symbiosis reported detecting attempts to move Binance attack-related stolen funds through its platform, saying it is working to block the transfers, has contacted Binance, and will tighten screening mechanisms that were designed for normal fund flows and proved weak against fast, complex operations.
