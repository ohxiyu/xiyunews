Current outcome

Lido launched 0x02 CSM, letting permissionless node operators run 0x02 validators with up to 2048 ETH effective balance by posting only a bond of 32 ETH for the first key and 30 ETH for each subsequent key, under an initial cap of 0.01% of Lido Core TVL, or about 32 keys.
