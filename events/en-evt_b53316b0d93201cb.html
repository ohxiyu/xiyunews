Current outcome

SkyAI liquidated all of its SOL holdings and is proceeding with a $201 million cash tender offer at $3.00 per share, drawing a public rebuke from major shareholder Bastion Trading, which demands the board justify its oversight and improve terms by raising the price to at least $3.50, permitting 100% tender of pre-funded warrants, and excluding insider-related advisor warrants.
