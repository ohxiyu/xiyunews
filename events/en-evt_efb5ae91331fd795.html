Current outcome

Perplexity added the open-source REA (Reverse Engineer Anything) tool to its Computer agent, letting users describe a target application and have the AI analyze its behavior and rebuild a similar version, with online demos of Figma, LibreOffice Calc, Photopea, Harvey and Audacity clones plus up to 10,000 free credits through October 31.
