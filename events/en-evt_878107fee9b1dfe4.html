Current outcome

AI voice company Cartesia released an upgraded H-Net architecture that lets models learn their own byte-level segmentation and compression rather than depend on a fixed tokenizer, reporting ~22% lower compute at equal validation loss, roughly 2x downstream efficiency, parity with a ~11T-token open model at about one-tenth the training compute, and 6-7x efficiency gains in DNA modeling.
