    Abstract to Shut Down on Dec. 15; Igloo Says It Lost Tens of Millions Over Two Y

    Abstract, the consumer L2 backed by Pudgy Penguins parent Igloo, will shut down on December 15, 2026 after Igloo lost tens of millions of dollars over two years, with remaining resources redirected to Pudgy Penguins, Pudgy NFTs and PENGU.

    Abstract, the consumer-focused Layer 2 associated with Pudgy Penguins, announced it will shut down on December 15 after roughly two years of operation, with on-chain daily revenue reportedly just $2,646.

    Source evidence: foresightnews

    Pudgy Penguins founder Luca Netz confirmed Abstract is being wound down after cumulative losses in the tens of millions of dollars, with parent Igloo Inc refocusing entirely on Pudgy Penguins, Pudgy NFTs and PENGU.

    State after update: Abstract will shut down on December 15; founder Luca Netz confirmed cumulative losses in the tens of millions, and parent Igloo Inc will refocus on Pudgy Penguins and PENGU.

  4. #0410.07
    Confirmation2026-10-07 04:31 · publication time

    Pudgy Penguins’ Abstract becomes second Ethereum layer 2 to shut in a week

    The report adds key quantified facts: about $76 million in user assets remained on Abstract as of Wednesday and must be moved before the Dec. 15 shutdown or become inaccessible; it also reports roughly $3,900 in 24-hour chain fees versus about $39,000 in application revenue, notes Abstract launched in January 2025 and was funded by Igloo for about 18 months, and says the team explicitly rejected launching a token or ICO.

    State after update: Abstract will shut down on Dec. 15 with about $76 million in user assets still to be moved; Igloo, having lost tens of millions of dollars and declining to launch a token, will refocus resources on Pudgy Penguins, Pudgy NFTs and PENGU.
