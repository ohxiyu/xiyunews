Current outcome

Base completed its third mainnet upgrade, Cobalt, which includes condition-gated Validity Transactions, three new B20 token-standard features, and an opt-in administrative balance-seizure rule (seizeWithMemo) for B20 tokens.

    Base’s Cobalt upgrade adds another rule to affect token balances

    The Cobalt upgrade introduces an opt-in administrative balance-seizure rule (seizeWithMemo) for B20 tokens, requiring issuer eligibility configuration, SEIZE_ROLE authorization, and a separate recipient policy, while preserving token supply and keeping burnBlocked deprecated but callable.
