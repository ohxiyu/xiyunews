Current outcome

Scale AI's Scale Labs released the 'Humanity's Sixth Sense' benchmark of 522 image/video questions probing implicit real-world cues, finding that 25 multimodal models top out at 53.6% accuracy (GPT-6 Astra) versus 93.1% for humans, with failures overwhelmingly driven by missed perceptual clues rather than flawed logic.
