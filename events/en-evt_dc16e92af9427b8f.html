Current outcome

Rain and Modern Treasury have each applied to the OCC for national trust bank charters; Rain's proposed New York trust bank would provide fiduciary custody of digital assets and dollars, manage stablecoin reserves, and issue/redeem dollar-backed tokens without accepting deposits or offering FDIC insurance, and its application remains subject to OCC approval and public comment.
