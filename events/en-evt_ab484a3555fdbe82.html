Current outcome

Researchers documented a Chinese-speaking attacker who used the open-source Cairn multi-agent framework, driven through Claude Code and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, to break into more than 100 e-commerce sites including John Deere and SharkNinja at roughly $50 per successful breach — and whose own control panels were left unsecured, exposing 14 related backends, some of which targeted credit cards and crypto.
