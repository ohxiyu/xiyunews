Current outcome

The NFL filed an amicus brief with the Supreme Court supporting New Jersey's cert petition in the Kalshi case, arguing states may impose gambling regulation on prediction-market sports event contracts and disputing the CFTC's claim of exclusive federal jurisdiction, citing unmet manipulation safeguards, insider-betting limits, and age-21 requirements that Kalshi does not follow.
