    Tenstorrent hits $6.2B valuation to scale open-weight AI chips

    AI chip company Tenstorrent closed a $1.5 billion Series E at a $6.2 billion post-money valuation, the company announced on October 19. The round was led by Trace Capital and Innovation X, with participation from new and existing investors including ARK Invest, Fidelity Management & Research, Morgan Stanley, Sandisk, T. Rowe Price, Tether Investments, Virtu Financial and XTX Markets. Tenstorrent said it raised the money to stay independent after drawing acquisition interest earlier this year, keeping an alternative supplier of AI accelerators outside the Nvidia orbit. The company plans to use the proceeds to deploy its Galaxy servers at scale for open-weight large language and video generation models. The investor list also includes Agostinelli Group, Brett Barna, Cadence Ventures, Eclipse, Maverick Silicon, Protagonist, Troluce Capital and Tether Investments. Beyond server deployments, Tenstorrent will put funding toward licensing its AI and CPU intellectual property to customers designing their own chips.
