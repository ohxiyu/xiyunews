Current outcome

The Ethereum Foundation scheduled the Glamsterdam upgrade to activate on the Hoodi testnet on October 26 at 17:42 UTC, lifting the block gas limit to 200 million and introducing ePBS, block-level access lists, and revised gas pricing for state creation and data access.
