Current outcome

An AIFC court in Kazakhstan granted the financial regulator's request for injunctions barring Coinbase, BingX, Bitcoin.com, WEEX and two other centralized exchanges from offering or promoting unlicensed digital asset services and distributing their apps within the Astana International Financial Centre's jurisdiction.
