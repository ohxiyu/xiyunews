Current outcome

Thailand's SEC filed a criminal complaint against BG Limited, the operator of Bitget, for unlicensed digital asset trading and instructed the Ministry of Digital Economy and Society to block the exchange's website, associated domains and mobile app from December 5, urging users to act on their assets before the deadline.
