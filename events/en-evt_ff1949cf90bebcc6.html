Current outcome

Developers flagged that Zod's GitHub release tag v4.6.6 still points at a malicious commit (d821685) pushed with stolen maintainer credentials, part of a supply-chain campaign that used an Ethereum smart contract to deliver its JS payload and also hit standard-schema and nub — but which was caught, reverted, and never published to npm.
