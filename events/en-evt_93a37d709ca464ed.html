Current outcome

S&P Global has closed its acquisition of OpenZeppelin, which operates as a standalone business unit under the OpenZeppelin name, led by founder Demian Brener reporting to S&P Global Ratings president Yann Le Pallec; financial terms were not disclosed.

    Ratings giant S&P Global acquires OpenZeppelin in tokenized finance risk push

    S&P Global has acquired smart contract security firm OpenZeppelin to address technological risks facing banks and asset managers in tokenized finance, following its earlier investment in market-data provider Kaiko.
