Current outcome

Papertrade remains live, but PAPER is currently and near-term non-transferable; fees and minting curve have been adjusted, diluting early holders; PAPER pre-market perpetuals were force-settled by Paragon and switched to reduce-only with delisting planned by Aster; BTC and ETH open interest declined sharply, TVL fell to about $58.3 million, and negative sentiment including “soft rug” accusations has emerged.

    On October 20, 2026, Papertrade announced PAPER is non-transferable currently and near-term and adjusted its fee structure and token minting curve; Paragon paused and force-settled PAPER pre-market perpetuals, settling all open positions at about $0.026296; Aster switched PAPER pre-market perpetuals to reduce-only and prepared to delist; platform BTC open interest fell 41% (from about $390 million to $230 million), ETH open interest fell 55% (from about $150 million to $66.3 million), and TVL dropped about 7% to about $58.3 million.

    State after update: Papertrade remains live, but PAPER is currently and near-term non-transferable; fees and minting curve have been adjusted, diluting early holders; PAPER pre-market perpetuals were force-settled by Paragon and switched to reduce-only with delisting planned by Aster; BTC and ETH open interest declined sharply, TVL fell to about $58.3 million, and negative sentiment including “soft rug” accusations has emerged.
