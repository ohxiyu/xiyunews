    ⚡️Papertrade已正式上线，11,465个地址存入1.38亿美元

    Papertrade, a fully on-chain synthetic perpetuals protocol on HyperEVM that prices off Hyperliquid's order-book midpoint and mints its PAPER token solely from user losses and liquidations, has officially launched after closing pre-deposits of $138 million from 11,465 addresses.
