Current outcome

South Korea's government is seeking to postpone the crypto gains tax to 2029, while studying loss carryforwards and advancing related legislation; this follows the presidential office's earlier decision to again delay the tax originally set for January 2027.

  1. #0111.05
    Escalation07:07 UTC · publication time

    ⚡️韩国拟将加密货币收益税推迟两年至2029年，或允许亏损结转抵扣

    Finance Minister Lee Hyoung-il said the government will seek to delay the 22% crypto gains tax by two years to 2029 and will consult the National Assembly on legislation; it will also study allowing three-year loss carryforwards and push a digital asset bill. If approved, this would be the fourth postponement since 2020.

    Initial07:49 UTC · publication time

    韩国推迟原定于明年1月实施的加密资产所得税征收

    South Korea's presidential office has decided to again postpone the crypto asset income tax originally set to take effect in January 2027, citing the pending CARF information-exchange timeline and concerns that taxing early would concentrate the burden on domestic exchange users.
