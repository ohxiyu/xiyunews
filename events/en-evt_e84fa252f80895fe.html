Current outcome

Citi and Coinbase are expanding their partnership, building on existing fiat-stablecoin payment integration to plan a stablecoin checkout product for institutional merchants that allows fiat settlement without holding digital assets; general availability and supported tokens remain unspecified.

    Escalation20:35 UTC · publication time

    Citi Details Stablecoin Checkout With Coinbase and Fiat Payouts

    Citi outlined plans for a stablecoin checkout product built with Coinbase that lets institutional merchants settle in fiat without holding digital assets, in a US-first expansion.

    State after update: Citi and Coinbase are expanding their partnership, building on existing fiat-stablecoin payment integration to plan a stablecoin checkout product for institutional merchants that allows fiat settlement without holding digital assets; general availability and supported tokens remain unspecified.

    Source evidence: The Defiant

    Confirmation07:18 UTC · publication time

    Citi and Coinbase Expand Partnership to Bridge Fiat Banking and Stablecoin Payments

    Citi and Coinbase are expanding their partnership to link traditional fiat banking infrastructure with stablecoin payment rails, extending institutional access between bank accounts and on-chain dollar settlement.

    Source evidence: The Daily Hodl

  3. #0309.29
    Escalation06:00 UTC · publication time

    Coinbase powers Citi’s new stablecoin rails, and corporate banking faces a major shift

    Citi institutional clients can now accept stablecoin payments through Spring by Citi using Coinbase payments infrastructure, and Coinbase Virtual Accounts can automatically convert incoming fiat into stablecoins via Citi's Virtual Account Wallet.

    State after update: The Citi-Coinbase partnership has advanced from an exploratory plan to two defined customer payment paths: institutional clients can accept stablecoin payments via Spring by Citi, and Coinbase Virtual Accounts customers can auto-convert fiat receipts into stablecoins, though customer counts, volumes, and pricing remain undisclosed.

    Source evidence: CryptoSlate · Decrypt · tokenpost.com

    State after update: Citi and Coinbase are expanding their partnership to give businesses infrastructure connecting traditional fiat payments with stablecoins, extending round-the-clock fiat access to a combined user base of more than 150 million.
