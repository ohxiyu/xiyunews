Current outcome

The Citi-Coinbase partnership has advanced from an exploratory plan to two defined customer payment paths: institutional clients can accept stablecoin payments via Spring by Citi, and Coinbase Virtual Accounts customers can auto-convert fiat receipts into stablecoins, though customer counts, volumes, and pricing remain undisclosed.
