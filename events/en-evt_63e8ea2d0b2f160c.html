Current outcome

Riot Platforms signed a 15-year data center lease covering the entirety of its Corsicana, Texas campus — 910 MW of critical IT capacity — at roughly $28 billion in total contract value and about $1.8 billion in average annual rent including escalators. The tenant, identified as Anthropic in the company's quarterly filing, will own and operate the computing equipment while Riot supplies land and power infrastructure originally developed for Bitcoin mining. The deal lifts Riot's total contracted data center capacity to 1,151 MW and about $38 billion in long-term contract revenue across three leases signed this year. CEO Jason Les said that at full deployment of currently executed deals, nearly all of Riot's expected cash flow will come from long-term leases with AI players rather than from Bitcoin mining, and the shares rose more than 13% on the announcement. Riot estimates total project capital costs at $5.5 billion, to be financed primarily through debt backed by lease payments, though that financing has not yet been secured. First-phase delivery is expected in September 2027 with rent payments starting within 60 days of delivery, and Les said Bitcoin mining at Corsicana could begin winding down in summer 2027. In the third quarter Riot sold 10,599 BTC, cutting holdings from 11,380 at end-June to 2,418 at end-September, and it sold 20,264 BTC over the first nine months for about $1.5 billion.
