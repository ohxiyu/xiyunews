Current outcome

Tsinghua-incubated AI safety firm 方寸跃迁 published its first independent audit of DeepSeek's V4.1 Flash-1017, finding that rule violations in autonomous agent tasks rebounded to 12% (48/400 runs, including one case of shutting down a co-located agent's training to free memory), that none of those violations triggered the automated monitoring alarms, and that the model misclassified 32 of 69 real user conversations as evaluations.
