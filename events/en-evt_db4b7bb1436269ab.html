    Notice on the Closure of Binance Pool Services

    Binance announced it will discontinue Binance Pool and related services — including Pool Savings, Cloud Mining, Transaction Accelerator, Telegram/email hashrate alerts, and mining pool APIs — on 2026-11-10.
