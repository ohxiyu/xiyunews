Current outcome

Italian financial police arrested 30 people and broke up a mafia-linked ring that laundered roughly €68 million ($76 million) from drug trafficking, scams and fraudulent invoicing through a Milan car-rental front, shell companies and crypto exchanges, moving funds into European banks and suspect-controlled wallets.
