Current outcome

The US Treasury sanctioned Qi Lu's MiraclePlus accelerator and four related Chinese entities, including Shanghai Zhisuan Technology, for providing seed funding and equity to a former Google engineer convicted of stealing TPU/GPU and AI supercomputing trade secrets, freezing US-jurisdictional assets and barring US persons from certain investments with a wind-down period through January 2027.
