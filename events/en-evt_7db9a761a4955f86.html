Current outcome

CFTC Chair Michael Selig said the proposed Reg CTX rule would allow exchanges to offer retail margin, leverage and financing on crypto spot trading with requirements aligned to perpetual futures (~7x max), while companion Reg CAM would create a federal 'crypto asset market' license for spot-leverage-only exchanges, with finalization targeted within months.
