Current outcome

Samsung Wallet will add USDC on Sui in late October 2026 and make it the default dollar stablecoin for US users; it will enable USDC transfers to compatible wallets and off-ramp to bank accounts in 60+ countries via Bastion infrastructure; custody is provided by Bastion and Coinbase Prime (NYDFS-compliant); available on 82 million compatible Galaxy devices in the US with identity verification; Solana and Sui are technical partners; no fee for transfers to external wallets, international bank transfers incur fees; future online and in-store stablecoin payments may follow.
