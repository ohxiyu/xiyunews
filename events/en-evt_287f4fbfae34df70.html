Current outcome

The Utah Attorney General's Office filed criminal charges against Kalshi and its co-founders for allegedly promoting illegal online gambling in the state, citing undercover bets placed from Utah, while Kalshi called the action an overreach and vowed to fight it in court.

  1. #0110.20
    Initial2026-10-20 21:19 · publication time

    Utah AG sues Kalshi, pursues profits from prediction market company

    Utah Attorney General Derek Brown sued Kalshi, its affiliates and two Susquehanna trading companies, alleging the platform has facilitated illegal event-contract betting for over 32,000 state residents since 2024 and asking a court to force the return of about $30–60 million in profits.

    Source evidence: Cointelegraph · theblockbeats · foresightnews

  2. #0210.22
    Escalation2026-10-22 23:50 · publication time

    Pentagon bans US forces from using military info to bet in prediction markets

    Pentagon chief Pete Hegseth issued a memo prohibiting all Department of War personnel from using classified or nonpublic information to place wagers on military actions via prediction markets such as Kalshi and Polymarket, citing operational security and judgment risks.

    Source evidence: Cointelegraph · foresightnews

  3. #0310.26
    Confirmation2026-10-26 16:50 · publication time

    NBA与CFTC签署预测市场监管协议，Kalshi和Polymarket成首选合作平台

    The CFTC and the NBA signed a memorandum of understanding to share information and coordinate on preventing insider trading and manipulation in prediction markets, with the NBA designating Kalshi and Polymarket as its preferred prediction-market platforms while state-versus-federal jurisdiction over such contracts remains contested.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats · Cointelegraph · foresightnews · Decrypt

  4. #0410.27
    Escalation2026-10-27 19:21 · publication time

    CFTC sues Utah to block action against prediction markets

    The CFTC sued Utah and its attorney general to block state action against Kalshi, arguing that Utah is improperly interfering with CFTC-registered exchanges and seeking to recover up to $60 million in prediction-market profits.

    Source evidence: Cointelegraph · foresightnews

  5. #0510.29

    The Utah Attorney General's Office filed criminal charges against Kalshi and its co-founders for allegedly promoting illegal online gambling in the state, citing undercover bets placed from Utah, while Kalshi called the action an overreach and vowed to fight it in court.

  6. #0610.29
    Escalation2026-10-29 05:11 · publication time

    美国犹他州对预测市场平台Kalshi及其两名联合创始人提起14项刑事指控

    Utah criminally charged Kalshi and its two co-founders, Tarek Mansour and Luana Lopes Lara, with 14 counts, including one felony, 12 gambling-promotion charges and one misdemeanor gambling charge; prosecutors said an undercover officer used local details to place 12 sports-event bets on Kalshi in September.

    State after update: Utah has escalated beyond its civil suit by filing 14 criminal charges against Kalshi and its co-founders; Kalshi argues its sports-event contracts are CFTC-regulated derivatives rather than gambling, while the CFTC has sued Utah to block state action, leaving civil and criminal proceedings underway in parallel.
