Current outcome

MetaMask is exiting 17,000 validators tied to its non-custodial staking security incident, covering over 523,000 stETH (~$1.4 billion), after rewards from 18 validators were diverted to a Tornado Cash-funded address; Renzo previously said its Basis/ezETH were unaffected with only 214.75 stETH in its withdrawal queue.
