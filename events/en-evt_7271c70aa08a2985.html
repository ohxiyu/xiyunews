Current outcome

Mastercard has opened early access to Agent Pay for Machines, letting AI agents discover and pay for services within human-set budgets via x402 payment vouchers settled through Coinbase wallets, Base on-chain settlement and BVNK fiat/stablecoin rails; it has also run a live demo, offered developer APIs/SDKs/sandbox, and begun pilots with Cloudflare, AWS and Coinbase.
