Current outcome

FINMA has approved Switzerland's first Swiss-law spot Bitcoin ETF for retail investors, BIT Asset Management's BBIT, expected to begin trading on SIX Swiss Exchange in November with public offering restricted to Switzerland; other fund providers have also submitted Bitcoin ETF applications.

    Switzerland's FINMA approved the country's first spot Bitcoin ETF for retail investors, using a regulated investment-fund structure with stricter custody, operational-risk and disclosure requirements instead of the debt-security format used by existing Swiss Bitcoin ETPs.
