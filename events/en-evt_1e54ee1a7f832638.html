Current outcome

FINMA approved BIT Asset Management's BBIT spot Bitcoin ETF on Oct. 20; it is expected to begin trading on SIX Swiss Exchange in November but the exact date is undetermined. Public offering remains restricted to Switzerland, while overseas banks and brokers can access it via approved or existing trading participants; other crypto-asset ETFs are not automatically approved by this decision.
