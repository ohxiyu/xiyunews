Current outcome

Kintsu, a liquid staking protocol on the Monad blockchain, said on October 31 that its official website kintsu.xyz has been restored and again points to the project's own infrastructure. The team warned users to keep avoiding the site and not to connect wallets until DNS caches fully refresh and it issues an official security confirmation, noting that some networks may still reach the malicious site for up to an hour. Security firm Blockaid, which flagged the ongoing front-end attack, advised users not to use the affected decentralized applications while it investigates. Kintsu's warning indicates the risk is not fully cleared even after the domain was restored, because DNS propagation delays can keep serving the malicious version. According to Blockaid, malicious scripts were injected into the affected sites so that users who clicked "Connect Wallet" could be redirected to a page designed to move their assets. Blockaid said the attack was linked to the StepDrainer toolkit and involved three sites: kintsu, flap and stonkbrokers.
