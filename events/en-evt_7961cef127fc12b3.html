    Zcash holders overwhelmingly back faster transactions and bitcoin-style halvings

    Zcash holders approved NU7 governance measures with overwhelming majorities, including 25-second block times, preserving the bitcoin-style halving schedule, delaying Network Sustainability Mechanism fee reissuance until 2031, and disabling obsolete Sprout transactions.

    Source evidence: CoinDesk

  2. #0210.05
    Aftermath2026-10-05 04:41 · publication time

    Zcash’s 25-second blocks go live on public testnet ahead of schedule

    Zcash's NU7 upgrade activated on public testnet, cutting target block time from 75 seconds to 25 seconds ahead of schedule; 60% of transaction fees redirected to a future mining reward reserve; legacy Sprout privacy system disabled. Mainnet planned for Nov. 5, with developers to review test results and decide on activation Oct. 20.
