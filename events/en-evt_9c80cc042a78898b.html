Current outcome

South Korea's Financial Intelligence Unit has expanded its crackdown on unregistered crypto exchanges by blocking website access to 27 platforms including MEXC and KuCoin, in addition to existing app-store restrictions, while barring registered VASPs from transferring virtual assets to undeclared entities.
