Current outcome

Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon, its next frontier model, claiming "significant gains" in coding, agentic tasks and cybersecurity. Google says the model takes the top spot on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (v5.0), ahead of Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 and OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra. Google is positioning Carbon as the leader on a widely cited composite benchmark that weighs agents, coding, general capability and scientific reasoning, placing it ahead of the two rival flagship models it names. According to Google's announcement, Gemini 4 Carbon launches at the same introductory price as Argon — $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input tokens at 95% off the input price. Google says internal teams have seen the model "run for days to persist through dead ends" on complex, multi-step engineering tasks, while some third-party coverage has described earlier reporting on the model as based on internal testing.
