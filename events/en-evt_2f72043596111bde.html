Current outcome

South Korea's FIU approved domestic website blocks on 27 unregistered overseas crypto exchanges and VASPs, including KuCoin, MEXC, Phemex, Bitrue, BingX, BitMart and Poloniex, following Korea Media and Communications Standards Commission reviews on Oct. 6–7; it plans to provide withdrawal guidance through DAXA with operator cooperation.

    South Korea Orders Website Blocks on 27 Unregistered Overseas Crypto Exchanges,

    South Korea's Financial Intelligence Unit ordered domestic website blocks on 27 unregistered overseas crypto exchanges including KuCoin, MEXC, BingX, BitMart and Poloniex, expanding existing app-level restrictions and referring 44 unregistered VASPs to investigators, with withdrawal guidance to be provided to affected users through DAXA.
