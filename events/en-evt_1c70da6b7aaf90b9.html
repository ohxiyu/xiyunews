Current outcome

OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic have aligned on a shared robots.txt rule (`User-Agent: __usage_ai-training` + `Disallow: /`) that lets sites opt out of AI training in one step instead of configuring each crawler separately, with Anthropic adopting it now and the others rolling it out between the coming months and 2027 under an IETF draft.
