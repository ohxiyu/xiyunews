    SEC Staff Clarifies Token Buybacks and Liquid Staking in New FAQs

    SEC staff released new FAQs clarifying that qualifying liquid staking receipts fall outside securities laws and reaffirming earlier guidance on token buybacks for maintenance and development funding.

    SEC Staff Says Token Buybacks Don't Make Crypto a Security—If the Network Works

    新报道详细说明了同一批SEC员工FAQ中关于代币回购的指引：在已具备功能性的加密网络上，回购公告不构成Howey测试下的“关键管理努力”；但在尚未具备功能性的网络上，若将回购宣传为收益或回报来源，仍可能触发证券法。
