Current outcome

Tesla and NVIDIA will invest a combined 3.7 trillion KRW (~$2.5B) in Samsung Electro-Mechanics to expand next-generation FC-BGA substrate lines — Tesla's dedicated Sejong line for FSD/Optimus AI chips under a 2029–2033 supply deal, and NVIDIA-funded Sejong and Busan expansions for AI compute and datacenter networking substrates, with mass production targeted for H2 2028.
