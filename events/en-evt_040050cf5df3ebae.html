Current outcome

Google DeepMind has open-sourced EmbeddingGemma 2, an Apache 2.0-licensed 740M-parameter multimodal embedding model unifying text, code, image, video and audio retrieval, with flexible on-device loading and vector dimension reduction, improving code retrieval benchmarks over the prior generation.
