Current outcome

From Nov. 1, Binance will require Brazilian users to declare the purpose and counterparty of cross-border crypto transfers, block withdrawals without the questionnaire, potentially return incomplete deposits, report transactions monthly to Brazil's central bank, and cap some transfers at $100,000 where the counterparty is not FX-authorized in Brazil.
