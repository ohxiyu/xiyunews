    Escalation2026-10-09 01:04 · publication time

    Binance to Restrict 8 Services and Delist 22 Tokens in Brazil to Comply With New

    Binance will restrict eight services and halt trading in 22 tokens for Brazilian users starting October 27 and migrate them to a local group entity by October 29 to comply with new central bank crypto regulations.

    Source evidence: wublockchainenglish · Hokanews · FinanceFeeds · CryptoRank

  3. #0310.05
    Escalation2026-10-05 01:32 · publication time

    Binance to Block Brazil Cross-Border Crypto Transfers From Nov. 1 Without Purpos

    Added: withdrawals will be blocked without the completed questionnaire; incomplete deposits may remain pending or be returned; transfers up to $50,000 use a 10-purpose list while larger ones require one of 96 classifications; some transfers are capped at $100,000 if the counterparty is not FX-authorized in Brazil; transfers between Brazilian residents are unaffected.

    State after update: From Nov. 1, Binance will require Brazilian users to declare the purpose and counterparty of cross-border crypto transfers, block withdrawals without the questionnaire, potentially return incomplete deposits, report transactions monthly to Brazil's central bank, and cap some transfers at $100,000 where the counterparty is not FX-authorized in Brazil.

    Source evidence: wublockchainenglish
