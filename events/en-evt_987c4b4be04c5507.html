Current outcome

DeepSeek open-sourced its DeepInfer inference framework and disclosed that its cluster handles about 155 trillion tokens per day, equivalent to roughly $38 in theoretical billable revenue per GPU-hour, though this figure does not account for utilization, paid-request share, or hardware and power costs.
