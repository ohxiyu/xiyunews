Current outcome

The US Independent Community Bankers of America sued the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, alleging it exceeded its National Bank Act authority by granting national trust bank charters to crypto companies such as Coinbase, Circle, Crypto.com and WLFI, which enter the banking system under lighter capital, liquidity, supervision and FDIC-insurance requirements than community banks.
