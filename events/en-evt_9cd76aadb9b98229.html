Current outcome

BitMart confirmed a new hack on Monday, and founder Sheldon Xia said in a post on X on Wednesday that the exchange's security team is "currently severely understaffed" following restructuring. Xia described the amount involved as small without disclosing a figure, while blockchain analytics firm Bitquery identified approximately $391,000 in suspicious transfers from wallets mostly associated with BitMart across three blockchains. Bitquery traced the funds to a wallet also implicated in the more than $46 million Mercado Bitcoin hack on Monday, Nov. 2, supporting Xia's preliminary suspicion of a possible connection between the two incidents. Xia said threats against employees and alleged retaliation by former staff had complicated efforts to retain technical personnel. Cointelegraph said it had contacted BitMart for details on the restructuring, security staffing and the latest breach but had not received a response by publication.
