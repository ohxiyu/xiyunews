Current outcome

Coinbase has published a post-quantum readiness proposal urging Bitcoin to act now, recommending flexible post-quantum signatures, staged adoption, and a backup quantum-proof key, while separating the dormant-wallet issue. The plan remains a proposal, not deployed.
