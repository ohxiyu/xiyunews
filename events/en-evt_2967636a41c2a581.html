Current outcome

Meta's equity financing plan has been upsized to $97.5B, or up to about $103B including the greenshoe, comprising $25B of common stock, about $12.5B of Series A depositary shares and up to $60B through an at-the-market program; the $25B common stock offering drew about 5x oversubscription at $625 per share to fund AI investments.
