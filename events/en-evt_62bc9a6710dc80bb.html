Current outcome

Coverage for assets stolen in Binance's security incident has risen to 90%, with ten major tokens fully covered and the remaining 10% in less liquid assets; a new proof-of-reserves snapshot is complete and withdrawals remain scheduled to reopen on Nov 1.

    Remediation2026-11-01 01:24 · publication time

    【币安：被盗资产补足比例升至 90%，提现仍计划周日恢复】

    Binance said stolen-asset coverage rose from about 80% to 90%, with BTC, ETH, USDT, BNB, SOL, USDC, XRP, USD1, RLUSD and ZEC fully covered; a new proof-of-reserves snapshot was completed on Oct 31 at 08:00, with the full zero-knowledge proof expected around Nov 3; withdrawals remain planned to reopen Nov 1, and part of the SAFU draw is expected within 24 hours.

    Initial2026-10-30 08:48 · publication time

    【币安：预计 45 小时后恢复提现，后续调查发现更多入侵点】

    Binance said its investigation into the security breach uncovered additional intrusion points beyond a compromised laptop, that roughly $3 billion in affected assets has been frozen with partners, that all losses will be covered by the SAFU fund and corporate capital, and that withdrawals should resume in about 45 hours while deposits remain unaffected.
