Current outcome

CoinDCX has admitted operational losses from a failed process to move deposits out of previously compromised wallets, saying affected accounts were credited and current wallets are safe; scrutiny continues over the roughly $16 million in suspicious transfers and whether old addresses remained in use.

Progress timeline

4 material updates
  1. #0110.22
    Initial2026-10-22 08:06 · publication time

    CoinDCX Faces Suspected $16 Million Hack After $44 Million Breach in 2025

    On-chain investigators flagged roughly $16 million in USDT and TRX flowing from CoinDCX hot wallets and deposit addresses to a single TRON address before being split toward Binance, OKX and Gate deposit addresses, an unconfirmed breach at the Indian exchange that previously lost about $44 million in July 2025.

    Source evidence: wublockchainenglish

  2. #0210.22
    Response2026-10-22 09:05 · publication time

    【CoinDCX 称旧钱包链上活动与当前运营无关，ZachXBT 质疑其仍用于归集用户存款】

    CoinDCX responded that the on-chain activity involved old wallets compromised in July 2025 and unused since, unrelated to current operations; ZachXBT disputed this, alleging a linked deposit address was still used by a liquidity provider through 2026 to receive gas and consolidate user deposits into CoinDCX’s current public hot wallet.

    State after update: CoinDCX denies that the old-wallet activity affects current operations and says user funds are safe, while ZachXBT disputes whether a linked deposit address was still used to consolidate user deposits; the earlier roughly 15.99 million USDT transfer to a TRON address remains disputed and under monitoring.

    Source evidence: foresightnews

  3. #0310.22
    Escalation2026-10-22 10:17 · publication time

    【Salus CMO 质疑 CoinDCX 的旧钱包解释，称最新储备证明所列地址向疑似黑客地址转账】

    Salus CMO Darcy further disputed CoinDCX’s legacy-wallet explanation, alleging that two addresses listed in CoinDCX’s latest proof of reserves sent funds to suspected hacker addresses and that one hot wallet kept distributing 15 TRX for gas around the alleged incident, with some funds flowing to Binance, OKX, Gate and Huione/Xinbi-linked addresses.

    State after update: CoinDCX denies that the old-wallet activity affects current operations and says user funds are safe, but ZachXBT disputes whether a linked deposit address was still used to consolidate user deposits, and Salus CMO Darcy alleges that addresses in the latest proof of reserves sent funds to suspected hacker addresses; the suspected roughly $16 million breach remains disputed and under monitoring.

    Source evidence: foresightnews

  4. #0410.22
    Response2026-10-22 10:53 · publication time

    CoinDCX admits ‘operational losses’ after $16M wallet transfers draw scrutiny

    CoinDCX admitted “operational losses” after failing to sweep user deposits out of wallets compromised in its July 2025 hack, saying it absorbed the losses and credited all affected accounts, while maintaining that current wallets were unaffected, no active-wallet funds were compromised and customer assets remained in segregated cold wallets.

    State after update: CoinDCX has admitted operational losses from a failed process to move deposits out of previously compromised wallets, saying affected accounts were credited and current wallets are safe; scrutiny continues over the roughly $16 million in suspicious transfers and whether old addresses remained in use.

    Source evidence: Cointelegraph · theblockbeats
