Current outcome

Nasdaq-listed Evernorth has selected Ripple, Kraken, Coinbase and BitGo as its first institutional partners to deploy roughly 473 million XRP (about $648 million) starting in Q4, with later expansion into XRP Ledger lending and on-chain DeFi strategies led by former Coinbase Asset Management co-CIO Hugo Jeandesboz.
