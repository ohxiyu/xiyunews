Current outcome

Ripple-backed Evernorth completed its SPAC merger with Armada Acquisition Corp. II and will begin trading on Nasdaq under ticker XRPN on October 12, carrying roughly 473 million XRP and about $300 million in gross cash proceeds from investors including Ripple, SBI Group, Pantera Capital, Kraken and GSR, while its accounting model recognizes impairment losses without allowing reversals when XRP recovers.
