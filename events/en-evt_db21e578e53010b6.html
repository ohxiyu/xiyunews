Current outcome

tBTC minting and redemption remain paused, but tBTC remains fully BTC-backed with no unauthorized minting, Bitcoin transfers, or fund loss found; node operators are patching or offline, and the bridge will reopen after security maintenance is completed and nodes resume on patched systems.

    Response00:00 UTC · publication time

    Threshold Network暂停tBTC跨链功能，称资产储备充足且未发生资金损失

    Threshold attributed the tBTC pause to Linux kernel vulnerability CVE-2026-98432, saying all tBTC node operators patched or temporarily took nodes offline; one operator separately reported an unrelated infrastructure incident in which the Ethereum fee account for its tBTC node lost about 0.5 ETH, with no evidence of signing-key material access.

    State after update: tBTC minting and redemption remain paused, but tBTC remains fully BTC-backed with no unauthorized minting, Bitcoin transfers, or fund loss found; node operators are patching or offline, and the bridge will reopen after security maintenance is completed and nodes resume on patched systems.

  2. #0211.01
    Initial04:01 UTC · publication time

    【Threshold：tBTC 全额抵押，新存款、铸造和赎回仍处于暂停状态】

    Threshold Network reported that tBTC remains fully collateralized with no unauthorized minting or losses, but new deposits, minting, and redemptions are paused for cross-chain security maintenance while transfers on Ethereum and L2s continue.
