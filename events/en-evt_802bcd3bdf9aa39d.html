Current outcome

South Korea's FSC Chairman Lee Eog-weon told a National Assembly hearing that deliberations on the Digital Asset Basic Act are nearly complete and the government will push to introduce the framework—covering exchange licensing, stablecoin issuance rules and clearer legal definitions—as soon as possible, while saying crypto taxation should account for various circumstances as the repeatedly delayed levy is set for January 1, 2027.
