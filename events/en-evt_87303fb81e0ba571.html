Current outcome

SkyAI remains in an ongoing SOL liquidation: it has disclosed selling 1 million SOL for about $107M and may sell further; on-chain outflows since early October total about 1.57M SOL (~$175M), with unrealized losses over $160M on a ~$194 average cost basis, while the previously announced buyback of 95% of shares continues to be funded.
