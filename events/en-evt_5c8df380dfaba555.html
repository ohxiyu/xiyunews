Current outcome

Solidus Labs analysis published October 29 alleges a trader used two Hyperliquid accounts to place over $170 million in anomalous crude oil perpetual orders — repeatedly cancelling large quotes while filling on the opposite side at roughly a 30:1 ratio — netting about $119,000 over three days, conduct it says matches spoofing though intent is unproven, while Hyperliquid notes all order and trade records are public on-chain.
