Current outcome

The OCC has issued its final rule implementing the GENIUS Act, setting reserve, redemption, risk-management, custody and capital requirements for payment stablecoin issuers under its jurisdiction when the law takes effect on January 18, 2027; AML and sanctions rules are deferred to a joint rulemaking with Treasury, and the timing for accepting applications and waivers will be announced separately.

    【美国 OCC 发布《GENIUS Act》首个最终规则，不预设现有稳定币奖励计划合规】

    The U.S. OCC issued its final rule implementing the GENIUS Act — the first such final rule from a federal banking regulator — establishing the framework for regulated and foreign firms to issue and custody stablecoins from January 18, 2027, while refusing to presume existing stablecoin rewards programs are compliant or to offer them a safe harbor, applying a case-by-case reading of the ban on rewards paid 'solely' for holding or using a stablecoin, and replacing proposed rebuttable presumptions and prescriptive reserve diversification limits with principles-based supervision.

    OCC Finalizes GENIUS Act Stablecoin Rules Ahead of January Deadline

    The final rule further clarifies that Bank Secrecy Act, AML and sanctions requirements are excluded and will be handled in a separate joint rulemaking with Treasury; the OCC will separately announce when it begins accepting issuer applications and waiver requests, and the rule covers reserves, redemption, risk management, custody, capital and state-qualified issuer transitions.
