Current outcome

The controversy over the Senate report has entered a public-response phase: Blumenthal continues to seek Treasury and Justice Department scrutiny of Tether's sanctions compliance, while Tether CEO Ardoino rebuts that its freezes preceded formal sanctions and cut Iran-related stablecoin volume by 94%, without addressing the Cantor Fitzgerald records-request deadline.
