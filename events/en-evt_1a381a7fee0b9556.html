Current outcome

Ripple announced a partnership with Brazil's central securities depository CSD BR to use the XRP Ledger as an additional record and audit layer, starting with tokenized BTG Pactual investment fund shares issued under the Multi-Purpose Token standard and later expanding to native issuance and trading of assets such as CRI and CRA.
