Current outcome

Open USD is natively issued on Tempo, Base, Ethereum, and Solana with more than 200 partner organizations; circulation is about 468 million tokens with over $400 million in on-chain liquidity. Stripe is set to distribute OUSD through its payments stack, Coinbase began supporting the network on Oct. 1, and Mastercard has announced OUSD access via BVNK, enabling fiat and stablecoin conversion and settlement.
