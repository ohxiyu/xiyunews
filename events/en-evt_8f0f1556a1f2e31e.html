Current outcome

The two Franklin Templeton U.S. equity and Bitcoin combination ETFs (FBEQ and FIBT) have launched on Nasdaq with an initial 95% equities / 5% Bitcoin allocation and systematic reinvestment of stock dividends into Bitcoin.

    富兰克林邓普顿拟推两只「股息再投资比特币」ETF

    Franklin Templeton filed Form 8-A with the SEC for two Nasdaq-listed index ETFs (FBEQ, FIBT) that hold roughly 95% US equities and 5% bitcoin exposure, automatically reinvesting stock dividends into bitcoin-linked instruments.
