Current outcome

Metaplanet is issuing ~¥52B (~$328M) of 2031-maturity zero-coupon convertible bonds, with ~$149M of net proceeds to repay Bitcoin-collateralized loans and ~$179M for share buybacks under a new up to $380M repurchase program; no BTC holdings will be sold, and its loan-to-BTCNAV ratio of ~17.7% remains above its 10% target. Additionally, CEO Simon Gerovich will lend up to 55 million personal shares at 0.75% annual interest to optimize bond issuance conditions; the August 18 announced investment in Super League Enterprise (SLE) is expected to complete soon, after which SLE plans to rename to Superplanet, Inc.
