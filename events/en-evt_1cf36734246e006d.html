Current outcome

The stolen NEAR Intents funds have been fully recovered; the team says it identified and contacted the responsible party within 24 hours of the attack and credited the AI security layer SHIELD. NEAR says it will continue strengthening its security systems, and the incident has moved into post-recovery wrap-up.
