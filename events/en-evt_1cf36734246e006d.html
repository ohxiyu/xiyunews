Current outcome

After confirming an exploit of over $3.8M through a flaw in its Omni deposit/withdrawal infrastructure and contract interactions and patching the vulnerability, NEAR Intents has now halted services, suspended deposits and withdrawals across 11 networks for repairs, and pledged full compensation to affected users.
