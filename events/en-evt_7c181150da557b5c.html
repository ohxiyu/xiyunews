Current outcome

Initia's L1 mainnet and all Rollups remain paused; the co-founder said the exploit likely stemmed from a MoveVM Day 0 vulnerability, the native INIT token was unaffected, and the team is still investigating the impact and further details.

Progress timeline

3 material updates
  1. #0110.23
    Initial2026-10-23 15:40 · publication time

    【Initia 链疑遭无限铸币攻击，攻击者已套现约 260 万美元】

    Security researcher spreekaway flagged that Initia appears to have suffered an unlimited-mint exploit in which multiple tokens were arbitrarily minted, with the attacker cashing out about $2.6M in ETH while still holding roughly $4.54M in iUSD, USDC, AUSD and ETH on Initia.

    Source evidence: foresightnews · theblockbeats

  2. #0210.23
    Response2026-10-23 16:03 · publication time

    Initia因安全事件暂停L1主网及Rollup运行

    Initia halted its L1 mainnet and all Rollups due to the security incident and is coordinating with security teams, validators, and exchanges to assess the impact.

    State after update: Initia has paused its L1 mainnet and all Rollups following the security incident while the team investigates the impact and warns users about unofficial information.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats

  3. #0310.23
    Confirmation2026-10-23 16:25 · publication time

    Initia联创：漏洞攻击涉及MoveVM，原生代币INIT未受影响

    Initia co-founder Zon said the incident likely stemmed from a Day 0 vulnerability in its Cosmos SDK-based MoveVM that allowed extra token minting on Initia L1, while the native INIT token was unaffected; roughly $2.7M in AUSD, ETH and USDC was bridged out before the chain was paused.

    State after update: Initia's L1 mainnet and all Rollups remain paused; the co-founder said the exploit likely stemmed from a MoveVM Day 0 vulnerability, the native INIT token was unaffected, and the team is still investigating the impact and further details.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats
