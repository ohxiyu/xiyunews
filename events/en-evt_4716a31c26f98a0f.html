Current outcome

Following a FinCEN special measure order targeting MEXC Global, users have reported that funds withdrawn from MEXC to centralized exchanges like OKX, Binance, and Gate are facing compliance reviews, deposit holds, or refund reviews. Separately, Bitget Wallet confirmed its card top-up service is not accepting funds identified as originating from MEXC. The reported actions by major exchanges and a wallet service indicate a proactive, risk-based compliance response to a significant U.S. regulatory action, potentially affecting user fund flows and exchange operations even before the FinCEN order takes effect. The FinCEN order has not yet taken effect, and the exchange-related reports are based on individual user accounts rather than official platform-wide announcements. The scope of restrictions and the final disposition of affected funds remain unclear.
