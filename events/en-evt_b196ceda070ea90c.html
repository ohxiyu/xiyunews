Current outcome

Bloomberg reported that Binance.US is seeking to raise up to $100 million in a private funding round at a target valuation of roughly $1 billion, in order to expand its US operations. World Liberty Financial, a crypto venture linked to the Trump family, was made aware of the fundraising but is not participating. The reported round would give the US affiliate fresh capital to expand at a time when it has lost significant market share following Binance's 2023 settlement with US authorities. The raise is being sought, not completed, and no terms have been disclosed. Binance.US is exploring expansion into prediction markets, seeking to restore certain state money transmitter licenses, and considering external sources of trading liquidity that could potentially include Binance's global exchange, though no final decision has been made.
