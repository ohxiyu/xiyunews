Current outcome

Perplexity open-sourced pplx-embed-v2-late embedding models in 0.6B and 9B sizes under MIT license, featuring shared vector spaces that let developers build an index with the 9B model and query it with the 0.6B model, plus token-level 128-dim vectors for fine-grained matching and OCR-free image/PDF page retrieval.
