Current outcome

MetaMask disclosed a security incident affecting part of its infrastructure and is preemptively exiting roughly 17,000 validators holding about 523,000 ETH; on-chain researcher Kaden said that out of 19 validators receiving block rewards, 18 rewards were not sent to the intended fee recipient and instead went to an address funded by Tornado Cash, with the attacker actually obtaining about 0.36 ETH; about 821 potentially affected validators have not yet exited, and compromised signing keys could pose residual slashing risk; MetaMask said its staking service is non-custodial and it does not control users’ withdrawal keys, but has not disclosed the cause, full scope, or whether signing keys were exposed; new information shows Ethereum’s validator exit queue swelled to 773,447 ETH, the largest backlog since December 2025, implying about a 13-day 10-hour exit wait plus a 7.6-day sweep, with an entry queue of about 27 days; Lido previously said the full exit, withdrawal and re-entry cycle could take up to about 45 days.
