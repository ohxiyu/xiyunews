  2. #0210.05
    Aftermath06:40 UTC · publication time

    Ethereum investors are stuck in a two-week staking exit line. Here's why.

    Ethereum’s validator exit queue became the longest of 2026 after MetaMask’s precautionary validator exits: about 851,000 ETH on Oct. 2, with about 786,000 ETH still queued on Oct. 5 and an estimated nearly 14-day wait; Lido expects the affected ETH to be gradually restaked over a process that could take up to about 45 days, with the last affected MetaMask validators expected to stop staking by Oct. 7. The entry queue separately fell to about 1.5 million ETH/25 days, down from about 2 million ETH/35 days in early September.

    State after update: The precautionary validator exits tied to MetaMask’s infrastructure security incident pushed Ethereum’s exit queue to a 2026 peak of about 851,000 ETH on Oct. 2; about 786,000 ETH remained queued on Oct. 5 with an estimated wait of nearly 14 days. Lido expects the affected ETH to be gradually restaked over a process that could take up to about 45 days, with the last affected MetaMask validators expected to stop staking by Oct. 7. MetaMask said it found no indication wallets or customer funds were affected, and client withdrawal keys remain outside its control.

    Source evidence: CoinDesk

  3. #0310.03
    Response00:36 UTC · publication time

    Joseph Lubin: No Indication MetaMask Wallets or Customer Funds Were Affected by

    Consensys founder Joseph Lubin publicly responded for the first time, saying there is no indication that MetaMask wallets or customer funds were affected by the infrastructure security incident; as a precaution, his team and partners rotated validator keys, and he emphasized that validator and withdrawal keys are separate and the team does not hold clients' withdrawal keys, preventing unauthorized transfers of staked ETH.

    State after update: Consensys founder Joseph Lubin publicly stated that MetaMask wallets and customer funds were not affected by the infrastructure security incident; validator keys have been rotated as a precaution, and withdrawal keys remain separately held by clients, so no unauthorized transfers of staked ETH occurred.

    Source evidence: wublockchainenglish · foresightnews

  4. #0410.01
    Escalation13:40 UTC · publication time

    MetaMask security scare pushes Ethereum validator exits to a nine-month high

    Ethereum’s validator exit queue reached 773,447 ETH, the largest backlog since December 2025, implying about a 13-day 10-hour exit wait plus a 7.6-day sweep; the entry queue is about 27 days.

    State after update: MetaMask disclosed a security incident affecting part of its infrastructure and is preemptively exiting roughly 17,000 validators holding about 523,000 ETH; on-chain researcher Kaden said that out of 19 validators receiving block rewards, 18 rewards were not sent to the intended fee recipient and instead went to an address funded by Tornado Cash, with the attacker actually obtaining about 0.36 ETH; about 821 potentially affected validators have not yet exited, and compromised signing keys could pose residual slashing risk; MetaMask said its staking service is non-custodial and it does not control users’ withdrawal keys, but has not disclosed the cause, full scope, or whether signing keys were exposed; new information shows Ethereum’s validator exit queue swelled to 773,447 ETH, the largest backlog since December 2025, implying about a 13-day 10-hour exit wait plus a 7.6-day sweep, with an entry queue of about 27 days; Lido previously said the full exit, withdrawal and re-entry cycle could take up to about 45 days.

    Source evidence: CryptoSlate
