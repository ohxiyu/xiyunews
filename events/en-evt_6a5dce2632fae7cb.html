Current outcome

Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group will take roughly a 5% stake in SBI Holdings and Startale Group's joint venture SBI Startale Marketing to drive planning and blockchain financial product development for the trust-type yen stablecoin JPYSC, which SBI Shinsei Trust Bank issues and SBI VC Trade distributes, with outstanding issuance around ¥20.8 billion as of October 30.
