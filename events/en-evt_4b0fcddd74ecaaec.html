Current outcome

Standard Chartered announced the launch of a tokenized deposit solution for corporate clients in Hong Kong, making the city the first market in its global network to offer the service, with plans to extend it to more markets. Built on the bank's own blockchain infrastructure and developed with support from the HKMA's distributed ledger technology regulatory incubator, the solution relies on EnsembleTX to let tokenized deposits move between participating banks in real time, with interbank settlement in central bank money — including wholesale CBDC — on a 24/7 basis. The service is aimed at corporate clients' intra-group liquidity management and digital treasury and investment workflows, allowing tokenized deposit transfers among participating banks to be settled in central bank money around the clock. BlockBeats reported on November 2, citing CoinDesk, that early partners include China AMC, Fireblocks and HashKey. Hong Kong is currently the only market where the bank offers the service.
