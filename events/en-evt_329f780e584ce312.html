Current outcome

ENS founder Nick Johnson has submitted a draft executable proposal to the ENS DAO forum to activate ENSv2 on Ethereum mainnet; it would freeze new v1 .eth registrations, migrate resolution to v2, shorten the grace period from 90 to 28 days, and transfer all v2 contract admin control to the DAO, with existing .eth holders needing no action; the proposal remains in draft stage.
