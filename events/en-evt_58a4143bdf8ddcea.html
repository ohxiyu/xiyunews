Current outcome

ESMA has issued an opinion requiring EU crypto firms to cease services involving non-compliant stablecoins by January 8, 2027, with national regulators expected to enforce; limited temporary services are allowed for clients to exit positions.

    ESMA Proposes MiCA Rules for DeFi Gateways, Staking and Lending

    ESMA proposed MiCA rules that would require licensing for DeFi gateways, staking, and lending services and bar crypto firms from offering MiCA-licensable services involving noncompliant stablecoins.

    ESMA's September 30, 2026 MiCA review response further proposes extending non-compliant stablecoin restrictions to custody and transfer services, potentially removing licensed-custodian access for existing holders after trading pairs are delisted.

    【ESMA 要求欧盟加密企业 3 个月内停止非合规稳定币服务】

    ESMA issued an opinion requiring EU crypto firms to cease services involving non-compliant stablecoins within three months, setting a January 8, 2027 deadline for national regulators to wind down remaining exposures; the opinion expands January 2025 guidance to cover all MiCA-regulated crypto services including trading platforms, exchange, order execution, custody, transfer, investment advice and portfolio management.

    State after update: ESMA has issued an opinion requiring EU crypto firms to cease services involving non-compliant stablecoins by January 8, 2027, with national regulators expected to enforce; limited temporary services are allowed for clients to exit positions.
