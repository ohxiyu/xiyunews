Current outcome

ESMA issued an opinion requiring national regulators to force MiCA-authorized firms to remediate EU clients' exposure to non-compliant stablecoins within three months, with only narrowly supervised liquidation, conversion, withdrawal, transfer or safekeeping allowed in the interim.
