Current outcome

Standard Chartered launched direct Bitcoin and Ethereum trading for its affluent UAE clients after receiving a Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority license in Dubai, offering buy/sell, custody and settlement through existing bank accounts in a closed-loop model built with Zodia Markets.

    Standard Chartered expands Bitcoin and Ether trading to professional investors in UAE

    Standard Chartered expanded its spot Bitcoin and Ether trading in the UAE to professional investors, allowing eligible clients to trade within existing bank accounts; eligibility requires at least AED 4 million (~$1.1M) in net assets, and the bank plans to broaden access further.

    State after update: Standard Chartered now offers spot Bitcoin and Ether trading in the UAE to both institutional clients and professional investors, with plans to expand to more market participants.
