Current outcome

Ripple's xrpld 3.4.1 disclosure details a critical payment-engine integer overflow that could have minted spendable XRP from thin air (patched 25 September, no exploitation found) and a batch-transaction encapsulation validation flaw addressed by the fixBatchV1_2 amendment, activated on mainnet on 9 October.
