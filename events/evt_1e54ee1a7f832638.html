    瑞士FINMA批准该国首只面向零售投资者的现货比特币ETF

    Switzerland's FINMA approved the country's first spot Bitcoin ETF for retail investors, using a regulated investment-fund structure with stricter custody, operational-risk and disclosure requirements instead of the debt-security format used by existing Swiss Bitcoin ETPs.
