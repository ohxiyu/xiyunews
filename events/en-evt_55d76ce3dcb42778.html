Current outcome

On its Q3 earnings call, Circle said it is developing "Arc Sectors" for its Arc chain — dedicated lanes for different types of financial activity, including a privacy sector for institutions with selective confidentiality and disclosure, a payments sector targeting more than 100,000 transactions per second, and an AI Agent sector with identity verification and audit trails. Circle expects AI agents to be discoverable, hireable, payable and trusted on Arc by the first quarter of 2027. The disclosure pairs a concrete infrastructure roadmap with strong network metrics: Circle Payments Network annualized payment volume exceeded $58 billion, up more than 150% from $23 billion on July 31, with 205 financial institutions having joined, and Circle expects CPN to contribute meaningful revenue in 2027. As of late October, about $1.5 billion in USDC, cirBTC and EURC was held on Arc, with over $30 billion in USDC having flowed through the chain since launch at roughly 500,000 daily transactions. In Q3, USDC accounted for 79% of stablecoin on-chain transaction volume versus 66% a year earlier, while USDC mint and redeem totaled a record $208 billion, up 42% year over year.
