Current outcome

China's Commerce and Technology ministries proposed placing 23 AI technologies — including frontier large models, agents, and compute clusters above 10^26 training FLOPs or 2T parameters — under export controls covering weights, source code, and training techniques, while explicitly exempting already-public open-source work and continuing to permit overseas API and app access, with pre-export registration requirements effective October 16 and the catalogue open for comment until November 15.
