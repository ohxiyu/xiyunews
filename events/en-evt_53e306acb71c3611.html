Current outcome

A New York jury found security consultant Jonathan Spalletta guilty of the 2021 Uranium Finance attacks that drained roughly $55 million, exposing him to up to 20 years in prison on Tornado Cash-linked money-laundering charges, with sentencing scheduled for February 16 and authorities having seized over $31 million in crypto plus rare collectibles and artifacts.
