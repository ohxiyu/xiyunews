Current outcome

The European Banking Authority classified Circle's USDC as a 'significant e-money token' under MiCA, placing the $73 billion stablecoin under joint EU and French supervisory oversight with heightened compliance requirements.

Progress timeline

2 material updates
  1. #0110.05
    Initial2026-10-05 12:10 · publication time

    Circle can delay European USDC redemptions if reserves cannot cross borders

    Circle's European USDC redemption policy permits temporary delays if reserves cannot move between its French and U.S. issuers, creating a potential cash-access boundary for EEA holders despite their par-value redemption right.

    Source evidence: CryptoSlate

  2. #0210.14
    Escalation2026-10-14 16:27 · publication time

    USDC被列为欧盟首个「重要电子货币代币」

    The European Banking Authority designated Circle-issued USDC as the EU's first 'significant' e-money token under MiCA, transferring direct supervision of its capital, reserves, liquidity and recovery/redemption plans to the EBA from France's ACPR starting 12 November 2026, and paired with a new EBA–NYDFS cooperation memorandum.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats · Cointelegraph · theblockbeats
