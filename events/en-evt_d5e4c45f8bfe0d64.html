Current outcome

Standard Chartered signed a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of crypto custodian Zodia Custody, folding its regulated custody business into the bank's financing and securities services division while spinning its institutional digital asset infrastructure platform into a separate SC Ventures entity, Zodia Solutions, pending regulatory approval.
