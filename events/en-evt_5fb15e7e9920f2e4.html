Current outcome

The CFTC is investigating former Illinois Republican congressman Adam Kinzinger over trades on a Kalshi account between December 2024 and January 2025, including a bet on whether he would receive a presidential pardon — a bet he won hours before Biden's pre-inauguration clemency grant, drawing scrutiny over insider trading rules on prediction markets.
