Current outcome

Mirendil, founded by former Anthropic/DeepMind researchers with a $200M seed round, released its first model Fern 0.1 with a 10M-token context window aimed at AI R&D workloads, following a disclosed experiment in which a single researcher and an AI agent pretrained the underlying base model from scratch in 3.5 months for under $10M, with the agent writing over 99% of the code.
