Current outcome

Microsoft is offering voluntary severance to about 60,000 employees (~27%) across parts of the US, Canada, UK and India, including Copilot and AI experiences teams but excluding some datacenter, sales and marketing roles; it is the company's second such program this year, with employees deciding by early December and leaving by year-end.
