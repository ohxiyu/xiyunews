    Escalation17:09 UTC · publication time

    OpenAI发布GPT-6 Cyber：专攻网络安全漏洞，风险评级升至「关键」

    OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber to a small set of vetted customers, raised its cybersecurity risk rating from High to Critical, disclosed findings of 81 high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities and working exploits for 948 Linux kernel vulnerabilities, and made it available to select customers in the Daybreak Red tier.

    State after update: GPT-6 Cyber has been released to vetted security customers, its risk rating has been raised to Critical, and it has demonstrated vulnerability discovery and exploit development; OpenAI plans broader access to approved customers in the coming weeks and is also making an earlier GPT-5.6 Cyber model available.

    Initial09:00 UTC · publication time

    GPT‑6 Cyber: Advancing the frontier of aligned cybersecurity agents

    OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Cyber, a cybersecurity-focused model built to find exploitable vulnerabilities and develop fixes while staying within authorized scope.
