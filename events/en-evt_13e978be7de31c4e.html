Current outcome

Anthropic has announced a Public Benefit Fund with a 5% share commitment, but the commitment remains subject to stockholder approval and is not confirmed as completed; the fund is a wholly owned LLC with a 2% share ratchet per 50% valuation increase and is barred from political lobbying or donations; including co-founder and employee pledges, roughly 20% of shares are intended for public causes. The company is also preparing an IPO as soon as November.
