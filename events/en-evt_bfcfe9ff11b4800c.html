Current outcome

Tether-backed Utexo has obtained a commercial license to issue USDT on Bitcoin and plans to launch native USDT this month using the RGB protocol and Bitcoin's UTXO model, supporting private transfers, native BTC-USDT swaps and BTC-collateralized lending, with Lightning Network expansion to follow.

    Tether支持的Utexo计划本月在比特币网络发行USDT
