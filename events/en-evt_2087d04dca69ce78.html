  1. #0111.02
    Confirmation22:14 UTC · publication time

    Anthropic warns that it’s very close to RSI capability, aka self-building AI

    Anthropic publicly cautioned that it is approaching recursive self-improvement (RSI) — the threshold at which AI systems become good enough at training their own successors to drive a sharp acceleration in capabilities — framing the moment as a critical safety juncture. Anthropic's framing places RSI at the center of the debate over how quickly frontier labs should deploy increasingly capable systems and how regulators should structure oversight of that process. The statement is an assessment of proximity rather than a demonstrated capability jump or a disclosed safety incident. RSI is described as the point where AI becomes so effective at training itself that capability gains accelerate dramatically.
