Current outcome

Ethereum's Glamsterdam upgrade activates on Sepolia testnet on October 6, raising the block gas limit from ~60M to 200M; validators need clients like Prysm 7.2.1 with updated gas limit settings to automatically propose larger blocks. The 200M gas setting currently applies only to Sepolia testnet; Glamsterdam is not yet activated on Ethereum mainnet.
