Current outcome

Stripe is integrating Privy into Treasury, Link and Connect, and building a unified API for holding yield-bearing stablecoin balances, fiat on/off-ramps and card-coordinated spending; Link balances are launching soon.

    Confirmation2026-10-15 15:58 · publication time

    Stripe联创：收购Privy后其收入增至此前约9倍，钱包资产规模增至约25倍

    Stripe co-founder Patrick Collison said Privy's wallet assets have grown ~25x, monthly active developers ~3x, and revenue ~9x since acquisition, with Stripe now deeply integrating Privy into Treasury, Link and Connect and building a unified API for stablecoin balances, yield, fiat on/off-ramps, card spending and AI-agent transactions processing nearly $25B monthly.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats

    Stripe partnered with Plasma to launch multi-currency stablecoin cards that let users spend directly from local-currency or stablecoin balances across 30+ markets, with plans to reach 100+ countries by 2027.

    Stripe is taking its stablecoin card business global, targeting coverage in more than 100 countries by the end of the year, with existing clients including Kraken, Ramp and Morse, and Privy co-founder Henri Stern now overseeing Stripe's stablecoin and crypto operations.
