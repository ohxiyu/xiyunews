Current outcome

79th Vault disclosed that a private-key compromise on Oct 7 let an attacker drain roughly $12.5M in 79AU tokens on BNB Chain, and said it has fixed the permissions, identified the attacker address, and opened a white-hat settlement negotiation the attacker has answered while pursuing asset recovery with exchanges and security firms.
