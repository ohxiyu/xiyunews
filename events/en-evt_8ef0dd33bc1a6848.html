Current outcome

Scammers operated a counterfeit GIWA blockchain — cloned RPC, bridge, and Chain ID 9134 for an L2 mainnet that had never launched — and drained 766 of the 767 ETH deposited by 1,333 wallets, about $2 million, with 177 ETH since sent to Tornado Cash and 589 ETH still sitting in four wallets.
