Current outcome

Meituan released LongCat-2.5 with 1.6T total / ~48B active parameters and 1M-token context, reporting Terminal Bench v2.1 rising from 70.8% to 88.4% (near Claude Opus 5's 89.1%) and v4.0 from 3.0% to 38.5%, now available via online demo and API at 2 yuan per million uncached input tokens and 8 yuan per million output tokens, with weights not open-sourced.
