Current outcome

Metaplanet plans to issue ¥52B zero-coupon convertible bonds due 2031, with up to ¥28.3B for share buybacks (including up to ¥25.3B via off-exchange block for ~7.1% of shares) and ~¥23.6B to repay credit lines and release pledged bitcoin; no proceeds directly for additional BTC purchases.

    Metaplanet发行520亿日元零息可转债，用于偿还比特币担保借款及回购股份

    Metaplanet announced a ¥52B zero-coupon convertible bond maturing October 2031, using ¥23.6B to repay bitcoin-backed borrowings and up to ¥28.3B for share buybacks, with no proceeds going directly to additional BTC purchases.

    Metaplanet拟发行520亿日元零息可转债，部分用于偿还信贷并释放抵押比特币

    Added details: of the up to ¥28.3B buyback, up to ¥25.3B will be via off-exchange block trade for up to 96,934,800 shares (~7.1% of outstanding); ~¥23.6B to partially repay credit lines and release pledged bitcoin; CEO states conversion price above issuance price and 1.0x mNAV, aiming to replace secured interest-bearing borrowings with unsecured non-interest debt, lower interest costs, and increase flexibility for future BTC purchases.
