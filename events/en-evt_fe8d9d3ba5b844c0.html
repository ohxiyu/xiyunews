Current outcome

France's National Assembly Finance Committee approved an amendment to the 2027 budget bill that would tax crypto-to-stablecoin swaps as capital gains events starting January 1, 2027, and extend the exit tax on unrealized gains to crypto investors with household holdings above €800,000 relocating abroad.
