Current outcome

France's full National Assembly has passed by a single vote a 2027 budget amendment removing tax deferral for crypto-to-MiCA-stablecoin conversions, treating them as taxable disposals from January 1, 2027; the finance committee had also adopted a 31.4% rate, self-custody reporting above €100,000, and a crypto exit tax, while rejecting the revenue section; the measures remain in first reading, are not law, and still require Senate review, with votes expected Oct. 20 and Nov. 17.

    France Considers 31.4% Tax on Crypto-to-MiCA Stablecoin Conversions From 2027

    Adds the specific 31.4% capital gains tax rate on crypto-to-MiCA-regulated-stablecoin conversions in France's 2027 budget amendment; the finance committee rejected the government's revenue section by 31 votes to 3; a revenue vote is expected Oct. 20; no measure is law yet.

    State after update: France's National Assembly Finance Committee has adopted amendments including a 31.4% crypto-to-MiCA-stablecoin tax, self-custody wallet reporting above €100,000, and a crypto exit tax, then rejected the budget's revenue section 31-3; the amendments return to the full National Assembly, with a revenue vote expected Oct. 20 and no measure yet law.

    Proposed French tax on regulated stablecoins could drive crypto traders offshore

    New facts: a separate Oct. 7 amendment backed by the finance committee would allow crypto losses to be carried forward up to 10 years; Chainalysis' France CEO warned the 31.4% regulated-stablecoin tax could push trading toward unregulated offshore stablecoins; lawmakers have until Dec. 10 to pass the 2027 budget.

    State after update: France's National Assembly finance committee has adopted multiple crypto tax amendments in the 2027 budget, including a 31.4% capital gains tax on crypto-to-MiCA-stablecoin swaps, annual reporting for self-custody wallets holding €100,000 or more, an expanded exit tax, and 10-year crypto loss carryforwards; it then rejected the budget's revenue section 31-3. The amendments must be resubmitted to the full National Assembly, a revenue vote is expected Oct. 20, and the budget must pass by Dec. 10; no measure is yet law.
