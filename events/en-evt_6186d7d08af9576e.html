Current outcome

Marvell's all-stock acquisition of Lumentum (~$115B enterprise value, 4.500-share exchange ratio, ~23.9% premium, 67/33 ownership split) is announced but not closed, targeted for H1 2028 pending shareholder and regulatory approvals; premarket shares of both companies turned lower after the announcement.
