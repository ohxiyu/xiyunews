Current outcome

The $60 billion AI chip financing package has entered syndication, led by Bank of America, Citigroup and Morgan Stanley distributing to other banks; the ~$42 billion Broadcom-backed senior secured loans have launched syndication, while the $18 billion subordinated debt will be introduced later, with Blackstone committing about $9 billion of it.
