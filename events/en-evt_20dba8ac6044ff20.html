Current outcome

Project North Star financing remains in syndication; Blackstone has warned investors that Anthropic may receive a junk-grade rather than investment-grade rating after IPO, potentially limiting its funding sources and raising borrowing costs for its $800 billion compute buildout. More than $42 billion in senior loans are Broadcom-backed; the $18 billion subordinated debt has been rated BB+ by KBRA and BB by Morningstar DBRS; Blackstone funds invested $9 billion of it at a yield above 10%; Fitch, Moody's and S&P have not yet formally assigned Anthropic a credit rating.
