目前结论

Canada's draft stablecoin regulations would require issuers to hold one-to-one reserves with Canadian custodians, redeem within three business days for a fee of no more than 1%, and refrain from paying yield, backed by penalties up to C$10 million and Bank of Canada oversight, with a 60-day comment period ahead of a targeted fall 2027 effective date.
