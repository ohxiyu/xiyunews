Current outcome

The Independent Community Bankers of America sued the OCC to overturn its national trust bank rule and Interpretive Letter 1176, challenging the federal charter pathway relied on by 13 crypto-linked trust banks and putting stablecoin, payments, and custody plans at risk of restructuring or delay.

    Bankers sue to overturn OCC trust-bank rule used by crypto firms

    The Independent Community Bankers of America sued the OCC to overturn its national trust bank rule and Interpretive Letter 1176, challenging the federal charter pathway relied on by 13 crypto-linked trust banks and putting stablecoin, payments, and custody plans at risk of restructuring or delay.
