Current outcome

Beijing police have opened a criminal investigation into CreditEase-related companies, detaining founder Tang Ning (Tang) and other suspects, four months after the firm froze ~RMB 30bn of quasi-fixed-income redemptions for roughly 100,000 clients — a firm whose funds had invested in crypto projects including Circle and Figure.
