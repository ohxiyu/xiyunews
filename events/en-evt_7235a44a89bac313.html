Current outcome

French authorities jointly proposed MiCA revisions that would require crypto asset service providers to vet and continuously monitor DeFi protocols they give clients access to, and to assume liability for certain incidents affecting those clients, while also backing DeFi/smart-contract certification, ESMA oversight of significant CASPs, a lower significance threshold, and revised ART/EMT rules.
