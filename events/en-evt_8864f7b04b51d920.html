Current outcome

JPMorgan Asset Management opened its tokenized money market funds MONY (a 506(c) private fund) and JLTXX (a US-registered government money market fund aimed at GENIUS Act-compliant stablecoin issuers) to qualified US investors on Solana, with subscriptions and redemptions via the Morgan Money liquidity platform and token balances delivered to on-chain addresses.
