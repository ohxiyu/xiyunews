Current outcome

Russia's central bank authorized Sberbank (alongside VTB and other firms) as a licensed crypto exchange operator and custodian, with the lender preparing to offer BTC, ETH and USDT trading through its platforms starting Dec. 1, 2026.

    Russia clears first crypto exchanges, custodians under new law

    The Bank of Russia published its first registers of licensed crypto exchange operators (T-Invest Lab, Zefir, Sistema-Crypto, VTB) and digital custodians (Sberbank, Atomyze, Voltari, Cloud Infrastructure, VTB), with Sberbank planning to launch Bitcoin, Ether and USDT products on Dec. 1.
