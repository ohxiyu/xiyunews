Current outcome

The Bank of Russia published its first registers of licensed crypto exchange operators (T-Invest Lab, Zefir, Sistema-Crypto, VTB) and digital custodians (Sberbank, Atomyze, Voltari, Cloud Infrastructure, VTB), with Sberbank planning to launch Bitcoin, Ether and USDT products on Dec. 1.
