Current outcome

Solana liquidity protocol Orca and on-chain lending protocol Loopscale have completed a merger and will operate under the new name Formation, led by Loopscale co-founder Luke Truitt as CEO, combining trading/liquidity infrastructure with credit and custody infrastructure to offer assets an end-to-end path from issuance to financing and liquidity.
