Current outcome

NYSE and Blockchain.com have signed a memorandum of understanding to explore giving crypto-native users access to US-listed stocks and ETFs on NYSE's planned 24/7 tokenized securities venue, with a two-way market-data arrangement; regulatory approval, launch timing, eligible markets and available securities remain unresolved.
