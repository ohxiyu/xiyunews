Current outcome

At Ripple's Swell conference, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent argued that US leadership in digital assets reinforces dollar dominance and that dollar-backed stablecoins are a growing source of Treasury demand, while pledging to use Treasury's convening power with the SEC and CFTC to advance administrative crypto rulemaking and guardrails against debanking after Congress failed to pass the Clarity Act.
