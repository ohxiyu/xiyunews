Current outcome

Brazilian exchange Mercado Bitcoin suspended deposits and withdrawals on Monday after detecting a security incident in one of its systems in the early hours of the morning. Blockchain security firm Hypernative flagged more than $40 million in suspicious transfers across at least 10 blockchains, while Arkham analyst Emmett Gallic put the losses at over $46 million and said the attacker is still sweeping old deposit addresses and selling off memecoins and altcoins. The exchange says no customer funds or data were affected, but the platform-wide suspension of deposits and withdrawals blocks all users from moving assets while funds are still being traced on-chain. Tether has frozen more than 4.5 million USDT tied to the incident, according to CEO Paolo Ardoino. Mercado Bitcoin published attacker destination addresses on five networks — EVM chains, Bitcoin, Solana, Stellar and Tron — saying they were released so others can identify and track the funds, and it has not confirmed the $40 million-plus figure or disclosed how the attackers gained access. Hypernative's preliminary analysis spans at least 10 blockchains, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and Tron.
