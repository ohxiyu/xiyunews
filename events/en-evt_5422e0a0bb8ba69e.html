Current outcome

xAI updated its developer documentation to say Grok API outputs for Grok 4.7/4.6/4.5/4.3 and the Grok 4.20 models will carry invisible text watermarks by default worldwide starting November 7, including structured outputs and tool-call parameters, to satisfy EU AI Act transparency rules; developers can disable it per model only by confirming a legal exemption and assuming disclosure responsibility.
