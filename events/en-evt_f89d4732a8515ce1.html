Current outcome

The U.S. SEC proposed a new regulatory framework governing crypto asset custody by investment advisers and regulated funds, establishing a compliance path under pre-internet rules, permitting state trust companies to serve as custodians, and allowing adviser self-custody in limited cases where no qualified custodian exists.
