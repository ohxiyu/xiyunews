Current outcome

Brooklyn prosecutors and the FBI have seized or disrupted 112 servers and 241 websites tied to a Russian-language crypto investment scam that allegedly stole tens of millions from Russian-speaking U.S. victims; about $300,000 has been seized, no arrests were announced, and investigators targeted backend servers to prevent replacement sites.
