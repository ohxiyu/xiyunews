Current outcome

AMD announced it is acquiring World Labs, the AI research lab co-founded by Dr. Fei-Fei Li, in an all-stock deal worth approximately $8.2 billion. Dr. Fei-Fei Li will become AMD's chief scientist. World Labs launched in 2024 and had reached a $1 billion valuation within months. The purchase is AMD's second-largest acquisition on record and extends the chipmaker's AI portfolio beyond hardware into world-model research. The consideration is entirely in AMD stock. World Labs' first commercial product is a world generation model, and the company has introduced the Marble world model. Bloomberg estimates that upon completion of the deal, Fei-Fei Li's net worth will rise to at least $1.6 billion; she holds about 19% of World Labs and became a billionaire after the company's February funding round valued it above $5 billion; World Labs' research team and models will receive AMD compute support.

    AMD announced it is acquiring World Labs, the AI research lab co-founded by Dr. Fei-Fei Li, in an all-stock deal worth approximately $8.2 billion. World Labs launched in 2024 and had reached a $1 billion valuation within months of founding. The purchase is AMD's second-largest acquisition on record and extends the chipmaker's AI portfolio beyond hardware into world-model and foundation-model research, where it would compete more directly with Nvidia. AMD had previously invested in World Labs before the deal. The consideration is entirely in AMD stock rather than cash. World Labs' first commercial product is a world generation model, and the company has introduced the Marble world model, initially offered as a limited-access beta preview.
