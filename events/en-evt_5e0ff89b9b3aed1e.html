Current outcome

XRP Ledger's decade-old payment-engine flaw was discovered by an AI agent, could have minted ~18 trillion XRP, and was emergency-patched by RippleX outside the usual amendment process; no unauthorized XRP was created and no funds were lost.

Progress timeline

2 material updates
  1. #0110.10
    Initial2026-10-10 04:24 · publication time

    XRP Ledger修复存在近11年重大漏洞，攻击者曾可利用其凭空生成XRP

    XRP Ledger developers disclosed a critical payment-path vulnerability dating to 2015 that could have let attackers mint and spend XRP beyond the 100 billion supply cap, which they reproduced and fixed in xrpld 3.4.1 with no evidence of exploitation in the wild.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats · CoinDesk · foresightnews · theblockbeats · The Defiant

  2. #0210.11
    Aftermath2026-10-11 10:30 · publication time

    AI discovers XRP Ledger flaw that could mint 18 trillion tokens and put $94 billion market at risk

    New disclosure reveals the flaw was found by Veria Labs' AI security agent, could generate ~18 trillion XRP in a single transaction, and prompted RippleX to deploy an emergency fix bypassing standard governance, with a $250,000 bounty; no on-chain exploitation occurred.
