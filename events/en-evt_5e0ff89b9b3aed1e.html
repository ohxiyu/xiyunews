Current outcome

Security researchers disclosed a flaw dating to 2015 in the XRP Ledger's internal exchange that could have allowed an attacker to create and spend XRP from nothing, which RippleX fixed in the xrpld 3.4.1 release with no evidence of exploitation on public networks.
