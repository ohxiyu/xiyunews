Current outcome

The XRP Ledger Foundation continued disclosing details of the supply-cap-related bug, explaining it stayed hidden for years because normal ledger parameters never triggered it; the XRP supply cap was not broken and no exploitation occurred.

  5. #0510.12
    Confirmation2026-10-12 00:35 · publication time

    XRP Ledger基金会澄清安全修复疑问：未增发XRP，供应量仍固定

    The XRP Ledger Foundation clarified that the xrpld 3.4.1 fix addressed a rule-bypass vulnerability requiring hundreds of deliberately crafted operations, not XRP inflation; total supply remains fixed at 100 billion, with about 99.986 billion currently circulating. It also said the flaw was responsibly disclosed by Cayden Liao and Veria AI, public binaries were released on September 25, and 3.4.1 later became a required version through a normal amendment vote with over 80% support sustained for two weeks.

    State after update: The old XRP Ledger payment-engine vulnerability was fixed in xrpld 3.4.1; the Foundation confirmed the fix did not involve inflation and XRP supply remains fixed at 100 billion. The flaw was discovered and responsibly disclosed by Cayden Liao and Veria AI, the fix was distributed via emergency coordination and later ratified through the normal amendment vote, with no unauthorized XRP minting or fund loss.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats

  6. #0610.12
    Response2026-10-12 01:21 · publication time

    XRP Ledger defends emergency patch for unlimited XRP bug

    The XRP Ledger Foundation and developers publicly defended the Sept. 25 emergency patch, saying waiting for the usual two-week validator vote would have exposed the flaw; they rejected centralization claims, stressing the decision was joint among the Foundation, RippleX, core developers and validators, and that 3.4.1 later became required via a normal amendment vote with over 80% support sustained for two weeks.

    State after update: The old XRP Ledger payment-engine vulnerability is fixed in xrpld 3.4.1, which became a required version through a normal amendment vote; the Foundation and developers are publicly responding to questions about the emergency patch bypassing normal governance and XRPL's decentralization, with no unauthorized XRP minting or fund loss confirmed.
