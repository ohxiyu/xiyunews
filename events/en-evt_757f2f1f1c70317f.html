Ahead of the US midterms, CNN, CBS News, Dow Jones, and CNBC have struck commercial partnerships with prediction markets Kalshi and Polymarket, whose election-related trading volume totals about $1.5 billion, even as multiple states sue the platforms and some media critics question objectivity.

    Kalshi、Polymarket中期选举前密集与CNN、CNBC等媒体合作

    Ahead of the November 3 US midterms, CNN, CBS News, Dow Jones and CNBC struck commercial deals with prediction markets Kalshi and Polymarket to cite election trading odds in coverage — including a CNBC revenue-share and minority equity investment in Kalshi and a WSJ odds column powered by Polymarket — with roughly $1.5B traded across the two platforms' election markets.
