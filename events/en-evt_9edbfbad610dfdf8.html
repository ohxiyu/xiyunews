Current outcome

Balancer's community approved BIP-928 for an orderly wind-down and rejected the BIP-929 fork, moving the protocol into shutdown execution: pools run until Oct 30, the V3 Vault pauses Nov 30, and BAL redemption against DAO treasury assets opens from late May 2027.

    Balancer proposes winding down protocol and distributing treasury to BAL holders

    Balancer has proposed winding down the protocol and distributing its treasury to BAL holders, six months after Balancer Labs shut down operations following a 2025 exploit that drained $128 million.

  2. #0209.30

    Balancer社区通过有序关停提案，分叉方案未获通过

    BAL holders approved the orderly wind-down proposal BIP-928 while the fork proposal BIP-929 was rejected; pools run normally until Oct 30 (partners may apply to extend certain V3 pools to Nov 30), withdrawals remain available throughout, the V3 Vault pauses on Nov 30, and from late May 2027 holders can burn BAL for a pro-rata share of DAO treasury assets.
