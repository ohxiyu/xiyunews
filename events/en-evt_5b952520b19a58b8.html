Current outcome

Balancer's community approved BIP-928 for an orderly wind-down and rejected the BIP-929 fork, moving the protocol into shutdown execution: pools run until Oct 30, the V3 Vault pauses Nov 30, and BAL redemption against DAO treasury assets opens from late May 2027.
