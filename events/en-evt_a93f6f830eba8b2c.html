Current outcome

Polymarket says it has fixed the supply-chain compromise and restored service, and has promised full compensation to affected traders; actual compensation progress remains subject to official follow-up announcements.

    Polymarket users reported unauthorized fund transfers and hundreds of thousands of dollars in losses, prompting the platform to enter cancel-only mode, halt relayer actions, and open an investigation into a suspected frontend compromise.

    Confirmation2026-10-29 18:53 · publication time

    Polymarket Pauses Trading as Prediction Market Investigates Security Incident

    Polymarket confirmed a third-party provider was compromised, referred the matter to law enforcement, and said affected users will be made whole; researchers linked a DebugBear-hosted wallet-draining script to a suspected attacker wallet holding about $2.65 million in POL.

    State after update: Polymarket remains in cancel-only mode with relayer actions paused while investigating a confirmed third-party provider compromise; law enforcement has been notified, affected users are promised reimbursement, and a suspected attacker wallet and malicious frontend script have been identified.

    Source evidence: Decrypt

    Polymarket's systems were restored and all were reported operational as of 19:05 UTC after trading was temporarily halted and the platform entered cancel-only mode due to a compromised third-party provider.

    State after update: Polymarket's systems were restored and operational after a temporary trading halt and cancel-only mode caused by a compromised third-party provider; impacted users were promised reimbursement, with law enforcement referral in the background.

    Source evidence: Cointelegraph

    Polymarket被盗赔付拟分档执行：10K以下直接补回，大额需签和解免责协议

    Polymarket's reimbursement plan adds tiered execution: losses under $10K are to be restored directly by Oct 30, while larger claimants must sign a release/waiver and, per reports, submit identity details before being made whole.

    State after update: Polymarket's reimbursement after the third-party provider breach has moved into tiered execution: smaller losses are to be restored directly, while larger claims require a release/waiver and reportedly identity details; on-chain analysis shows about 710 wallets and roughly $2.7 million transferred out, after the platform pledged full reimbursement and reported the matter to law enforcement.

    GoPlus attributed the Polymarket international-site user asset theft (~$2.71M across 710 DepositWallets and 731 addresses) to a supply-chain compromise of third-party performance-monitoring vendor DebugBear's RUM script; Polymarket said it fixed the issue, restored service, and promised full compensation to affected traders.

    State after update: Polymarket says it has fixed the supply-chain compromise and restored service, and has promised full compensation to affected traders; actual compensation progress remains subject to official follow-up announcements.
