Current outcome

Polymarket's reimbursement after the third-party provider breach has moved into tiered execution: smaller losses are to be restored directly, while larger claims require a release/waiver and reportedly identity details; on-chain analysis shows about 710 wallets and roughly $2.7 million transferred out, after the platform pledged full reimbursement and reported the matter to law enforcement.

    State after update: Polymarket remains in cancel-only mode with relayer actions paused while investigating a confirmed third-party provider compromise; law enforcement has been notified, affected users are promised reimbursement, and a suspected attacker wallet and malicious frontend script have been identified.
