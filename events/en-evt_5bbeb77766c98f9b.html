Current outcome

The UK has sanctioned Cryptomus, Heleket, TokenSpot, related payment platforms and a Moscow bank over Russia sanctions evasion, adding designations including Xeltox Enterprises, Tsunami Payments, Processing KG and its director, along with internet services sanctions, citing facilitation of Russian sanctions circumvention.

    UK Targets Cryptomus and TokenSpot in New Russia Sanctions Package

    The report adds further specific designations from the same UK Russia sanctions round: Xeltox Enterprises (Canadian owner of Cryptomus), Kyrgyz payment firms TokenSpot and Tsunami Payments, Processing KG (VexPay operator) and its director Ulan Bukabaev; it also notes internet services sanctions and identifies the A7 network as a Kremlin-backed illicit finance network.
