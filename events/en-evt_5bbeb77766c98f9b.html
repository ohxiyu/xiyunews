Current outcome

The UK imposed a new round of Russia-related sanctions covering three crypto trading platforms — Cryptomus, Heleket and TokenSpot — along with payment platforms and a Moscow bank, citing alleged facilitation of sanctions evasion, with TRM Labs reporting TokenSpot moved over $950 million to already-sanctioned venues.
