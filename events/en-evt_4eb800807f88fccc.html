Current outcome

The PHP Foundation has released emergency patches; some government websites in Japan, Europe, and Australia paused or took content offline as a precaution; Cloudflare reports millions of exploitation attempts after disclosure; China's MIIT NVDB has now issued a risk advisory specifying affected version ranges.
