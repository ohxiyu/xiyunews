    State after update: PHP released fixes on Oct 21; OpenAI's retrospective confirms the AI-assisted discovery and two-month coordination, and discloses that an early forum leak forced PHP to issue an early alert and move the patch release up by one day, while Cloudflare observed early attack attempts and some government sites proactively went offline.
