Current outcome

Science Corporation, the neurotech company led by former Neuralink president and co-founder Max Hodak, has implanted its PRIMA vision-restoring chip in its first patient with Stargardt disease. Hodak announced the implant on stage at TechCrunch Disrupt, describing the PRIMA work as part of the company's path toward a novel brain-computer interface (BCI). It is the first human implant of PRIMA in a Stargardt disease patient, a concrete milestone for Science Corporation's neurotech program. Hodak framed the implant as a step along the company's longer-term BCI ambitions. The announcement covers a single patient, and Hodak presented it at TechCrunch Disrupt rather than through a peer-reviewed publication. Science Corporation has separately raised a $230 million Series C round, disclosed in March 2026, as it pushes to bring its brain implant to patients.
