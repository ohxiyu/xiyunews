Current outcome

A trader netted $1.28 million by pushing Hyperliquid's ETH prices through Papertrade, a newly launched exchange offering up to 1,000x leverage, according to the report. The same wallet later lost $450,000 when 30 short positions were liquidated on Saturday night after having been held open for more than three hours. The report frames the episode as a concrete cross-venue price-manipulation vector touching Hyperliquid, one of the largest perpetual futures DEXs, rather than a routine trading loss. The 30 short positions were liquidated on Saturday night, more than three hours after they were opened.
