Current outcome

THORChain treasury solvency has been restored after a user returned the 10 ETH, but trading remains paused across most affected chains while core developers work on fixes and node operators decide whether to re-enable each chain after safety checks.

    Remediation00:21 UTC · publication time

    THORChain：某用户主动退回10枚ETH，金库已恢复偿付能力

    THORChain said the erroneously paid 10 ETH was voluntarily returned by a user and treasury solvency was restored; however, trading remained paused on ten chains—ATOM, BCH, BSC, BTC, DOGE, ETH, LTC, SOL, TRON and ZEC—while AVAX, BASE, RUNE and XRPL stayed active, pending fixes and node-operator safety confirmation before re-enabling.

  2. #0210.31
    Initial17:58 UTC · publication time

    Chainalysis: Ethereum Reorg Triggers THORChain Emergency Pause, Delaying Launder

    Chainalysis reported that an Ethereum reorganization on the morning of October 31 caused THORChain to mistakenly issue a double refund on a 10 ETH deposit, prompting an emergency pause that cut off cross-chain swaps across most of its bridges for all users and inadvertently stalled laundering of funds stolen from Binance.
