Current outcome

Binance Pay is live on Japan's PayPay QR payment network via HIVEX, enabling eligible overseas visitors to pay with crypto at participating merchants while merchants receive yen; Japan residents and certain merchant categories are excluded.

Progress timeline

  1. #0110.01
    Confirmation08:40 UTC · publication time

    Japan’s biggest payment network opens its doors for crypto via Binance Pay

    Added details: the integration runs through the HIVEX cross-border payment network, is restricted to identity-verified overseas visitors and excludes Japan residents; it supports 100+ cryptocurrencies, merchants receive yen, refund amounts can vary with exchange rates and FX spreads, and exclusions apply to some merchant categories.

    State after update: Binance Pay is live on Japan's PayPay QR payment network via HIVEX, enabling eligible overseas visitors to pay with crypto at participating merchants while merchants receive yen; Japan residents and certain merchant categories are excluded.

    Source evidence: CryptoSlate

    Binance Pay now allows users to spend USDT directly at merchants on Japan's PayPay QR payment network, extending stablecoin payments into mainstream Japanese retail.
