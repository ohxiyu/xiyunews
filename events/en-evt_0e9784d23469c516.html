Current outcome

US government-linked wallets have moved Bitcoin on three consecutive nights, including 12,267 BTC on Oct. 8 (~$1.006 billion), of which 9,000 BTC (~$739 million) was traced to Coinbase Prime; earlier transfers included about 8,428 BTC on Oct. 7 and 834 BTC on Oct. 6 to Coinbase Prime. BTC fell to about $82,500 over the period. Whether the assets are for custody or eventual liquidation remains unconfirmed.
