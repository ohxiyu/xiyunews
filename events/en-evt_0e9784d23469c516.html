Current outcome

After earlier routing roughly $670 million into Coinbase Prime, US government-linked addresses moved an additional 12,267.02 BTC (~$1.006 billion), with 6,000 BTC sent to a new address and about 6,267 BTC held in another new address; whether this is for custody or eventual liquidation remains unconfirmed.
