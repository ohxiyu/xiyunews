Current outcome

Anthropic added an automated tuning loop to Claude Code's claude-api Skill that iteratively rewrites prompts, tool descriptions, model choice and reasoning effort against evals while using held-out cases to avoid overfitting, demoed on 44 support tickets where accuracy rose from 78.6% to 90.5% at about one-fifth the cost.
