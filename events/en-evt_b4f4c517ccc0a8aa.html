Current outcome

Indian crypto exchange CoinDCX has acknowledged a new security breach, confirming the incident only after the fact rather than at the time it occurred. Separately, Ireland's central bank issued another penalty against Coinbase's Irish entity, with reports citing a €3.25 million ($3.6 million) fine over safeguarding and outsourcing obligations. The two disclosures land on the same exchange-risk themes — custody security and regulatory compliance — that supervisors have been tightening. Ireland's central bank framed the Coinbase action as customer protection, stating: "This action against Coinbase Ireland underlines the Central Bank's focus on ensuring that customers are at all times protected and that the integrity of the financial system is maintained." The Coinbase penalty reported in the safeguarding and outsourcing case requires approval from Ireland's High Court before taking effect. Delano separately reported a €21.5 million penalty against Coinbase Europe for anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing failures, described as Ireland's first enforcement action in the crypto sector.
