    Mastercard and BVNK Launch 'Global Money Platform' to Merge Fiat and Stablecoins

    Mastercard and BVNK launched the Global Money Platform, a modular infrastructure layer enabling businesses to hold multi-currency fiat and stablecoin balances side by side, pay out via Mastercard Move, and spend stablecoin balances across Mastercard's merchant network, with Nuvei and Marqeta among the first integrators.
