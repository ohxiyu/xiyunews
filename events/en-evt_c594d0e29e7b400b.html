Current outcome

Ledger is investigating reported fund losses tied to Southeast Asian authorized reseller CryptoBilis; Ledger has asked CryptoBilis to suspend sales and shipments and has warned customers who bought devices in the past 90 days. Onchain researchers estimate losses at roughly $72 million to $86 million, but Ledger has not confirmed the loss amount, number of affected customers, or whether devices were compromised, and its investigation is ongoing.
