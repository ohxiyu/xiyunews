Current outcome

The CryptoBilis-linked Ledger wallet drain incident now spans multiple regions, with spyware-tampered devices alleged in Asia, Europe, and South America; Ledger confirmed the October 9 incident affected a limited number of CryptoBilis devices, losses exceed $86 million (Bitquery previously estimated $93.2 million with about $10 million frozen), Ledger continues to investigate and offers no repayment plan to affected buyers; former CryptoBilis CEO says the firm was acquired and he stepped down; a buyer reports a second tampered Ledger device from MediaMarkt, names distributor Aqipa, calls for a recall, and sends the device to Ledger for analysis.
