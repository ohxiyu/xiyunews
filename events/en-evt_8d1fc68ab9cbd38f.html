Current outcome

OKX CEO Star said OKX will help bring perpetual futures to the US regulated market through a structure in which ICE operates a new US trading and clearing venue for 24/7 perps across equities, indices, commodities, FX and crypto, Trade.xyz serves as ICE's exclusive partner, and an ICE-OKX joint venture handles retail distribution.
