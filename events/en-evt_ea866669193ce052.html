Current outcome

The crypto industry has spent roughly $176 million across more than 100 US midterm races this cycle to back friendly candidates, Reuters reported, with the Fairshake super PAC — supported by Coinbase, Ripple and a16z — leading the effort. Of the 58 primary or special election candidates Fairshake backed, 53 advanced, according to the report. The spending is described as part of a multi-year plan to build a bipartisan bloc of congressional allies so the industry can keep advancing its priorities in Washington after Trump leaves office. The strategy focuses on candidates judged to have strong odds of winning, with affiliated PACs running advertisements that often do not mention crypto directly. Reuters framed the effort as preparation for a post-Trump policy environment rather than a single-election push.
