Current outcome

Speaking in a keynote at the Plan ₿ Forum in Lugano, Tether CEO Paolo Ardoino said the company owns approximately 150,000 BTC and serves more than 730 million users, adding about 30 million new wallets per quarter. He cited Tether's Q3 2026 attestation, which showed roughly $122.8 billion in directly held U.S. Treasury bills — $132.4 billion including repurchase agreements — and about $7.95 billion in excess reserves. The remarks make public the approximate scale of Bitcoin held by the largest stablecoin issuer, alongside reserve figures that market participants track when gauging USDT's backing. It was a keynote statement rather than an announcement of a new purchase or a regulatory filing. The figures come from Tether's Q3 2026 attestation, a periodic assurance report rather than a full audit of the company's financial statements. Ardoino also described Tether's wider Independence Stack initiative, covering energy, Bitcoin mining, peer-to-peer communications, self-custody wallets and on-device AI.
