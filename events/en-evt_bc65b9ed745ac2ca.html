Current outcome

Bitcoin Core developer Cory Fields proposed a 'quantum mine' scheme on November 7, which would require anyone moving bitcoins from wallets vulnerable to future quantum attacks to post collateral equal to the coins' value and undergo a waiting period. The proposal represents a novel, concrete defense mechanism against a potential future threat to Bitcoin's security model, aiming to raise the cost for quantum attackers attempting to steal from vulnerable wallets. Legitimate owners can claim their assets and the attacker's collateral by revealing a pre-prepared secret proof of ownership during the waiting period. The proposal also includes a challenge auction mechanism for dormant assets with lost private keys.
