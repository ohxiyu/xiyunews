Current outcome

Meta's equity financing plan has been upsized to $97.5B, or up to about $103B including the greenshoe, comprising $25B of common stock, about $12.5B of Series A depositary shares and up to $60B through an at-the-market program to fund AI investments; Nvidia's previously announced $3B investment and expanded AI collaboration remain in place.

    Meta盘前转涨1.12%，英伟达、AMD和博通有意购买至多50亿美元Meta股票

    Meta launched a roughly $35B dual underwritten equity offering — about $22.5B of Class A common stock via forward sale agreements and about $12.5B of Series A mandatory convertible preferred depositary shares — with Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom reportedly interested in purchasing up to $5B of Meta stock each, lifting META 1.12% in premarket trading.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats

  2. #0210.27
    Escalation2026-10-27 06:11 · publication time

    英伟达将向Meta股权发行投资30亿美元，双方扩大AI基础设施与模型合作

    Nvidia will invest $3 billion in Meta's equity issuance, with the two companies expanding their AI infrastructure and model partnership.

    State after update: Within Meta's roughly $35B equity offering, Nvidia will invest $3B; the companies are also expanding collaboration around the OpenShell reference architecture, Nvidia Sentry, Vera Rubin, Vera CPU, Spectrum-X and BlueField-4 deployments, and deepening work on MUSE models.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats · theblockbeats

  3. #0310.27

    Meta小幅上调股票融资计划至975亿美元，最高融资额或达1030亿美元

    Meta raised its planned stock offering from about $95B to $97.5B, or up to roughly $103B including the greenshoe; it increased the common stock tranche from $22.5B to $25B while retaining about $12.5B of Series A depositary shares and an at-the-market program of up to $60B.

    State after update: Meta's equity financing plan has been upsized to $97.5B, or up to about $103B including the greenshoe, comprising $25B of common stock, about $12.5B of Series A depositary shares and up to $60B through an at-the-market program to fund AI investments; Nvidia's previously announced $3B investment and expanded AI collaboration remain in place.
