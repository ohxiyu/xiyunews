Current outcome

Microsoft announced its third-generation Maia 300 AI chip, positioned as the primary inference system for its own products and services with improved cost efficiency and support for reasoning and agentic workloads, reducing its reliance on Nvidia GPUs; production targeted for 2027 and multi-gigawatt internal deployment planned, with model training continuing on Nvidia and AMD.
