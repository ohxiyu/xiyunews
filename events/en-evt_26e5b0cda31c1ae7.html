Current outcome

Microsoft announced its third-generation Maia 300 AI chip, positioned as the primary inference system for its own products and services, with plans to scale to multi-gigawatt deployments from 2027 to reduce reliance on Nvidia GPUs; model training continues on Nvidia and AMD. New details: over 1 million Maia 300 units may be deployed next year; compute dies use TSMC 2nm process, each with 288GB HBM4 memory and 18TB/s bandwidth; each rack holds 96 chips at about 167kW, with inference throughput up to 7x Maia 200 and ~40% more tokens per MW than leading GPU systems; previous-generation Maia 200 is already running in Microsoft Copilot.
