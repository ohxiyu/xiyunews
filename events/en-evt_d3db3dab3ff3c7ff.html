Current outcome

Taiwanese authorities, acting on a court order from New Taipei, blocked access to Coinbase and Kalshi over alleged violations of election-gambling laws ahead of the November 28 local elections, with users reporting loss of access since Wednesday and Polymarket already blocked in the region.
