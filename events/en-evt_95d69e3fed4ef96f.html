Current outcome

France's Treasury, AMF, Banque de France and ACPR urged EU lawmakers to let third parties such as crypto exchanges pay conditional rewards on stablecoin holdings while keeping the ban on issuer-paid interest, setting them against the ECB's call to extend the prohibition to third-party rewards, lending and staking.
