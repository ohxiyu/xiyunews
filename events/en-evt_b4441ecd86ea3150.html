Current outcome

Payward, Kraken's parent, has announced its intention to become the first CFTC-regulated HIP-3 deployer on Hyperliquid, with Bitnomial as DCM/DCO to create, own, administer, clear and settle perpetual futures markets and NinjaTrader Clearing as FCM to carry client accounts, subject to regulatory approval. An optional HIP-3* permissioned-market allowlist feature is available on testnet for a future network upgrade.
