Current outcome

Swift's blockchain ledger is now live, with at least 19 banks expected to use it by end-2026 for tokenized-deposit transactions in 5 major currencies, enabling 24/7 payments. Previously, IBM's Digital Asset Haven beta connection enabled 17 participating banks to instruct ledger transactions via ISO 20022 messages, with an on-premises beta also available.
