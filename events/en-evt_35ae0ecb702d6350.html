Current outcome

Thailand's SEC issued 11 crypto ETF regulations effective October 16, allowing only passive BTC and ETH exposure in the first phase with an 80% minimum net asset exposure and regulated custody, while permitting mutual/private funds to invest in domestic crypto ETFs and barring brokers from margin financing and from serving non-institutional clients with offshore crypto ETFs.
