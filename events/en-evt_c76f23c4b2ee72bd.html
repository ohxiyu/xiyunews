Current outcome

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority advanced its EnsembleTX project to 24/7 real-value tokenized settlement using tokenized central bank money as the base layer, with tokenized Exchange Fund Bill secondary-market trades completed and regulated stablecoins slated for the next trial phase, while HSBC reported its HKD stablecoin RedCoin reached 50,000 users about ten days after launch on PayMe.
