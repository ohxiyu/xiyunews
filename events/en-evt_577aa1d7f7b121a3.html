Current outcome

The OCC issued a final report finding that nine major U.S. national banks—including JPMorgan, Bank of America, Citi, Wells Fargo, U.S. Bank, Capital One, PNC, TD and BMO—maintained policies between 2020 and September 2025 that denied or restricted services to clients based on political or religious beliefs or lawful business activity, with digital assets among the sectors flagged for access limits, prompting the Comptroller to weigh changes to the supervisory and examination framework.
