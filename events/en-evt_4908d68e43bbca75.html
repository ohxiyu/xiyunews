    Largest Philippines crypto exchange, Coins.ph, said to face central bank regulatory action

    CoinDesk reports that BSP enforcement against Coins.ph is tied to serving restricted offshore gambling operators and could include license revocation; the DCPay Philippines deposit restrictions are reported as not time-bound, and neither BSP nor Coins.ph commented.

    State after update: BSP's inbound InstaPay/PESONet transfer restrictions on DCPay Philippines remain, reportedly not time-bound; Coins.ph faces broader reported BSP enforcement over allegedly serving restricted offshore gambling operators, with potential license revocation.

  3. #0310.01
    Initial2026-10-01 07:44 · publication time

    Philippine Central Bank Curbs Bank Transfers to Major Crypto Platform Coins ph

    The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas partially suspended inbound InstaPay and PESONet bank transfers to DCPay Philippines, the e-money operator of leading local crypto platform Coins.ph, while outbound transfers and the separately licensed Betur Inc. trading entity remain unaffected.
