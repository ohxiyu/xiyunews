Current outcome

Mysten Labs announced a partnership with Google Cloud to launch a Verifiable Agent Arbitrator (VAA) that produces independently verifiable records of AI agent activity, keeping private telemetry in customer-controlled Google Cloud Storage while storing cryptographic proofs on Walrus and coordinating them via the Sui blockchain, aimed at helping enterprises meet upcoming EU AI Act logging requirements.
