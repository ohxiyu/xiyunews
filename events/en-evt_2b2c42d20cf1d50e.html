Current outcome

Meta's newly released open-weights model Muse Spark Open scored 39 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index, displacing Nvidia's Nemotron 3.5 Super VL as the top US open model but trailing Chinese models like Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Pro (46) and Zhipu GLM-5.3 (45), with notably weak agent-test performance offset by best-in-class knowledge accuracy and lower token costs.
