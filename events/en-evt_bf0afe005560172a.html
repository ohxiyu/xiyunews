Current outcome

According to on-chain analyst Ai Yi (@ai_9684xtpa), a hacker suspected of stealing roughly $106 million from an EU entity bought 27,553 ETH in a single day, worth about $62.83 million, with all purchases executed on-chain at an average price of about $2,280.34; buying appears to have paused as of the Nov. 3 report. The purchase is the largest concrete on-chain trace disclosed so far in a case where the alleged proceeds are being converted and moved rather than frozen, giving investigators and exchange compliance teams a fresh set of addresses to track. On-chain detective ZachXBT said in a post on his personal channel that an EU entity appeared to have lost more than $106 million from accounts across multiple centralized exchanges. The hacker has been converting large amounts of USDC into ETH, moving funds to an instant exchange service to swap into Monero (XMR) via Wagyu, and bridging across multiple chains.
