Current outcome

Kraken has blocked withdrawals of the VANRY token until December 10, according to CryptoTicker. The report also states that VANRY on Kraken is in a "cancel only" trading state with a price of zero. According to CryptoTicker, "cancel only" is a trading state exchanges set ahead of a delisting, meaning VANRY holders on Kraken cannot move the token off the exchange until December 10. CryptoTicker describes VANRY's status on Kraken as "cancel only, a price of zero, withdrawals blocked." The suspension is limited to VANRY and the stated end date is December 10.
