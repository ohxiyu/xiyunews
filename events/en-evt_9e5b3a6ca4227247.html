Current outcome

After disclosing unauthorized access to its environment-variable storage, Railway issued guidance for users with Google Cloud service-account keys to rotate credentials, redeploy, and audit cloud activity; the incident remains reported as possibly linked to the suspected Swell DAO admin-key compromise.
