Current outcome

The Financial Stability Board (FSB) warned that stablecoin issuers could face redemption crises even when total reserves are sufficient if reserves are spread across jurisdictions and become difficult to mobilize. The FSB specifically flagged risks in stablecoins issued by multiple affiliated companies that share liability for the same token while holding separate reserves, and noted that pseudonymous transactions and differences in national insolvency laws complicate determining responsibility. FSB Chair Andrew Bailey said international regulatory coordination remains underdeveloped, and the risks will be discussed at the Oct. 15 G20 meeting of finance ministers and central bank governors.
