Current outcome

Reserve announced it is pausing liquidity provision for LCAP and CMC20 tokens on the MEXC exchange and will withdraw related funds. This action follows a US FinCEN order that effectively cut off MEXC's access to the US financial system due to alleged illicit transactions. The suspension represents a direct operational consequence of the US Treasury's enforcement action, forcing a major liquidity provider to exit the platform. This move highlights the immediate compliance risks and market access limitations imposed on MEXC by the regulatory order. FinCEN's order alleges MEXC facilitated at least $16 billion in illicit transactions, including activities related to evading sanctions on Iran. Reserve clarified that liquidity for these tokens on other venues, including on-chain platforms, remains unaffected.
