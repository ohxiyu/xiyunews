Current outcome

The Consensys-ClearToken partnership remains in place to provide round-the-clock DvP settlement between tokenized securities and fiat, tokenized deposits, and stablecoins; ClearToken's CSD has received Bank of England Digital Securities Sandbox Gate 2 approval to operate a limited digital securities depository, while clearing operations still require Bank of England authorization.
