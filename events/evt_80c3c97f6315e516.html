目前结论

Qubic disclosed that a seed-generation randomness flaw allowed an attacker to steal roughly 2.94 trillion QU from 1,404 wallets, prompting a network halt at tick 85,371,949 with 87.1% of the funds frozen on-chain and the remaining 12.9% sent to MEXC also frozen, while the flaw itself was patched in version 4.26.4.
