Current outcome

Blink Wallet started charging a $1-per-month inactivity fee on custodial BTC and USD balances dormant for 12 months, deducting until balances are depleted, affecting roughly 100,000 accounts holding over $800,000, with fees refunded automatically if users log back in while self-custodial accounts are unaffected.
