Current outcome

Databricks says it used GPT-6 Astra and Opus 5 in an automated optimization loop that rewrote and tuned GPU kernels, placing first in all four tracks of NVIDIA's 235-task SOL-ExecBench for about $70,000 in token cost, extending the same agent-based kernel optimization approach previously applied to Kimi Delta Attention on B300 with a reported 2.96x speedup over the official FlashKDA.
