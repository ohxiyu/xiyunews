Current outcome

Keystone is remediating the October 20 website breach: the authentication key was stolen, old-key device authentication is invalid, and 18,835 inventory devices are to be destroyed; all shipments are suspended and affected devices are quarantined across all warehouses, including Amazon; it confirmed 25,964 buyer records and 12,873 checkout records were leaked and has requested Shopify delete related data; logistics data retention is cut to 90 days with on-request deletion after order receipt; devices delivered before October 20 are considered safe, while devices delivered afterward require additional verification.

    【Keystone 硬件钱包暂停所有发货，隔离各仓库受影响设备】

    Keystone has suspended all shipments and quarantined potentially affected devices across every warehouse it uses, including Amazon; it notified affected customers via support@keyst.one and warned it will never request seed phrases; logistics data retention is cut to 90 days, with on-request deletion after order receipt.

    State after update: Keystone is remediating the October 20 website breach: the authentication key was stolen, old-key device authentication is invalid, and 18,835 inventory devices are to be destroyed; all shipments are suspended and affected devices are quarantined across all warehouses, including Amazon; it confirmed 25,964 buyer records and 12,873 checkout records were leaked and has requested Shopify delete related data; logistics data retention is cut to 90 days with on-request deletion after order receipt; devices delivered before October 20 are considered safe, while devices delivered afterward require additional verification.

  2. #0210.27
    Escalation2026-10-27 01:55 · publication time

    Keystone breach deepens: Hackers stole key used to verify wallets as genuine

    Keystone further disclosed that the attacker escalated privileges via a third-party monitoring tool vulnerability and stole a device-authentication key that could let counterfeit wallets pass authenticity checks; 25,964 buyer records were stolen, along with 12,873 incomplete checkout records retained by Shopify; devices manufactured and delivered before the October 20 incident are considered safe, while authenticity of devices delivered after that date cannot be guaranteed.

    State after update: Keystone is remediating the October 20 website breach: the authentication key was stolen, old-key device authentication is invalid, 18,835 inventory devices are to be destroyed, and website shipments are paused until new-key devices are produced; it confirmed 25,964 buyer records and 12,873 checkout records were leaked and has requested Shopify delete related data; devices delivered before October 20 are considered safe, while devices delivered afterward require additional verification.

    Source evidence: Cointelegraph

  3. #0310.26
    Remediation2026-10-26 14:44 · publication time

    Keystone宣布将销毁1.88万台设备，确认设备验证密钥遭泄露

    Keystone confirmed the leaked data included device-authentication keys, and said it will retire the old keys, destroy 18,835 inventory devices built with them, pause shipment of unfulfilled website orders until new-key devices are produced, and cut customer data retention from 180 to 90 days.

    State after update: Keystone is remediating the October 20 website breach: old device-authentication keys are revoked, 18,835 inventory devices are being destroyed, website shipments are paused until new-key devices are produced, and a Shopify data-deletion request is expected to complete by November 3.
