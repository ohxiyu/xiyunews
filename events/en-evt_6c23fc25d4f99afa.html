Current outcome

Ant Group's Bailing open-sourced the MIT-licensed Ming-3.0 speech suite of five models, including a 0.5B TTS system that ranked first among open-source models on Artificial Analysis' same-voice blind listening test at 1069 Elo, a 126B-parameter (6.5B active) audio-understanding model, and an ASR model averaging 4.41% error across four test categories.
