Current outcome

CryptoCom will discontinue its consumer app in Hong Kong effective February 1, 2027, according to Bloomberg. The company said the move is part of efforts to optimize global operations and resource allocation, and that affected users have been notified and will receive instructions on account closures and fund withdrawals. The shutdown removes CryptoCom's retail-facing app from Hong Kong even as the company keeps pursuing an SFC virtual asset trading platform license through Foris DAX HK Limited for its institutional exchange, leaving Hong Kong consumer users with a fixed deadline to close accounts and withdraw funds. The consumer app closure is dated February 1, 2027, and is framed as an operational and resource-allocation decision rather than a regulatory action or insolvency event. The institutional CryptoCom Exchange business and its separate licensing application in Hong Kong are described as continuing.
