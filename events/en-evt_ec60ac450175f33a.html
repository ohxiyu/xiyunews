Current outcome

Chainalysis disclosed that an external attacker used 'offensive agentic techniques' to breach its internal systems and tools, compromising non-sensitive customer data such as user IDs, full names, emails, and organization role labels, while stating that investigative content, credentials, MFA secrets, and session tokens were not accessed and that the incident is unrelated to the recent Binance-related crypto theft.
