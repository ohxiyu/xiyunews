Current outcome

MYRIAD disclosed that a leaked internal wallet private key, stemming from a third-party library flaw in its backend, was used to manipulate settlement of 10 AMM prediction markets on BNB Chain; the team revoked the wallet's permissions within minutes, voided the 10 markets, and will automatically refund holders at pre-attack prices using recovered funds and market liquidity.
