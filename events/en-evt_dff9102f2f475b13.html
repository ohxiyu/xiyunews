Current outcome

The UK FCA opened a consultation on draft DeFi guidance clarifying that regulated firms using DeFi must meet existing operational resilience and financial-crime requirements, including risks from smart contracts, cross-chain bridges, oracles, third-party dependencies, and AML/KYC controls, with comments due November 18 and final guidance planned for Q1 2027.
