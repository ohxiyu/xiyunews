Current outcome

Anthropic's S-1 filing disclosed that in October 2025, the company bought back about 4.45 million forfeited Series B preferred shares from the U.S. government for approximately $627 million at $140.97 per share, then resold them at the same price to nine qualified investors; those shares are now worth roughly $3.94 billion at the company's pre-IPO valuation, ahead of its Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT.
