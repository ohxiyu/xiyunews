Current outcome

Bybit Stocks is live, letting eligible users trade over 1,000 US-listed stocks and ETFs with USDC; Bybit CEO announced future support for stock collateral, lending, and broker transfers.

Progress timeline

4 material updates
  1. #0110.13
    Escalation07:44 UTC · publication time

    Bybit CEO：Bybit股票交易后续将支持抵押品、借贷及券商转仓

    Bybit CEO Ben Zhou announced that Bybit stock trading will in the future support stocks as trading collateral, offer stock lending services, and enable transfers in from brokers such as Futu and Moomoo.

    State after update: Bybit Stocks is live, letting eligible users trade over 1,000 US-listed stocks and ETFs with USDC; Bybit CEO announced future support for stock collateral, lending, and broker transfers.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats

  2. #0210.13
    Escalation07:31 UTC · publication time

    【Bybit 正与 Mantle 合作开发代币化股票产品 mStocks，目标于下月上线】

    Bybit CEO Ben Zhou announced at a keynote that Bybit is developing mStocks, a tokenized stock product with Mantle, targeting launch next month with 1:1 real-stock backing, 24/7 conversion, DEX AMM trading on Mantle, and eligibility as collateral in Bybit's Unified Trading Account.

    Source evidence: foresightnews · theblockbeats

  3. #0310.12
    Escalation07:50 UTC · publication time

    【Bybit Stocks 正式上线，符合条件用户可交易逾 1000 只美股及 ETF】

    Bybit Stocks moved from an announced expansion plan to a live launch: KYC-verified users in permitted jurisdictions can trade over 1,000 US-listed stocks and ETFs with USDC, with fractional shares, 24/5 trading, zero commission and corporate-action support, executed and custodied by US self-clearing broker Alpaca, with the service provided by Mauritius FSC-regulated Infra Capital Limited.

    State after update: Bybit Stocks is now live, letting eligible users trade over 1,000 US-listed stocks and ETFs with USDC from their Bybit accounts.

    Source evidence: foresightnews · BlockTelegraph · Crypto News · The Defiant · www.tokenpost.com · Bitcoin News

  4. #0410.12
    Initial07:00 UTC · publication time

    Bybit Expands Beyond Crypto With Direct Access to U.S. Stocks & ETFs

    Bybit announced it is expanding beyond crypto by offering users direct access to U.S. stocks and ETFs on its platform.

    Source evidence: Bybit
