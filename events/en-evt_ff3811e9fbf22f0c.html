Current outcome

The XRP Ledger activated the fixCleanup3_4_0 amendment to patch a permission-delegation flaw that could have allowed burn-only authorized accounts to mint tokens for issued assets such as stablecoins, while XRP itself was unaffected and no exploitation has been confirmed.
