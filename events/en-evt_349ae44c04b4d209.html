Current outcome

The Binance ~$6.4B hack remains under investigation; Binance says it has covered about 80% of losses, allocated over $6.34B including $5B in tokens for full compensation, about $3B in stolen funds has been frozen on-chain, and it is buying remaining tokens via OTC and other means; the FBI and Secret Service are involved, and TRM Labs and Chainalysis attributed it to North Korea-linked TraderTraitor; Circle and Tether froze about $823M in USDC and $975.1M in USDT respectively; the investigation found “additional points of breach” beyond the compromised DevOps engineer's laptop but no evidence of wallet private-key leakage; the CFTC chair is citing the hack to push for U.S. oversight of offshore exchanges.
