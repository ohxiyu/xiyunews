DevelopingCryptoSecurity incidentFirst tracked 2026-10-29Last changed 2026-10-30

Current outcome

The Binance incident remains under investigation; preliminary findings pointed to a compromised DevOps device and signing-infrastructure misuse, with estimated impact about $6.3B, about $3B frozen, deposits/withdrawals temporarily suspended, the affected address authorization revoked, and losses to be covered by SAFU and company funds. Blockaid further analyzed that the attacker exploited EIP-7702 authorization by submitting 50 different-nonce authorizations in one transaction; only one succeeded, delegating the Binance 14 hot wallet to a malicious sweep contract.

    Binance suffered a $5.6B hack — the largest exchange theft ever — that reportedly wiped out its ~$1B SAFU insurance fund, with about $2.4B of the stolen USDT/USDC already frozen by Tether and Circle.

    Binance's preliminary investigation says attackers may have used a compromised DevOps engineer laptop to access a service container and submit unauthorized withdrawal transactions, with no evidence of private-key leakage; Binance raised the estimated impact to about $6.3 billion, said about $3 billion was frozen, and plans to cover losses via SAFU and company funds, revoke the affected address authorization, and publish updated Proof of Reserves.

  12. #1210.30
    Escalation2026-10-30 02:46 · publication time

    Blockaid Reveals How Hacker Took Control of Binance Wallet: One of 50 EIP-7702 A

    Blockaid analysis disclosed that the attacker submitted 50 EIP-7702 authorizations with different nonces in a single Type-4 transaction; only one matched, delegating the Binance 14 hot wallet to an unverified malicious transfer contract. The contract only lets the attacker-designated address call sweep(), allowing ETH and ERC-20 tokens to be transferred while retaining 1 ETH for gas.

    State after update: The Binance incident remains under investigation; preliminary findings pointed to a compromised DevOps device and signing-infrastructure misuse, with estimated impact about $6.3B, about $3B frozen, deposits/withdrawals temporarily suspended, the affected address authorization revoked, and losses to be covered by SAFU and company funds. Blockaid further analyzed that the attacker exploited EIP-7702 authorization by submitting 50 different-nonce authorizations in one transaction; only one succeeded, delegating the Binance 14 hot wallet to a malicious sweep contract.
