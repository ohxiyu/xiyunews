6 material updates
  1. #0110.29
    Initial2026-10-29 21:38 · publication time

    Binance-hacker-1029 – $5.6B stolen from Binance

    Binance suffered a $5.6B hack — the largest exchange theft ever — that reportedly wiped out its ~$1B SAFU insurance fund, with about $2.4B of the stolen USDT/USDC already frozen by Tether and Circle.

    Source evidence: Tiberium · Cointelegraph · Decrypt · wublockchainenglish

  2. #0210.29
    Aftermath2026-10-29 23:47 · publication time

    Binance黑客集群持仓监控：涉案65个币种总计58.75亿美元，其中25.4亿美元已被冻结

    Arkham data shows the Binance hacker cluster now holds about $5.875 billion across 65 tokens, and Binance says it has coordinated the freezing of about $2.54 billion in stolen funds.

    State after update: Binance is still investigating the hack; the hacker cluster holds about $5.875 billion across 65 tokens, and Binance says about $2.54 billion of the stolen funds has been frozen through coordination.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats

  3. #0310.29
    Escalation2026-10-29 23:51 · publication time

    Arkham: Binance Hacker-Linked Addresses Hold $5.88B in Assets; Binance Confirms

    Arkham identified 66 addresses tied to the 'binance-hacker-1029' entity holding roughly $5.88B in crypto, including 1.768B USDT, 18,270 BTC, 466,622 ETH and 824.56M USDC, while Binance confirmed $2.54B of hack-linked assets have been frozen with recovery ongoing.

    Source evidence: wublockchainenglish

  4. #0410.29
    Confirmation2026-10-29 23:52 · publication time

    【CZ：Binance 疑遭黑客攻击，用户资金安全】

    Binance founder CZ stated that Binance was suspected of being hacked while user funds remain safe, thanking Tether (~$971M) and Circle (~$823M) for rapidly freezing the related funds.

    Source evidence: foresightnews

  5. #0510.29
    Response2026-10-29 23:55 · publication time

    【何一：Binance 暂停提币，直至确认环境安全，用户资金安全】

    Binance co-founder Yi He said the exchange has paused withdrawals until it confirms the environment is secure, asserted user funds remain safe under SAFU, and thanked World Liberty Financial for helping freeze $27 million in USD1 plus 48 million WLFI tokens (worth about $2 million).

    State after update: Binance has paused withdrawals until it confirms the environment is secure; the hack remains under investigation, with Binance previously saying about $2.54 billion in stolen funds had been frozen through coordination, and World Liberty Financial helped freeze $27 million in USD1 plus 48 million WLFI tokens (about $2 million).

    Source evidence: foresightnews

  6. #0610.29
    Escalation2026-10-29 23:57 · publication time

    【神鱼：Binance 14 热钱包仍未撤销 7702 委托，一笔 2961 ETH 的交易正转向该钱包】

    神鱼警告 Binance 14 热钱包的 EIP-7702 委托两小时后仍未撤销，一笔约 2,961 ETH 的待处理转账一旦上链可能被攻击者扫走。

    Source evidence: foresightnews
