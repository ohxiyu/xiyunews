    Mastercard Brings Its Fraud-Fighting Tools to Stablecoin Payments

    Mastercard unveiled Stablecoin Security, an API-based risk-intelligence service built with Borderless.xyz and Keyrails that scores stablecoin payments for fraud, scams and sanctions exposure before settlement, launching first in North America with global availability planned for 2027.
