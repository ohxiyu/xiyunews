Current outcome

Coinbase received CFTC approval to register Coinbase Clearing LLC as its own derivatives clearing organization (DCO); the registration became effective Monday, supporting native USDC collateral and 24/7 settlement, and authorized to clear fully collateralized futures, options on futures, and swaps, but not leveraged products; margined derivatives and upcoming single-stock perpetuals still rely on existing partners.
