Current outcome

Marinade disclosed that an attacker exploited a flaw in its DAO voting program to gain inflated voting power and filed two malicious proposals — one to swap out the voting program and one to drain the treasury — both of which were rejected with no funds lost, followed by a hotfix, plans for a permanent code fix, added safeguards for sensitive proposals, AI-driven proposal review, and a heads-up to Realms DAO maintainers.
