Current outcome

Moonshot AI has published the open weights for Kimi K3.1 Pro, with the model repository live on Hugging Face for download and self-hosting. The release drew 426 points and 125 comments on Hacker News, where users noted that third-party inference providers began listing the model within hours. Commenters reported that third-party providers are already serving the model, with one saying Together offers roughly double the throughput of Moonshot AI's own endpoint at the same price and Modal roughly double that again — a sign that hosted access is opening up beyond the releasing lab. Discussion centered on whether the model is tuned to maximize benchmarks: one commenter argued it does not clearly outpace DeepSeekV4.1-Flash and scores modestly on another lab's internal benchmarks, while another said their own testing did not show benchmark-gaming and called it a strong open model slightly off the frontier. One user estimated running it locally would require something like an 8x B300 node.
