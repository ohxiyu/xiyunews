Current outcome

Qubic disclosed that an attacker exploited insufficient randomness in wallet seed generation to drain roughly 2.94 trillion QU from 1,404 wallets, halted the network, froze 87.1% of the stolen funds, and patched the flaw in version 4.26.4 while exchanges suspended QUBIC deposits and withdrawals.
