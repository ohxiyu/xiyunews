Current outcome

SlowMist continues to warn that DarkSword targets iOS crypto users, exploiting iOS vulnerabilities to break sandbox protections and steal Keychain credentials, local wallet private keys, and notes; it advises users to promptly update iPhone/iPad systems and beware poisoned installed apps.
