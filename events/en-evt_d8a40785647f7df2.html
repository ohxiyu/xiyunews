Current outcome

Filecoin introduced its Solstice reward mechanism that reallocates and burns rewards, announced a ~75% drop in daily FIL issuance as genesis vesting ends, and launched enterprise/AI-agent storage products including Integral Spacetime, S3-compatible Fil One, and agent-facing on-chain storage payment tools.

    【Filecoin 创世代币六年归属期将于 10 月 14 日结束，FIL 年化新增量预计将下降约 77%】

    Filecoin's six-year genesis token vesting period ends on October 14, ending ~68.3M FIL annual vesting releases and cutting total annual new FIL supply by roughly 77% (from 88.9M to 20.6M tokens, about 9.7% to 2.2% of circulating supply), while storage-provider block rewards remain unaffected with 25% liquid and 75% vesting over 180 days.
