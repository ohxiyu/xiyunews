Current outcome

ILITY reported a security breach and temporarily halted its cross-chain bridge, while on-chain analyst Specter traced a linked wallet that unshielded roughly $41M in ZEC and moved funds via NEAR Intents into Bitcoin and Ethereum, with 4.1M ILY drained from the bridge and about $25M in ZEC still held.
