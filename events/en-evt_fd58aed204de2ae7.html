Current outcome

Binance will tighten cross-border crypto deposit and withdrawal procedures for Brazilian users from November 1, 2026 to comply with Brazilian central bank Resolution 521/2025, requiring transfer-purpose and counterparty identification, monthly reporting to the BCB, and a USD 100,000 per-transaction cap for transfers with unlicensed foreign counterparties.
