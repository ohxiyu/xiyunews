Current outcome

The IMF's latest Global Financial Stability Report finds that tokenized stocks see genuine retail demand — most trading happens outside U.S. market hours and in fractional amounts — but these tokens are about 1.5 times more volatile and less liquid than the underlying equities, with systemic risk still contained and regulators urged to consider circuit breakers for 24/7 trading.
