Current outcome

Circle will begin cutting legacy CCTP V1 burn limits on October 31 and pause the old contracts on December 1, 2026, forcing apps, wallets, and bridges onto the non-backward-compatible new CCTP, with Noble's USDC contracts and cross-chain routes set to pause January 12, 2027 ahead of manual redemption.
