Current outcome

Supabase announced it is acquiring Turso to give every AI agent an instantly provisioned database; the products remain separate for now (Turso Database stays open source), with gradual integration planned (small agent tasks on Turso, scaling into Supabase's Postgres), Turso founder Glauber Costa joining Supabase to lead agent-related services, and the deal value undisclosed.

    Supabase收购Turso：要让每个Agent都能随手开数据库

    Added deal details: the acquisition targets AI-agent workloads (Supabase says over 1M databases are created weekly, ~70% from agents/AI tools), Turso's Rust SQLite architecture can manage millions of on-demand databases per server; the products will not be merged immediately (Supabase stays on Postgres, Turso stays on SQLite, existing databases/APIs/workflows unchanged, Turso Database remains open source) with gradual integration planned; Turso founder Glauber Costa will join Supabase to lead agent-related services; deal value undisclosed.
