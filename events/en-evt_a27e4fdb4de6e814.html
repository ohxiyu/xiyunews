Current outcome

Mizuho, MUFG and Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corp published results of their joint yen stablecoin proof-of-concept, confirming the feasibility of a shared issuance model with MUFG Trust as trustee and validating cross-border issuance, transfer and redemption for Mitsubishi Corp, with plans for deposit-backed reserves and live transactions in FY2026.
