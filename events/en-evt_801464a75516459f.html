Current outcome

Ondo Private Markets has listed an OpenAI private markets note, offering 24/7 on-chain trading on the Ondo Perps spot orderbook to qualified investors for economic exposure tied to OpenAI's reference per-share value; the note does not represent OpenAI equity and Ondo has no affiliation or endorsement relationship with OpenAI.
