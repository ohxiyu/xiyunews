Current outcome

dYdX community opened an on-chain vote to close and finally settle 17 markets — including ONDO, ENA, WLFI, ORDI, WIF, APE, BLUR and 1INCH — with delisting set for Oct 30 if the proposal passes, citing near-zero trading volume in eight markets, high oracle maintenance costs for WTI and XAG, and the planned shutdown of the SOL/ETH/Base node infrastructure behind Slinky price feeds for ENA, ONDO and WLFI.
