Current outcome

Aleph Alpha released and open-sourced Kolibri, a German-English MoE LLM. It has 78.1B total parameters and ~3.46B active per token, with weights on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0. It supports tool calling and adjustable reasoning, with context extendable to ~1M tokens (trained to 262K). The official technical report details dataset and training recipe.
