Current outcome

Cardano DeFi protocol Genius Yield is winding down operations, and after users were unable to withdraw assets, co-founder Lars Brünjes independently released Genius Zero, an app that bypasses the protocol's backend to unstake and cancel DEX orders directly via Cardano smart contracts, while DEX fees still accrue to Genius Yield-controlled addresses requiring 3-of-4 founder signatures to adjust.
