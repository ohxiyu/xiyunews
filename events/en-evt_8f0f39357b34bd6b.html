Current outcome

Ai2 has open-sourced AstaBrief 8B, an Apache-2.0 Qwen3-8B-derived model that replaces its multi-step Claude-based report pipeline with single-pass generation, cutting average report time from 178.5s to 51.1s (~3.5x faster) while raising citation precision to 90.5 and recall to 78.2 on ScholarQA-CS2.
