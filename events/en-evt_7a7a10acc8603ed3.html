Current outcome

Developer Theo open-sourced tsc-rs, a Rust port of Microsoft's TypeScript 7 compiler built almost entirely by AI agents, which after Codex stalling at ~84% compatibility over five months and ~$400k in tokens was restarted and completed by Claude Opus 5.5 in roughly two weeks for ~$24k, passing 181,711 ported tests and type-checking about twice as fast as the Go original.
