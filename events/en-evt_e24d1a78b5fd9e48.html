Current outcome

Binance and Polymarket have submitted a Polygon governance proposal for a hard fork that would remove roughly 70.49 million POL (~$6.8M) from five attacker-controlled addresses tied to their respective hacks and redirect the funds to verified recovery wallets, without changing POL's total supply.
