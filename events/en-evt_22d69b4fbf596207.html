Current outcome

Mistral has released flagship model Mistral Large 4 (internally nicknamed Le Chonk), an MoE model with 1.05T total parameters and 49B active parameters, multimodal support and 1M-token context; API preview is available, open weights are planned for October 27, and Mistral claims it is the strongest open-weights model from the US or Europe with self-reported benchmarks.
