  2. #0209.25
    Confirmation2026-09-25 05:20 · publication time

    Ondo unlocks BlackRock portfolio strategies, but only non-US traders benefit

    Ondo Finance launched three tokenized portfolio tokens (BLKHIon, BLKDIGon, BLKGRWon) built on BlackRock-developed strategies, but they are Ondo-issued securities rather than BlackRock fund interests, and direct redemption is limited to onboarded non-US investors.
