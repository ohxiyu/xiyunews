Current outcome

Circle's H1 2026 revenue disclosure now includes a detailed breakdown: of ~$1.4B revenue, $819M (59%) went to USDC distribution partners and transaction costs, $473M (34%) to operating, compliance, and product development costs, with ~7% net profit; free 24/7 near-real-time USDC redemptions for CPN and eligible platform customers remain in place. USDC posted over $36T in H1 2026 on-chain volume and represented ~77% of global stablecoin volume in June.
