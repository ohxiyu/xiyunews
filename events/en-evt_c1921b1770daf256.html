Current outcome

Winklevoss has filed an S-1 with the SEC for a spot Zcash ETF (ticker 'WINK') on Nasdaq with a 0.25% fee; Winklevoss Asset Services is the sponsor, Gemini Trust is the ZEC custodian, Cypherpunk Technologies advises as Zcash ecosystem partner, and Winklevoss Capital Fund indicated nonbinding interest in purchasing up to $100M of shares.
