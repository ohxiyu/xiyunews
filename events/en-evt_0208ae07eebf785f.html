Current outcome

Arkansas Attorney General Tim Griffin sued Kalshi for allegedly operating an illegal, unregulated gambling platform in violation of the state's Deceptive Trade Practices Act, seeking restitution, penalties, taxes and an injunction, making Arkansas the latest of several states — alongside New York, Michigan, Kentucky and Utah — to take prediction markets to court as the CFTC defends its jurisdiction.
