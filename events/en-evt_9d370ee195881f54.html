Current outcome

Open-source AI assistant company Nous Research raised a $90M Series B led by Robot Ventures at a $1.5B valuation, with Nvidia, Microsoft M12, Samsung, Union Square Ventures and Menlo Ventures participating, to bring its open-source Hermes agent to enterprise customers.
