Current outcome

Researchers published a Nature paper — with an accompanying arXiv preprint (2511.07312) — describing an AI system, named Ataraxos, that beat the best human Stratego player in history. According to the reporting, it reached that level while playing roughly 34 times fewer games than DeepNash, DeepMind's 2022 Stratego agent. Stratego is a hidden-information game in which the best move depends on facts a player cannot observe, making look-ahead search difficult; the paper presents general techniques for self-play reinforcement learning and test-time search under hidden information. Commenters on Hacker News pointed to the training efficiency — far fewer games for a stronger result — as the critical element of the work. The Nature article describes Ataraxos as based on general techniques developed for both self-play reinforcement learning and test-time search under hidden information. According to the report, human players were surprised by how often the agent tucked its flag into a corner behind just two bombs, a rarely played setup.
