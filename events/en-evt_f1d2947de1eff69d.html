Current outcome

Replit signed a definitive agreement to acquire Chroma, the company behind the Apache-2.0 ChromaDB vector database, and will shut down Chroma's managed cloud service within roughly 60 days — freezing writes from November 22 and reads from December 22 — while keeping the open-source database and open-sourcing additional previously closed code.
