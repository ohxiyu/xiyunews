Current outcome

The Dutch central bank granted Qivalis an EMI license under MiCA; Qivalis is backed by 37 major European banks, still needs to meet remaining MiCA requirements including whitepaper, plans to issue token in coming months, and will distribute via banks, exchanges, and payment service providers.

    Dutch central bank grants Qivalis e-money license for MiCA-compliant euro token
