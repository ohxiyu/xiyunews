Current outcome

Hong Kong's IFEC guidance confirms that SFC-authorised tokenized funds can be bought and sold on the secondary market through licensed virtual asset trading platforms under an ETF-like regulatory framework, with two-way primary/secondary market interoperability and warnings about premium/discount, off-hours liquidity, price fragmentation and single-market-maker reliance.
