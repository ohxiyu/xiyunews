Current outcome

The UK FCA crypto authorization application window opened Sept. 30, 2026, closes Feb. 28, 2027, and the full regime starts Oct. 25, 2027. Eligible existing providers that apply on time and remain undecided at commencement can rely on the temporary saving provision to continue relevant services and take new business subject to conditions and a two-year cap; late applicants still pending at commencement are limited to pre-existing contracts, cannot enter new contracts, and enter restricted run-off. Approval is not automatic, and existing AML registration does not automatically convert.
