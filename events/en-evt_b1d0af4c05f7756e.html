Current outcome

On-chain perpetual futures platform Extended will migrate its settlement network from Starknet to Arc during the week of October 19, pausing deposits and withdrawals for about two hours, switching gas payments to USDC, and requiring users holding more than $1 of USDT or wBTC to convert or withdraw by October 21 or face account restrictions and possible forced liquidation.
