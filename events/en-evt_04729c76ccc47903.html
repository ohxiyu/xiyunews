Current outcome

Solana has scheduled Alpenglow's mainnet activation for October 21, retiring its original consensus mechanism and targeting finality of roughly 150 milliseconds versus today's ~12.8 seconds, amid a broader crypto selloff that saw majors drop 3-5% and over $900M in liquidations.

    Solana doubles block production speed to 200ms as network prepares for Alpenglow upgrade

    Solana activated 200-millisecond slots on mainnet on Oct. 9, completing the four-stage SIMD-0525 reduction from 400ms: block-production opportunities rose to five per second, epoch duration fell from about 48 hours to about 24 hours, and transaction blockhash expiry shrank from about 60 seconds to about 30 seconds.

    State after update: The 200-millisecond target of SIMD-0525 has been activated and declared complete by Anza; attention now shifts to the Alpenglow consensus overhaul targeting ~150ms finality, which has been deployed on testnet and devnet but has no announced mainnet activation date.
