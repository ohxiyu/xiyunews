Current outcome

Alpenglow is live on devnet and testnet, and Anza has now scheduled Mainnet-beta activation for around Oct 21, 2026 at 22:50 with a single-slot migration. Mainnet activation is now scheduled but not yet executed.

    Solana Schedules Final Cut to 200-Millisecond Slots

    Solana is scheduling its final cut to 200-millisecond slots, compensating with smaller per-block compute limits while validators face more frequent voting and shorter block-production windows.

    Source evidence: The Defiant

  6. #0610.09
    Escalation2026-10-09 05:00 · publication time

    Solana is about to halve its block times as final 200-millisecond upgrade nears

    Solana has progressed beyond the 350ms cut to 300ms and 250ms, and is scheduled to make a final reduction to 200ms target block time at epoch 1053, completing the SIMD-0525 sequence of cuts from 400ms.

    State after update: Solana mainnet has completed 350ms, 300ms, and 250ms slot reductions and is scheduled to activate 200ms target block time at epoch 1053, with the final mainnet rollout still dependent on network conditions.

    Source evidence: CoinDesk

  7. #0710.10
    Escalation2026-10-10 00:40 · publication time

    Solana doubles block production speed to 200ms as network prepares for Alpenglow upgrade

    Solana activated 200-millisecond slots on mainnet on Oct. 9, completing the four-stage SIMD-0525 reduction from 400ms: block-production opportunities rose to five per second, epoch duration fell from about 48 hours to about 24 hours, and transaction blockhash expiry shrank from about 60 seconds to about 30 seconds.

    State after update: The 200-millisecond target of SIMD-0525 has been activated and declared complete by Anza; attention now shifts to the Alpenglow consensus overhaul targeting ~150ms finality, which has been deployed on testnet and devnet but has no announced mainnet activation date.

    Source evidence: CryptoSlate
