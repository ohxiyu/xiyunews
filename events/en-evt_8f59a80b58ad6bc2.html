Current outcome

Solana Mobile removed a malicious third-party app called "SKR Claim" from its dApp Store after unauthorized access to the publishing portal let it be featured on recommendation and quest pages while soliciting users' wallet seed phrases, and it patched a flaw that could display unpublished apps.
