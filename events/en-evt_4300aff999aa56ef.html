  2. #0211.04
    Initial15:30 UTC · publication time

    Crédit Mutuel Arkéa Confirms Cyberattack After ZachXBT Ties It to $133M Crypto Theft

    French banking group Crédit Mutuel Arkéa confirmed in a statement to Decrypt on Wednesday that it was hit by a cyberattack that enabled fraudulent operations, saying the incident is "closed and now under control" and that operations are resuming gradually. The confirmation came one day after onchain investigator ZachXBT publicly named the bank as the alleged victim of a crypto theft exceeding $133 million. The bank said it alerted the relevant supervisory authorities as soon as it detected the attack and temporarily suspended some transactions as a precaution. ZachXBT accused the institution of failing to make a "proper incident disclosure," which he said prevented law enforcement from freezing some $30 million in stolen funds. Crédit Mutuel Arkéa did not mention crypto or state how much was taken, saying the affected amounts are still being assessed but are "in any event not of a nature to call into question the solidity and financial health of the group"; it said customer assets were unaffected and some transfers were only delayed. ZachXBT initially flagged more than $106 million in withdrawals from exchange accounts on November 1, later added $27 million, published four Ethereum addresses, and said he could state "with a high degree of confidence" the attacker is not the Lazarus Group.

    Source evidence: Decrypt
