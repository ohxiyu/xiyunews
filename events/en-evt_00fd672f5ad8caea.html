Current outcome

Hyperliquid Strategies (NASDAQ: PURR), the largest public HYPE treasury company, applied for US futures commission merchant registration to serve US clients on Hyperliquid's proposed HIP-3 permissioned perpetual futures markets, while reporting $620.6M in quarterly net income driven by $803M of unrealized HYPE gains and a treasury of roughly 38.9M HYPE.
