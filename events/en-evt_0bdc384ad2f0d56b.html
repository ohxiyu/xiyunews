Current outcome

Uniswap v4 Aggregator Hooks are live on Ethereum mainnet, Base, and Robinhood Chain, initially supporting seven external sources including Curve, Fluid, and PancakeSwap; swaps routed through them incur protocol fees contributing to UNI burns.

    【Uniswap Aggregator Hooks 上线，可封装外部流动性来源转化为 v4 流动性池】

    Uniswap launched Aggregator Hooks, letting v4 wrap external liquidity sources such as Curve, Fluid, and PancakeSwap into v4 liquidity pools, live on Ethereum mainnet, Base, and Robinhood Chain via the Uniswap Web app and API.
