Current outcome

Elon Musk said SpaceX has completed $40 billion of redeemable GPU debt financing, with the proceeds mainly earmarked for GPUs scheduled to arrive in the first and second quarters. According to BlockBeats, Musk credited the company's investment-grade credit rating and revenue from its Starlink satellite-internet and launch businesses with keeping its access to credit open. As described, the package would be among the largest disclosed debt raises aimed specifically at buying AI compute, indicating that GPU purchases are being funded through credit markets rather than only from operating cash flow. Musk said the spending will have little impact on the current quarter because the hardware has not yet arrived. Musk described the instrument as redeemable debt — borrowing with a defined repayment date — but the flash report did not disclose lenders, coupon, maturity or other terms, and there was no independent confirmation of the financing. BlockBeats listed the borrower under the ticker SPCX.O, although SpaceX is not a publicly listed company.
