Current outcome

SlowMist warned that two critical, already-exploited PHP vulnerabilities (CVSS 9.2) affect all PHP 5.4–8.5.11 deployments and pose systemic risk to Web3 off-chain infrastructure, noting that over half of the 1,488 crypto-related domains it scanned show PHP fingerprints, and urged projects to upgrade immediately to patched releases.
