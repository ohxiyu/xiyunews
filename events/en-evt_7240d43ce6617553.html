Current outcome

Abracadabra has moved to wind down the protocol after repeated hacks left its Magic Internet Money stablecoin with 22 million outstanding MIM against roughly $900,000 in actionable backing, prompting accusations of 'treasury looting' over earlier governance actions.

    Abracadabra Proposes Winding Down MIM at Four Cents on the Dollar

    Abracadabra has proposed a Snapshot vote to liquidate the protocol, swap remaining collateral into ether and pay MIM holders pro rata, with only about $900,000 of recoverable backing against $21 million in bad debt — roughly four cents on the dollar.

    Source evidence: The Defiant

    State after update: The Abracadabra Snapshot vote to shut down the protocol and liquidate MIM is open from 2026-09-29 05:24 UTC to 2026-09-30 17:24 UTC; votes for and against have been cast; the proposal cites ~$21M bad debt, ~$900K executable collateral, sub-$0.04 effective MIM backing, and declares SPELL valueless.
