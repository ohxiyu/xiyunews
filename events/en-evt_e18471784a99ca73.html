Current outcome

Binance has begun restoring user assets hit by the theft, earmarking more than $6.34 billion of company funds — $5 billion already held in the affected tokens with the remainder being bought via market and OTC channels to match the stolen asset mix — while roughly $3 billion has been frozen with industry partners and final balances will be publicly verifiable through Proof of Reserves.
