Current outcome

Vitalik Buterin has open-sourced a 'paranoid mode' privacy daemon for AI agents that routes each conversation through its own Tor circuit with random cover traffic, uses zkAPI to hide payment identity, sandboxes execution with bwrap/torsocks, and rewrites prompts via a local model behind an OpenAI-compatible interface, while noting Tor is suboptimal and cheaper zkAPI mainnet gas is coming.
