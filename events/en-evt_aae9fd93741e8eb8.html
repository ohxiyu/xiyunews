Current outcome

Bitwise NEAR ETF (NRR) has officially listed and begun trading on NYSE Arca on September 29, 2026, as the first U.S. spot NEAR ETP with a 0.75% management fee and plans to stake its holdings for roughly 5% average staking yield; on its first day it reached $36 million in assets under management, $35.5 million in net inflows, and $15.1 million in trading volume.
