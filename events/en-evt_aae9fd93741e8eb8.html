Current outcome

Bitwise's spot NEAR ETF has transitioned from approval pending launch to official launch. The product, ticker NRR, is the first US spot NEAR ETP, charging 0.75% and staking NEAR in-house with rewards passed through via NAV. It previously cleared NYSE Arca and SEC review.
