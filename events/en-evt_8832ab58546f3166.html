    Blur to Shut Down Frontend and Stop Matching New Blend Loans on November 12; BLU

    Blur announced that it will shut down its frontend on November 12, 2026, while its on-chain marketplace protocol will remain operational and BLUR will continue to control the protocol's fee switch. Its NFT lending protocol, Blend, will stop matching new loans on the same date, and borrowers have until then to repay their loans or allow them to default. Blend borrowers with open positions face a hard deadline to repay or default, and the NFT lending market loses the venue that matched its loans. Blur said it will subsequently provide a frontend tool for users to withdraw their assets from Blend. Following the announcement, BLUR fell to a low of 0.01410 USDT before recovering to 0.01523 USDT, down 16.55% over 24 hours according to Binance data. Blur raised $11 million in a seed round in March 2022, with investors including Paradigm, eGirl Capital and 0xMaki.
