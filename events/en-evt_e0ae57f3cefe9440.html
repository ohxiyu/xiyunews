6 material updates
  1. #0110.25
    Confirmation07:27 UTC · publication time

    【CoinDCX 确认旧钱包基础设施遭攻击损失 1599 万 USDT，损失由公司承担】

    CoinDCX published an announcement confirming that the October 21 abnormal activity originated from deprecated wallet infrastructure. That infrastructure had been compromised in the July 19, 2025 security incident and retired, but contained an unknown vulnerability. An automatic sweeping service failed for a period, allowing funds from users who mistakenly deposited to the deprecated address to accumulate, resulting in a 15.99 million USDT loss that the company will absorb. CoinDCX said current trading and wallet systems were unaffected, user funds are held in isolated cold wallets, deposits, withdrawals and trading operated normally over the past 72 hours, and it will strengthen monitoring of legacy systems; Salus CMO Darcy commented that this is a “clumsy lie.”

    State after update: CoinDCX has formally confirmed that the 15.99 million USDT loss stemmed from deprecated wallet infrastructure and a failed automatic sweeping service, saying it will absorb the loss and that current systems were unaffected; Salus's CMO publicly disputes that account.

    Source evidence: foresightnews · theblockbeats · Decrypt

  2. #0210.23
    Confirmation11:00 UTC · publication time

    CoinDCX belatedly admits latest hack; Ireland fines Coinbase anew

    Indian crypto exchange CoinDCX has acknowledged a new security breach, confirming the incident only after the fact rather than at the time it occurred. Separately, Ireland's central bank issued another penalty against Coinbase's Irish entity, with reports citing a €3.25 million ($3.6 million) fine over safeguarding and outsourcing obligations. The two disclosures land on the same exchange-risk themes — custody security and regulatory compliance — that supervisors have been tightening. Ireland's central bank framed the Coinbase action as customer protection, stating: "This action against Coinbase Ireland underlines the Central Bank's focus on ensuring that customers are at all times protected and that the integrity of the financial system is maintained." The Coinbase penalty reported in the safeguarding and outsourcing case requires approval from Ireland's High Court before taking effect. Delano separately reported a €21.5 million penalty against Coinbase Europe for anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing failures, described as Ireland's first enforcement action in the crypto sector.

    Source evidence: CoinGeek

  3. #0310.22
    Response15:30 UTC · publication time

    Crypto exchange CoinDCX ‘lying’ about $16M hack, say blockchain investigators

    CoinDCX responded that the addresses were old wallets created in January 2025 and compromised in July 2025, claimed they were no longer in use, and described the roughly $16 million as “operational losses”; blockchain investigators disputed the account, citing contradictory statements and laundering routes that differed from last year’s hack.

    State after update: The roughly $16 million CoinDCX suspicious outflow has moved from Salus’s initial suspected-theft report to a public dispute: CoinDCX acknowledges operational losses from failed sweeping of old compromised wallets while denying misleading statements, and blockchain investigators contest its account of address ownership and fund movement.

    Source evidence: Protos

  4. #0410.22
    Confirmation10:51 UTC · publication time

    【Coinbase 爱尔兰子公司就客户资金保障问题与爱尔兰央行和解，将支付 325 万欧元罚款】

    Coinbase's Irish e-money subsidiary will pay a €3.25 million fine to the Central Bank of Ireland after settling an investigation into past client-fund safeguarding failures, including cross-entity commingling and a code error that left safeguarding accounts roughly 0.54% short on average in 2023 and the first half of 2024.

    Source evidence: foresightnews

  5. #0510.22
    Escalation09:03 UTC · publication time

    ⚡️ZachXBT质疑CoinDCX：1580万美元用户存款去向不明

    ZachXBT alleged that about $15.8 million in CoinDCX user deposits remains unaccounted for and that a deposit address linked to a 2025 security incident continued collecting user funds and routing them to CoinDCX's current hot wallet, claims CoinDCX disputes.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats

  6. #0610.22
    Initial05:44 UTC · publication time

    【Salus CMO：CoinDCX 疑似被盗约 1599 万 USDT 及部分 TRX】

    Salus's CMO reported that roughly 15.99 million USDT and 5,783.81 TRX moved out of CoinDCX hot wallets and deposit addresses to a TRON address on October 21, with the funds subsequently split and transferred toward Binance, OKX and Gate deposit addresses, in what is being described as a suspected theft.
