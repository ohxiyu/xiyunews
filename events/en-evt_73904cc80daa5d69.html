Current outcome

Binance listed Hyperliquid's native HYPE token, and the asset fell 4.5% following the listing, according to Yahoo Finance and 24/7 Wall St. A Binance listing gives HYPE access to one of the largest centralized exchange user bases and order books; in this case the token traded down rather than up on the news, bucking the widely observed "listing pump" pattern. The reported move is a 4.5% decline, and coverage of the event is limited to the headline-level price reaction.
