Current outcome

BlackRock filed a preliminary prospectus with the SEC for the iShares Bitcoin Managed Protection ETF (PBIT), which would use options linked to its $65.7B spot IBIT fund to offer limited downside protection in exchange for capped gains, adding a third Bitcoin-linked product to its crypto ETF lineup without filing for Solana or XRP exposure.
