Current outcome

The Mesh-Visa partnership has been announced to let users spend directly from crypto and stablecoin wallets at Visa merchants; Mesh handles funding, asset conversion, KYC and Visa credential provisioning, with Visa's network covering 175M+ merchant locations, while participating wallets and launch dates are not yet announced.
