Current outcome

HSBC's RedCoin stablecoin is now in phased rollout to PayMe users, with initial P2P transfers and merchant payments, expected full availability before the weekend, and planned expansion to tokenized money market fund subscriptions by year-end.

    HSBC named its upcoming Hong Kong stablecoin HSBC RedCoin and outlined a phased rollout starting with P2P transfers and merchant payments before expanding to corporate and institutional applications, citing a survey of over 1,000 local customers showing broad stablecoin awareness.
