Current outcome

HSBC's RedCoin is in phased rollout to more than 3.3 million PayMe users, supporting P2P transfers and payments at over 100,000 merchants; ticker RCHKD, running on HSBC's private ledger, with about $10.2 million circulating backed by $10.4 million in cash and no deposit protection coverage; HSBC plans to expand to its HK Mobile Banking app and tokenized money market fund subscriptions by year-end, with first independent reserve attestation expected after Q4 2026.
