Current outcome

OpenAI is pushing to close a roughly $30 billion funding round at a valuation potentially exceeding $1.4 trillion before Anthropic's expected mid-November IPO, with SoftBank, MGX and existing backers in talks, citing annualized revenue of $50 billion after GPT-6 Astra and a year-end target of $70 billion.
