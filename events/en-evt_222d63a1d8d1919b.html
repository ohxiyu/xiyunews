Current outcome

Anthropic launched Claude Duet in research preview, a proactive agentic assistant built on Claude Opus 5.5 that runs in a cloud VM with its own browser, delivers daily briefings, executes multi-step business tasks across 6,000+ connectors, and requires explicit user approval for sensitive actions while prohibiting others such as securities trading and account transfers.
