Current outcome

Character.AI’s bankruptcy sale process is advancing: Disney is poised to pay $20 million for perpetual non-exclusive rights to some technology and patents and to hire 66 employees at an expected cost of about $180 million over three years; the company had only 18 full-time staff at filing, 40 state attorneys general intervened to protect user chat records, and November 20 is the bid deadline, with shutdown possible if no buyer keeps the platform running.

    Character.AI破产细节曝光：迪士尼三年拟花1.8亿美元留人，平台只剩18人

    Bankruptcy court filings show Disney had offered jobs to 66 Character.AI employees, expecting to spend about $180 million over three years including $30 million in retention bonuses, while bidding only $20 million for perpetual non-exclusive rights to some technology and patents; Character.AI had only 18 full-time staff when it filed; 40 state attorneys general intervened to protect user chat records; other bidders must submit offers by November 20 or the platform may shut down.

    State after update: Character.AI’s bankruptcy sale process is advancing: Disney is poised to pay $20 million for perpetual non-exclusive rights to some technology and patents and to hire 66 employees at an expected cost of about $180 million over three years; the company had only 18 full-time staff at filing, 40 state attorneys general intervened to protect user chat records, and November 20 is the bid deadline, with shutdown possible if no buyer keeps the platform running.
