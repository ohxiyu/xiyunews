Current outcome

CZ has endorsed BNB Chain's new L1 plan: the L1 will coexist with BSC, connect via a native bridge, issue no separate token, and continue using BNB for gas, staking and governance; its name is TBD, an internal multi-validator devnet is already running, and the official target is testnet in late 2026 and mainnet in early 2027.
