Current outcome

Due to failing to obtain EU MiCA authorization, Binance announced it will cease operations in France and close user accounts by December 31, 2026. Starting November 29, a monthly management fee of 0.025% with a minimum of 10 USDC will be charged on assets held in these accounts. The immediate imposition of fees creates a direct financial cost for users holding assets, while the exit signals significant regulatory friction for major exchanges operating under the EU's MiCA framework. Deposits are already suspended, and fees will be deducted primarily from USDC, with the first charge occurring within three days of November 29. Assets not withdrawn by the deadline may be converted, transferred, or otherwise handled under the Terms of Service.
