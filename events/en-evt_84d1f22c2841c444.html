Current outcome

The Cysic cross-chain bridge remains paused, the involved address is blacklisted on-chain with about 2.8 million CYS frozen, and the project says the mainnet and user wallet assets were unaffected while a security firm investigates and traces funds.

    Cysic added that the attack occurred on October 17 after the attacker gained access to a database related to the cross-chain service and submitted false mint messages; the incident was limited to the bridge's message-processing and minting functions, did not affect the Cysic mainnet or existing assets in user wallets, and the team hired a security firm to investigate the attack path and trace funds.

  2. #0210.18
    Initial2026-10-18 10:43 · publication time

    Cysic crypto bridge halted after unauthorized mint of 10M CYS

    Cysic suspended its cross-chain bridge after an attacker exploited a processing flaw to mint 10 million CYS on BNB Smart Chain, with the project blacklisting the recipient and restricting transfers of about 2.8 million remaining tokens.

    Source evidence: Cointelegraph
