目前结论

Talis Protocol suffered a contract exploit that let an attacker steal roughly 5,700 escrowed NFTs on its Injective marketplace and sell them through public collection bids, prompting the platform to halt new listings and auctions, return over 26,000 NFTs still in contract custody, and draft a plan to restore affected NFTs to holders and compensate bidders whose offers filled.
