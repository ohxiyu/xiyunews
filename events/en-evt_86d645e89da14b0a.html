Current outcome

Bithumb's internal Daou Office mobile system was breached by an unauthorized external party, with confirmed exposure limited to 6,444 users (844 readable) and 715 employee/partner records; exchange accounts, assets and trading systems were not compromised, access was blocked, the system was disabled, and the incident was reported to Korean authorities.

    Confirmation2026-10-29 09:44 · publication time

    Bithumb Details Internal System Breach: 6,444 Customers and 715 Employees Compro

    Bithumb disclosed that an external intrusion into its internal DaouOffice groupware on October 27 exfiltrated personal data of 6,444 customers — including names, phone numbers, resident registration numbers, bank accounts and ID documents, with 844 records directly exposed — plus 715 employee records, while stating that trading systems and asset databases were isolated and customer funds remain secure.

    Bithumb quantified the exposure: 6,444 users' data affected (5,600 encrypted/unreadable, 844 readable) plus 715 employee/partner records including names, phone numbers, emails, resident registration numbers, driver's license numbers and bank account numbers; the company reported the incident to KISA and the Personal Information Protection Commission and tightened real-time monitoring of abnormal transactions and withdrawals.
