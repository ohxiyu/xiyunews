Coinbase and Bithumb both disclosed user data leaks — Coinbase through third-party analytics vendor Amplitude and Bithumb through an external breach of its internal mobile groupware — with contact information exposed but no customer funds or core exchange systems affected.

    South Korea’s Second-Largest Crypto Exchange Bithumb Confirms Security Breach

    Bithumb confirmed that an unauthorized external party breached its internal mobile groupware system and leaked personal data, an incident discovered on October 27 that is now under investigation alongside cyber intrusions at Mirae Asset Global Investments and Daishin Securities, the latter exposing data on 38,490 customers, with South Korea's Financial Supervisory Service reviewing all three cases.
