Current outcome

The Tianjin No. 2 Intermediate Court has accepted the prosecutor's application to confiscate assets from Qian Zhimin's illegal fundraising proceeds; the case is in judicial process, with assets including over 61,800 bitcoins and other crypto and conventional assets.
