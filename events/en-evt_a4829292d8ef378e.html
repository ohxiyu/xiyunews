Current outcome

Senator Richard Blumenthal has demanded that Tether provide records on its dealings with Bo Hines and Nxum by Nov. 3; Tether responded that an external-counsel internal investigation found no evidence of wrongdoing, and the investigation remains ongoing.

    【WSJ：Bo Hines 任职白宫期间，其持股公司通过 Tether 相关合作获 630 万美元】

    The Wall Street Journal reported that Bo Hines held a stake in consulting firm Nxum, which collected $6.3M from Tether-related partnerships while he was executive director of the White House crypto council pushing the GENIUS Act, including a bid to extend its compliance transition from 18 to 36 months, before he became head of Tether's US operations.

    Source evidence: foresightnews

  2. #0210.15
    Escalation2026-10-15 17:13 · publication time

    Tether paid Bo Hines’ company while he was at White House, WSJ

    WSJ/Protos added details: Tether routed payments to Nxum via Bitfinex and intermediary Notorious, with the first $2.49M sent on May 11, 2025; the contract was transferred directly to Nxum after Hines left the White House; Nxum denied Hines received Tether-derived funds during his White House tenure; the report also mentioned related super PAC funding.

    State after update: WSJ/Protos reported that Tether paid $6.3M to Nxum Capital, co-founded by Bo Hines, while Hines was a White House digital-assets adviser, with funds routed via Bitfinex and intermediary Notorious; the first $2.49M payment was sent on May 11, 2025; the contract was transferred directly to Nxum after Hines left the White House; Nxum denies Hines received Tether-derived funds during that period; the report also mentions related super PAC funding and GENIUS Act advocacy.

    Source evidence: Protos

    美国参议员调查Tether向前白宫加密顾问Bo Hines旗下公司支付数百万美元款项

    Senator Richard Blumenthal is investigating Tether's multi-million-dollar payments to Nxum Group, founded by Bo Hines, and has sent letters to Tether and Nxum demanding disclosure of the arrangement; Tether has not yet responded.

    State after update: After WSJ/Protos reported that Tether paid $6.3M to Nxum, U.S. Senator Richard Blumenthal has opened an investigation into the payments and demanded that Tether and Nxum disclose the arrangement; Tether has not yet responded.

    Senator Richard Blumenthal is investigating Tether's payments of millions of dollars to Nxum Group, the consultancy of former White House crypto adviser and current Tether US head Bo Hines, and has demanded both parties disclose the arrangement.

  5. #0510.23
    Response2026-10-23 20:16 · publication time

    Senate Democrat Probes Tether Payments to Ex-White House Crypto Adviser’s Firm

    Decrypt added that Blumenthal sent a letter to Tether CEO Paolo Ardoino demanding records on dealings with Bo Hines and Nxum by Nov. 3; Tether responded that external counsel had conducted an internal investigation and found no evidence of wrongdoing; the letter also referenced a Nxum pitch deck for Tether to buy a 25% stake for $56 million and a separate Tether-linked letter to Cantor Fitzgerald earlier this month.

    State after update: Senator Richard Blumenthal has demanded that Tether provide records on its dealings with Bo Hines and Nxum by Nov. 3; Tether responded that an external-counsel internal investigation found no evidence of wrongdoing, and the investigation remains ongoing.
