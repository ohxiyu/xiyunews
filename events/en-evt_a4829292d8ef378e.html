Current outcome

WSJ/Protos reported that Tether paid $6.3M to Nxum Capital, co-founded by Bo Hines, while Hines was a White House digital-assets adviser, with funds routed via Bitfinex and intermediary Notorious; the first $2.49M payment was sent on May 11, 2025; the contract was transferred directly to Nxum after Hines left the White House; Nxum denies Hines received Tether-derived funds during that period; the report also mentions related super PAC funding and GENIUS Act advocacy.
