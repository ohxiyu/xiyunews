Current outcome

Anthropic research shows Claude Mythos 5, aided by an automated evaluation system, autonomously redesigned eight quantum circuits and produced a reversible modular inversion algorithm that cuts an elliptic-curve point-addition circuit's gate count roughly 17x (with 12% more qubits) and trims DQI Reed–Solomon decoding gates ~12x, though results remain unverified on real quantum hardware.
