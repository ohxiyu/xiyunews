  1. #0110.07
    Escalation13:26 UTC · publication time

    S&P brings ratings-style scrutiny to $10 billion crypto vault market as $6 million Base incident exposes risks

    S&P is extending ratings-style scrutiny to the roughly $10 billion crypto vault market just as a $6 million exploit on Base highlights the risks in on-chain yield products.

    Source evidence: CryptoRank

  2. #0210.05
    Aftermath18:44 UTC · publication time

    Base Vault Drained of About $6 Million in Aave Deposit Tokens

    On-chain data shows the attacker removed a newly deployed contract from the vault's borrower whitelist, added it back about one minute later, then executed six unauthorized outflows; some stolen funds were bridged toward Ethereum.

    State after update: An unclaimed vault contract on Base was attacked, with ~$6M (~1,783 wstETH) drained via a Safe multisig whitelist mechanism and Aave V3 redemption; the attacker executed six outflows and some funds were bridged toward Ethereum; ~$31.7M in assets remain at risk in the vault, while Aave core contracts and Base itself were unaffected.

    Source evidence: The Defiant

  3. #0310.05
    Escalation00:14 UTC · publication time

    GoPlus：Base链某金库合约遭攻击，损失约600万美元，仍有3170万美元资产处于风险

    GoPlus reports the attack stemmed from a Safe multisig whitelisting a malicious contract into the lending whitelist, with the attacker withdrawing 1,783 aBaswstETH and redeeming roughly 1,783 wstETH on Aave V3 for ~$6M in losses; it adds that ~$31.7M in assets remain at risk and that the team had not executed a Safe transaction for 25 days before the attack, possibly due to social engineering or insider collusion.

    State after update: An unclaimed vault contract on Base was attacked, causing ~$6M in losses (~1,783 wstETH) via a Safe multisig whitelist mechanism and Aave V3 redemption; as of the latest report, ~$31.7M in assets remain at risk in the vault, while Aave core contracts and Base itself were unaffected.

    Source evidence: theblockbeats · foresightnews

  4. #0410.04
    Escalation10:02 UTC · publication time

    【PeckShield：Base 上金库攻击事件损失扩大至约 600 万美元】

    PeckShield reports that losses from an attack on an unnamed Base vault have grown to approximately $6 million, or about 1,783 wstETH, after an attacker contract was whitelisted and drained aBaswstETH/aTokens across a handful of transactions.

    Source evidence: foresightnews
