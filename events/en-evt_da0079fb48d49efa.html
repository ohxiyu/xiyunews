Current outcome

France's National Assembly Finance Committee approved amendments to the 2027 budget bill that would tax crypto-to-stablecoin conversions as capital gains, permit 10-year loss carryforward, and apply an exit tax on unrealized crypto gains for taxpayers with holdings over roughly €800,000 who move their tax residence abroad.
