Current outcome

At the October 30 US close, the tech and semiconductor selloff was confirmed and deepened: Nasdaq fell 2.86%, S&P 500 fell 1.82%, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 7.2%; Intel closed down 28.58% in its worst single-day performance in at least four decades, with Nvidia, AMD, Qualcomm, and Micron also lower.
