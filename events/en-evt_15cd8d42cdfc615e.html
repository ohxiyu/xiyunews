Current outcome

Intel closed down 28.58% at $65.76 on October 30, its worst single-day performance since the 1970s; the US government's stake lost about $12 billion in value in one day and Nvidia's stake lost about $6 billion; Q4 revenue and EPS guidance came in well below consensus, the CFO expects PC-market weakness through at least H1 2027, and an explosion at its Israeli fab is expected to cost about $600 million in revenue; D.A. Davidson downgraded Intel to Neutral.

  1. #0110.31
    Aftermath10:03 UTC · publication time

    英特尔暴跌28.58%，美国政府持股价值单日蒸发约120亿美元

    The October 31 report adds post-selloff details: Intel closed at $65.76, down 28.58%; the US government's roughly 10% stake lost about $12 billion in value in one day, while Nvidia's Intel stake lost about $6 billion; Q4 revenue guidance was $15–16 billion versus LSEG consensus of $17.1 billion, and adjusted EPS guidance was $0.25 versus $0.43 expected; the CFO said PC-market weakness will persist at least through H1 2027 and memory/component costs are rising; an explosion at Intel's Israeli fab is expected to cause about $600 million in revenue loss over two quarters; D.A. Davidson downgraded Intel from Buy to Neutral.

    State after update: Intel closed down 28.58% at $65.76 on October 30, its worst single-day performance since the 1970s; the US government's stake lost about $12 billion in value in one day and Nvidia's stake lost about $6 billion; Q4 revenue and EPS guidance came in well below consensus, the CFO expects PC-market weakness through at least H1 2027, and an explosion at its Israeli fab is expected to cost about $600 million in revenue; D.A. Davidson downgraded Intel to Neutral.

    Confirmation00:50 UTC · publication time

    美股周五三大股指低收，费半跌超7%，英特尔暴跌28.58%

    The October 30 US close confirmed a sharp tech and semiconductor selloff: Nasdaq fell 2.86%, S&P 500 fell 1.82%, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 7.2%; Intel closed down 28.58%, its worst single-day performance in at least four decades, while SanDisk, Qualcomm, AMD, Micron, and Nvidia also fell.

    State after update: At the October 30 US close, the tech and semiconductor selloff was confirmed and deepened: Nasdaq fell 2.86%, S&P 500 fell 1.82%, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 7.2%; Intel closed down 28.58% in its worst single-day performance in at least four decades, with Nvidia, AMD, Qualcomm, and Micron also lower.

    State after update: The October 30 US tech selloff deepened and spread across semiconductors, with Intel plunging 30% on weak guidance, Nvidia's market cap falling below $5 trillion, Nasdaq 100 down as much as about 3%, and S&P 500 down about 1.25%; energy and healthcare gained as rotation intensified.
