Current outcome

SWI Group completed an €800 million (~$894 million) fundraise at a €6.1 billion valuation to convert the now-majority-controlled and renamed SWI Digital (formerly Genesis Digital Assets) bitcoin mining sites in the U.S. and Sweden into AI data centers, while selling Kazakhstan mining assets.

    比特币矿企Genesis Digital Assets将向AI数据中心转型

    State after update: After acquiring a controlling stake in Genesis Digital Assets for $500 million in June 2026, SWI Group announced on October 12 €800 million (~$900 million) in financing to convert Genesis Digital Assets' bitcoin mining sites in Texas, North and South Carolina, and Sweden into AI data centers serving large cloud providers, while divesting remaining Kazakhstan mining assets.
