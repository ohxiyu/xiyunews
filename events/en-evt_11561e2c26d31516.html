Current outcome

After acquiring a controlling stake in Genesis Digital Assets for $500 million in June 2026, SWI Group announced on October 12 €800 million (~$900 million) in financing to convert Genesis Digital Assets' bitcoin mining sites in Texas, North and South Carolina, and Sweden into AI data centers serving large cloud providers, while divesting remaining Kazakhstan mining assets.

    Alameda-Backed Bitcoin Miner Genesis Digital Raises About $900 Million for AI Da

    SWI Group announced it is raising about $900 million (€800 million) to fund the conversion of Alameda-backed bitcoin miner Genesis Digital Assets into an AI data center business serving large cloud providers, using its U.S. and Swedish sites while selling off Kazakhstan mining assets.
