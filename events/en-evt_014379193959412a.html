Current outcome

The UK High Court has ruled that Chinese investors' property-rights claims over more than 61,000 seized bitcoins in the Qian Zhimin case must be decided under Chinese law rather than English law; as a result, roughly 16,000 Chinese victims may recover only principal plus interest, not bitcoin price-appreciation gains.

    The UK High Court ruled that Chinese investors' property-rights claims over more than 61,000 bitcoins seized in the UK in the Blue Sky Gerui fraud case must be determined under Chinese law rather than English law, affecting assets linked to a ~£4.1bn (~RMB 40.2bn) scheme.
