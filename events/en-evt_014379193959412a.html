Current outcome

The UK High Court ruled that Chinese investors' property-rights claims over more than 61,000 bitcoins seized in the UK in the Blue Sky Gerui fraud case must be determined under Chinese law rather than English law, affecting assets linked to a ~£4.1bn (~RMB 40.2bn) scheme.
