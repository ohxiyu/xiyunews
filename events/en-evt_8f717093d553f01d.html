Current outcome

Loopscale has resumed operations for all its vaults, including the USDC Genesis Vault, and lifted remaining loan-management restrictions following a security attack. The protocol announced that a technical post-mortem will follow this restoration of service. The reopening signals the resolution of a critical security incident and restores user access to funds and lending functions on the platform. The restart specifically includes the USDC Genesis Vault, which had been subject to loan-management restrictions during the freeze.
