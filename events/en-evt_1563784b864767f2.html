Current outcome

Bitget reportedly lost about $388 million in a hack linked to North Korean actors, with its CEO seeing slim odds of recovery; the new report confirms it as Q3 2026’s largest crypto security incident, about 31% of the quarter’s $1.26 billion in losses, and adds that attackers exploited a third-party security product vulnerability to obtain credentials and forge withdrawal commands, with activity traced to an Aug. 31 zero-day exploit.
