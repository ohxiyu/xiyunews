Current outcome

Anthropic’s internal Claude research model reconstructed the likely origin of NIST elliptic-curve seed parameters, linking them to standard random-number generation and 1997–1998 timestamps; external cryptographers reportedly replicated the check, while the work did not break ECDSA or reveal a new curve vulnerability.
