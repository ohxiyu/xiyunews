Current outcome

Ant Group's BaiLing team released Ling-3.0-flash-Sante, an MIT-licensed open-weights medical model built on a 124B-parameter MoE architecture (about 5.1B active per token) that improved its HealthBench Professional score to 54.29 and complex medical reasoning to 76.92, while adding literature retrieval, tool calling and multi-agent collaboration for research workflows.
