Current outcome

Pirate Chain is in remediation: mandatory v6.0.14 has been released, the validation vulnerabilities are fixed, and the chain has been rolled back to block 4,176,944 to purge forged ARRR and restore the supply cap; old-version operators are required to upgrade immediately.

  1. #0110.22
    Remediation06:19 UTC · publication time

    【Pirate Chain 拟强制升级至 v6.0.14 版本，完成链回滚以消除约 3982 万枚伪造 ARRR】

    Pirate Chain released mandatory v6.0.14, requiring old-version users, nodes, miners, pools, exchanges and service providers to upgrade; by community consensus the chain was rolled back to block 4,176,944 to remove about 39.82 million forged ARRR and restore the 200 million supply cap.

    Pirate Chain确认近日遭攻击，代币供应量增发约20%

    Pirate Chain confirmed a transaction-validation vulnerability that created roughly 39.8 million unauthorized ARRR (about 20% of supply), prompting dPoW operators to pause notarization and the team to ask exchanges and mining pools to suspend operations while a recovery plan is drawn up.
