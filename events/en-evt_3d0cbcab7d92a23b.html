Current outcome

Passport hardware wallet maker Foundation traced more than 23,000 phishing emails — 19,057 via Mailgun and 1,503 via Google Workspace — to legacy WooCommerce database tables that retained poorly redacted customer data and possibly Shopify orders since May, while stating that Passport devices and private keys were not compromised and that affected credentials and legacy services have been revoked or taken offline.
