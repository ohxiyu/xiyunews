Current outcome

Moody's assigned Sky Protocol (formerly MakerDAO) a B3 long-term counterparty risk rating with a stable outlook — its first rating of a stablecoin protocol — citing roughly $10 billion in tangible managed assets against only about $90 million in tangible common equity, a capital position it called a significant credit weakness, alongside risks from confidence-sensitive stablecoin liabilities and its DAO structure.
