Current outcome

The Cloudflare Workers AI GLM-5.3 user-isolation leak has been fixed; related SGLang cache-management defects were patched with a recommendation to upgrade to v0.5.22; no malicious exploitation was found and other Workers AI models were unaffected.

    Initial2026-10-26 22:41 · publication time

    Cross-request KV cache exposure in the GLM-5.3 Workers AI model (155 responses)

    Cloudflare published a root-cause analysis of a Workers AI bug in which GLM-5.3's cross-request KV cache was not properly isolated between users, allowing potential overlap of session memory, traced to a prefill cache capacity mismatch and remediated by pulling the model from serving.

    Source evidence: wondersky

  2. #0210.27
    Remediation2026-10-27 10:12 · publication time

    Cloudflare AI用户隔离失效：GPU缓存不够竟照常回答，155条回复泄露其他客户内容

    Disclosed a separate SGLang cache-reuse bug, its fix version v0.5.22, and that the actual leak was fixed on September 9, with no evidence of malicious exploitation and other Workers AI models unaffected.
