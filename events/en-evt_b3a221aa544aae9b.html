Current outcome

Sui Devnet has added ML-DSA-65 as a native account signature scheme (reported as protocol version 139) and processed its first native post-quantum transaction; Testnet is targeted for end-2026 and Mainnet for Q1 2027, subject to independent audits and Testnet feedback.
