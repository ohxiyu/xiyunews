Current outcome

BaFin rejected Futurum Bank AG's MiCA application, leaving Bitcoin.de trading largely suspended since June 12 and euro deposits halted, while crypto withdrawals remain open. The platform plans to outsource trading and custody to two unidentified regulated German partners, with Futurum Bank continuing to hold assets until transfer; no restart date is set, and the company may object within one month or reapply.

    MiCA rejection forces German crypto platform into operational overhaul

    After BaFin's rejection, Bitcoin.de plans to rebuild trading and custody through two regulated German institutions: one as trading counterparty and one for crypto custody, while retaining its app, brand and customer interface. Withdrawals remain open, but trading and euro deposits are suspended; the partners are unidentified, no restart date is set, and the company may object within one month or reapply for authorization.
