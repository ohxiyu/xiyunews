Current outcome

JPMorgan Asset Management included Bitcoin in its long-term capital market assumptions for the first time, with a 10% base-case annualized return, 16.6% bull case, and -11.2% bear case, above U.S. large caps at 7.5% and gold at 5.5%, while stressing that high volatility and unstable correlations limit its current portfolio-diversification role.
