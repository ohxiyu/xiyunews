Current outcome

Ideogram launched Ideogram 4.5, an image editing model that limits iterative edits to specified regions while preserving the rest of the image, offering Precise Edit and Generate+Edit API modes with tiered pricing from $0.008 to $0.22 per image and a forthcoming open-weights release.
